Linguine with Heirloom Tomatoes and Anchovies

Tags

Michael Symon pastasummertomatopastaspaghettiItalian

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • caprese salad
  • pesto & bread & bread dip

Ingredients

  • salt
  • 1 cup bread crumbs
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Linguine
  • 4-5 tomatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1 tin anchovies, minced
  • 1 Tbs capers (we don’t typically use)
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • Parmesan cheese to serve

Instructions

Bring pot of water to boil for the pasta

Toast the breadcrumbs in a dry skillet

Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, add the garlic and red pepper, cook 1-2 minutes.

Add tomatoes, anchovies, (and capers) to the pan and simmer for about 2 minutes. Remove from heat and add olive oil and parsley.

Make a 'paste' out of garlic, anchovies, capers, pepper flakes to make a flavored oil.  Add tomatoes and cook for just a few minutes.  Use more tomatoes than you think for 1 lb of pasta.
