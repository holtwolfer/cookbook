Leon Salad

Ingredients

    Instructions

    1. add croutons
    2. Leon Salad
    3. 8 Tbs olive oil
    4. 4 Tbs sherry vinegar
    5. ½ tsp dried thyme
    6. ½ tsp dried oregano
    7. ¼ tsp dried mustard
    8. ¼ tsp crushed red chili flakes
    9. 1 clove garlic, smashed and minced into a paste
    10. 1 12-oz. can chickpeas, drained
    11. 4 oz. sliced provolone, cut into ¼" strips (preferably aged)
    12. 4 oz. baby mozzarella balls, drained and quartered
    13. 1 4 oz piece salami, halved lengthwise and thinly sliced crosswise into ¼"-thick pieces
    14. ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
    15. 1 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
    16. ¼ cup loosely packed flat-leaf parsley
    17. ¼ cup loosely packed mint leaves
    18. 2 hearts of romaine, finely chopped
    19. 1 head radicchio di Treviso or regular radicchio, roughly chopped
    20. salt/pepper
    21. In a large bowl, whisk together oil, vinegar, thyme, oregano, mustard, chili flakes, and garlic. Stir in chickpeas, provolone, mozzarella, salami, and onions; cover. Let marinate at room temperature for 1 hour.
    22. Combine basil, parsley, mint, romaine, and radicchio in a large bowl and transfer to a serving platter. To serve, top lettuces and herbs with marinated chickpea mixture and season with salt and pepper.
