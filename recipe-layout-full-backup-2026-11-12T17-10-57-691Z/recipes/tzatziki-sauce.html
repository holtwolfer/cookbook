Tzatziki Sauce

Tags

grillNYGreeksummer

Related recipes

  • Chicken Spiedies

Ingredients

  • Tzatziki sauce:
  • 1 English cucumber, seeded, chopped
  • 2 cups Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup mayo
  • 2 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1/2 Tbs dill
  • optional: garlic, mint

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the yogurt, sour cream, mayo, lemon juice, dill, garlic, and mint (if using). Stir the cucumber in with a spoon.
  2. Marinate the chicken overnight. Put everything on skewers.
  3. Heat the grill to medium high for the chicken skewers, medium for veggies. Put chicken, onions and peppers on first. After 10-15 minutes add the mushrooms and then tomatoes with only a few minutes left of cooking time. Grill the pita bread for about 1 minute a side.
  4. Original Tzatziki Recipe
  5. 1 pound (1 pint) plain yogurt (whole milk or low fat)
  6. 1 hothouse cucumber, unpeeled and seeded
  7. 1 tablespoon plus 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  8. 1 tablespoon Champagne vinegar or white wine vinegar
  9. 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice (1 lemon)
  10. 1 tablespoon good olive oil
  11. 1 1/2 teaspoons minced garlic
  12. 1 1/2 teaspoons minced fresh dill
  13. Pinch freshly ground black pepper
  14. Place the yogurt in a cheesecloth or paper towel-lined sieve and set it over a bowl. Grate the cucumber and toss it with 1 tablespoon of kosher salt; place it in another sieve, and set it over another bowl. Place both bowls in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 hours so the yogurt and cucumber can drain.
  15. Transfer the thickened yogurt to a large bowl. Squeeze as much liquid from the cucumber as you can and add the cucumber to the yogurt. Mix in the sour cream, vinegar, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, dill, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and pepper. You can serve it immediately, but I prefer to allow the tzatziki to sit in the refrigerator for a few hours for the flavors to blend. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
  16. 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  17. ¾ cup red wine vinegar
  18. ¼ cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
  19. 4 cloves garlic, peeled, smashed and roughly chopped
  20. 1 Tbs oregano leaves
  21. 1 Tbs basil leaves, rolled and chopped into chiffonade
  22. 1 tsp granulated sugar
  23. 1 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  24. 1 tsp red pepper flakes, or to taste
  25. Make the marinade: Whisk together all the ingredients in a large bowl.
  26. Add the meat to the marinade and cover tightly, or place into large, re-sealable plastic bags and refrigerate for 24 to 36 hours (or 10 to 12 hours for chicken).
  27. Build a fire in your grill, leaving about 1/3 of grill free of coals, or set a gas grill to high.
  28. Remove the meat from its marinade and thread onto metal skewers, or wooden ones that you have soaked in water for 30 minutes or so. The chunks can be placed quite close together. Sprinkle with salt and pepper.
  29. When coals are covered with gray ash and fire is still quite high (you can hold your hand 5 inches above coals for only a couple of seconds), place the skewers of meat directly over the flames. Allow the meat to cook, undisturbed, for approximately 3 to 4 minutes, then use kitchen tongs to turn them over and repeat on the other side. Continue turning the skewers every couple of minutes until the meat is deeply crisp at its edges, and cooked entirely through, approximately another 5 to 7 minutes. (Remove them to the cool side of the grill if they begin to burn, and cover the grill to allow them to smoke-roast until finished; this is much less likely to happen on a gas grill.)
  30. Allow the spiedies to rest on a cutting board for a few minutes, then remove the meat from the skewers onto cut hero rolls or a plate. Serve with hot sauce on the side.
