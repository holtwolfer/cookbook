Mustard Roasted Potatoes

Tags

Served with

    Ingredients

    • 2 tablespoons whole-grain Dijon mustard
    • 2 tablespoons honey
    • 2 tablespoons orange juice
    • 2 teaspoons orange zest
    • 2 teaspoons finely chopped rosemary, plus 4 whole sprigs

    Instructions

    Preheat oven to 425oF.

    Mix 'sauce' ingredients together and toss with potatoes on parchment paper. Roast for about 45 minutes until the potatoes are lightly browned and tender.
