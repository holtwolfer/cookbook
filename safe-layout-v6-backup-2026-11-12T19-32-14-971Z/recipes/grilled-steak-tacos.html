Grilled Steak Tacos

Ingredients

  • Beef short rib, cut from bone
  • Or skirt, flat iron

Instructions

  1. Rub
  2. Kosher salt
  3. Black pepper
  4. california or florida pepper blend (citrus)
  5. White pepper
  6. Salsa fresca or pico di gallo
  7. Queso fresco
  8. Avocado
  9. Beans rice
  10. 4 pounds flanken-style short ribs (see note)
  11. Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  12. 1 recipe chimichurri
  13. Light one chimney full of charcoal. When all the charcoal is lit and covered with gray ash, pour out and arrange the coals on one side of the charcoal grate. Set cooking grate in place, cover grill and allow to preheat for 5 minutes. Alternatively, set half the burners on a gas grill to the highest heat setting, cover, and preheat for 10 minutes. Clean and oil the grilling grate.
  14. Season short ribs liberally with salt and pepper and place directly over coals. Cook, turning frequently, until charred on all sides and probe thermometer inserted into thickest part of steak register 125°F, 8 to 10 minutes total. Transfer to a cutting board, tent with foil, and let rest for 5 minutes.
  15. Serve immediately with chimichurri.
  16. Pico di Gallo
  17. 1 1/2 pounds (680g) ripe tomatoes, cut into 1/4- to 1/2-inch dice (about 3 cups); see note
  18. Kosher salt
  19. 1/2 large white onion, finely diced (about 3/4 cup)
  20. 1 to 2 serrano or jalapeño chilies, finely diced (seeds and membranes removed for a milder salsa)
  21. 1/2 cup finely chopped fresh cilantro leaves
  22. 1 tablespoon (15ml) lime juice from 1 lime
  23. Directions
  24. 1.
  25. Season tomatoes with 1 teaspoon (4g) salt and toss to combine. Transfer to a fine-mesh strainer or colander set in a bowl and allow to drain for 20 to 30 minutes. Discard liquid.
  26. 2.
  27. Combine drained tomatoes with onion, chilies, cilantro, and lime juice. Toss to combine and season to taste with salt. Pico de gallo can be stored for up to 3 days in a sealed container in the refrigerator.
