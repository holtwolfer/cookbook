Kal's Kourage Hot Sauce

Ingredients

  • 12 Habaneros (around 5 ounces)
  • 6-8 Cayenne peppers
  • 5-6 Garlic cloves, whole, unpeeled
  • 2-3 Carrots (medium), small slices
  • 2-3 Celery stalks
  • 1 Onion (medium), chopped
  • 1 cup Cider vinegar
  • 1 cup Water
  • salt
  • 1/2 Tbs pepper
  • 1/2 tsp Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Xanthan gum

Instructions

  1. Roast peppers and garlic (unpeeled) on the grill (grill basket makes it easy) until charred. Move to a paper bag and let cool. Peel peppers and garlic, seed the habaneros.
  2. Sautee onions, carrots and celery in a little olive oil, 5-10 minutes. Add peppers, garlic, vinegar, water, salt, pepper and sugar. Bring to boil and then reduce heat to simmer, covered until carrots are tender, 15-20 minutes (maybe more). Turn heat off and stir in xanthan gum. Process until smooth.
