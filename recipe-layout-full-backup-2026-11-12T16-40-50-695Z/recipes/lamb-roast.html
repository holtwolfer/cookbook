Lamb Roast

Ingredients

  • sous vide 4 hours at 132oF
  • seasoned with salt and pepper
  • lined with rosemary sprigs, vacuum sealed
  • Tried searing the whole shoulder in a cast iron skillet and then under the broiler.
  • We couldn't figure out how to cut it. Ended up carving meat off the bones. It was medium rare, kind of chewy, but delicious.
  • Sous Vide
  • Medium Rare 129.9 for 2 hr
  • Medium 140 for 2 hr
  • Grilled Lamb Shanks

  • 1/2 cup olive oil

  • 2 Tbs sherry vinegar

  • dash of paprika

  • cilantro stalks from one bunch, minced
  • lightly dusted with dry ingredients.  Mixed garlic, cilantro with some olive oil and rubbed all over. Sealed in vacuum bag.  131 for 5 hours and 140 for 2 hours.... may not be necessary, but it worked perfectly.
  • Wanted to add a little spice rub and finish over super high heat on the grill.  Ended up adding nothing and browning in cast iron over high heat with a little grape seed oil.

Instructions

  1. Served with roasted fingerling potatoes, sliced lengthwise, tossed with salt, pepper, paprika, olive oil and roasted at 425 for about 50 minutes to an hour.  Tossed with a dash of olive oil and minced chives to serve.
  2. Asparagus (was going to do asparagus hash with lemon juice)
  3. Mint-Cilantro chimichurri sauce
  4. 1 ounce (30g) picked fresh mint leaves (about 1 cup), finely chopped  (Used 2 grocery store containers)
  5. 1 ounce (30g) picked fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems (about 1 cup), finely chopped (Used one bunch)
  6. 1 small shallot, finely minced
  7. 1 medium clove garlic, finely minced
  8. 1 red jalapeño or Fresno chili, finely minced, (Used about 1/4 of a green bell)
  9. 1 tablespoon (15ml) red wine vinegar
  10. 3 tablespoons (45ml) extra-virgin olive oil (Needed a little more)
  11. salt/pepper
  12. Instead of mincing, I put everything in the food processor.  Needed to add a little extra oil to adjust the consistency
  13. chimichurri
  14. 1 bunch parsley
  15. mint
  16. 3 scallions
  17. 1 clove garlic
  18. 3 Tbs sherry vinegar
  19. 3 Tbs olive oil
  20. salt
  21. Chimichurri
  22. 1 cup packed fresh parsley, washed and dried
  23. 5 medium cloves garlic, peeled
  24. 2 tablespoons fresh oregano leaves
  25. ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  26. ¼ cup red wine vinegar
  27. 1 teaspoon (4g) Diamond Crystal kosher salt; for table salt, use about half as much by volume or the same weight
  28. 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  29. Place parsley, garlic, and oregano in the workbowl of a food processor. Pulse until finely chopped, stopping to scrape down the sides of the bowl as necessary.
  30. Transfer to a medium bowl and whisk in oil, vinegar, salt, and red pepper flakes. Use immediately or store in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
  31. 1 large clove garlic, minced
  32. 1 tablespoon chopped fresh rosemary
  33. 1 (1-pound) boneless leg of lamb
  34. Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  35. 1 teaspoon extra virgin olive oil
  36. 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  37. 1 tablespoon chopped onion
  38. 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  39. 2 tablespoons dry red wine
  40. 3/4 cup beef stock
  41. Set the Anova Sous Vide Precision Cooker to 131ºF (55ºC).
  42. Mix garlic and rosemary together in a small bowl. Rub over lamb. Season with salt and pepper and rub with olive oil.
  43. Place lamb in a large zipper lock bag. Seal the bag using the water immersion technique. Place the bag in the water bath and set the timer for 8 hours. Cover the water bath with plastic wrap to minimize water evaporation. Add water intermittently to keep the lamb submerged.
  44. About 5 minutes before the lamb is finished, heat the broiler to high.
  45. When the timer goes off, remove the bag from the water bath. Remove the lamb from the bag and pat dry with paper towels. Reserve cooking liquid.
  46. Step 1
  47. Broil lamb until well browned, about 5 minutes. Transfer to a cutting board and let rest, covered for 5 minutes.
  48. Step 2
  49. Meanwhile prepare the sauce: Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. When the butter stops foaming, add the onion and cook, stirring, for 1 minute.
  50. Step 3
  51. Whisk in the flour. Add the reserved cooking liquid and wine, stirring until smooth. Slowly whisk in stock. Bring the sauce to a simmer, reduce heat to medium-low, and simmer until thickened, 3 to 5 minutes. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  52. Step 4
  53. Thinly slice the lamb and serve with the sauce.
