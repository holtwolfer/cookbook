Leftovers - Pork Steak/Chops and Leftover Stuffing

Ingredients

  • Pork Steaks with Leftover Stuffing
  • 2 pork steaks, trimmed
  • bourbon
  • leftover stuffing
  • spike/pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375.
  2. Heat large cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add pork steaks and brown on both sides, about 4 minutes per side. Remove steaks. Deglaze with bourbon. Add stuffing to the skillet, place steaks on top and season with spike and pepper. Put in oven and cook until pork is 145 degrees, about 20 minutes.
