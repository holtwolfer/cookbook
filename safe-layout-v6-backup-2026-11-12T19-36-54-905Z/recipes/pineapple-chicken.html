Pineapple Chicken

Ingredients

  • Grilled Pineapple Chicken
  • 3 Tbs dark brown sugar
  • 2 Tbs rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1 Tbs fish sauce
  • 2 Tbs olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp aleppo pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1 cup diced fresh pineapple (1/2-inch chunks)
  • ¼ cup finely diced red onion
  • 2 Tbs finely chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 avocado, chopped
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 Tbs finely grated fresh pineapple, including accumulated juices
  • 1 ½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, butterflied or kebabbed
  • Cooked white rice, for serving

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, stir together the brown sugar, rice vinegar, soy sauce, fish sauce, 2 Tbs olive oil, salt/pepper, garlic powder, coriander, paprika, aleppo pepper.
  2. Transfer 1 Tbs of this marinade mixture to a separate medium bowl and add the diced pineapple, red onion, bell pepper, avocado, and cilantro. This is your salsa; toss until well mixed and set aside.
  3. Add the grated pineapple and its juices to the marinade mixture in the large bowl, then add the chicken and toss to coat. Set aside to marinate at room temperature for 15 minutes (and no longer). marinated without the pineapple longer.
  4. Grill the chicken. Cherry wood. Over low heat. Flip and add marinade to tops.
  5. Serve the chicken over rice and top with the reserved pineapple salsa.

Served with

  • coconut rice
