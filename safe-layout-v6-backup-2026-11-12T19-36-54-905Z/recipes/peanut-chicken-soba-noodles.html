Peanut Chicken Soba Noodles

Ingredients

  • 12 oz dried soba noodles, or other buckwheat noodles
  • 3 Tbs sesame oil
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onions (green tops only)
  • 1 jalapeno, seeded, stem removed, and minced
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp minced gingerroot
  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup chicken stock
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 Tbs fish sauce
  • 1 Tbs sherry
  • 2 Tbs fresh lime juice
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup chopped red bell pepper
  • sugar snap peas, cut in half
  • 1-2 carrots, matchsticked
  • 2 whole skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cut into strips and lightly poached, and chilled (see poaching recipe below)
  • 1 cucumber, peeled, seeded and chopped
  • 2 Tbs chopped roasted unsalted peanuts

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the noodles and cook until tender, about 7 minutes. Drain in a colander and rinse under cold running water. Drain and transfer to a large bowl. Toss with 1 Tbs of the sesame oil, cover and refrigerate.
  2. Quickly sautee the peas and carrots in peanut oil.
  3. In the bowl of a food processor, combine 2 tablespoons of the green onions, the jalapenos, garlic, and ginger, and process on high speed. Add the remaining 2 Tbs of sesame oil, the peanut butter, soy sauce, chicken stock, cilantro, fish sauce, sherry, lime juice, sugar, and pepper flakes. Toss the sauce to taste with the noodles.
  4. To serve, toss the noodles with the chicken strips, bell pepper, cucumber, peanuts, and remaining 2 Tbs of green onions, and serve.
  5. 2 cups chicken stock
  6. 2-3 scallions, chopped
  7. 1 inch piece of ginger, chopped
  8. 2 cloves garlic, smashed
  9. 2-3 chicken breasts, thinly sliced
  10. In a large saucepan, combine the broth, carrots, the 5 scallions, the smashed ginger, the garlic, soy sauce, peppercorns. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer, covered, for 15-20 minutes.
  11. Strain the stock or use a slotted spoon to remove all the solids.
  12. Add the sherry and chicken and bring back to a simmer over moderately low heat, covered. Turn the chicken and simmer, covered, until the chicken is just done, about 5 minutes.
