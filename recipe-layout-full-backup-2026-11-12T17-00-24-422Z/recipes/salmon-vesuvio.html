Salmon Vesuvio

Tags

sitkafish

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb coho fillet
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp dry thyme, or 1½ tsp fresh
  • 2 tsp dry oregano, or 1½ tsp fresh
  • 1 Tbs lemon pepper
  • ¼ cup chicken stock
  • ¼ cup lemon juice
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 2 Tbs butter

Instructions

  1. Mix together salt, thyme, oregano, and lemon pepper. Cover fillet in the spice mixture (the entire spice mixture may not be needed).
  2. In a cast-iron skillet, heat olive oil. Meanwhile, set oven to 325°F. When the oil is hot, place fillet flesh-side down and cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Flip salmon skin side down and add stock, lemon juice, and garlic. Spoon the sauce and garlic over the salmon and place in oven for 10-12 minutes.
  4. Remove from oven and place on stovetop over low heat. Add butter and spoon over salmon for a minute or two.
  5. Garnish with fresh oregano and thyme and serve warm.
