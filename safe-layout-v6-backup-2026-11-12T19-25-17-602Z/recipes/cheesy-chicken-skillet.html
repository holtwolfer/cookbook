Cheesy Chicken Skillet

Ingredients

  • chicken breasts, butterflied
  • onion
  • red bell pepper
  • broccoli
  • zucchini
  • garlic
  • carrot
  • mushrooms
  • mozzarella balls and shredded
  • prosciutto
  • basil
  • preheat oven to 400
  • brown chicken in cast iron skillet, remove
  • stuff chicken with basil, cheese, prosciutto

Instructions

  1. cook veggies, add sauce, bring to simmer and place chicken in, top with cheese put in oven about 30 mins
