Salmon Cakes

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs olive oil, more for frying
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • 1 lb salmon
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs white wine
  • 1 lb russet potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced (2 cups)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 Tbs roughly chopped cilantro
  • 1 scallion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp aleppo pepper
  • ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour

ghee

  • olive oil

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet over medium heat, heat olive oil. Add garlic and cook until golden brown at the edges, about 2 minutes.
  2. Season fish with salt/pepper, cook for 1 minute per side. Add white wine and 2 Tbs water. Turn heat to low, cover and cook until fish is just barely cooked through, 8 to 10 minutes. Move fish to a plate.
  3. In the same skillet over high heat, add potatoes, salt/pepper and water to just cover. Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer, cover and cook until tender, about 15 minutes.
  4. Flake fish.
  5. Drain potatoes and garlic, and place in a large bowl. Roughly mash potatoes and garlic, then add flaked fish, eggs, cilantro, basil, scallion, aleppo pepper, and panko, and gently combine.
  6. Place flour on a plate. Form generous 1/4 cup fish patties about 1/2 inch thick. Dip patties into flour to lightly coat each side.
  7. In a large nonstick skillet heat enough olive oil and ghee to cover the pan over medium heat. Cook fish cakes until golden brown, about 5 minutes per side, adding more oil/ghee as needed. Move to a paper-towel-lined plate.

Served with

  • salad w/ lemon vinaigrette
  • remoulade sauce
  • fries
  • baked potato
  • hasselback potato
  • lemon parmesan broccoli
