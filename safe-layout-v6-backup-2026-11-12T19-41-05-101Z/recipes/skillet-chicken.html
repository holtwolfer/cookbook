Skillet Chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken halves (or parts)
  • Marinade
  • -lemon thyme
  • -
  • Marinate chicken overnight.
  • Preheat oven to 375.

Instructions

  1. Heat large skillet over medium-high. Add olive oil and then put chicken in skin-side down and press down to flatten as much as possible (can use 2nd skillet). Cook for 5-10 minutes until nicely browned. Flip over and continue to cook on stove top for 5-10 minutes and then move to oven to finish cooking, about 30-45 minutes.
