Sweet & Sour Chicken Stir-Fry

Tags

asianstir-fryrice

Served with

  • rice
  • coconut rice

Ingredients

  • Chicken:
  • 1 lb boneless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 Tbs plus 1 tsp cornstarch, divided
  • peanut oil for frying
  • 1/2 cup sake for deglazing
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp galangal
  • Sauce:
  • 3/4 cup fresh orange juice
  • 2 Tbs soy sauce
  • 2 Tbs rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 Tbs mae ploy
  • 1 Tbs orange marmalade
  • 1 Tbs mirin
  • 1 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp dry mustard
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 1.5 Tbs minced peeled fresh ginger
  • 2-3 tsp corn starch
  • Veggies:
  • red bell pepper
  • sugar snap peas
  • bamboo shoots
  • collards
  • carrots
  • Thai basil
  • 4 (4-oz) baby bok choy, trimmed and halved lengthwise
  • 1-2 red bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 can bamboo shoots
  • 1-2 medium carrots, matchsticked
  • green beans
  • kale, chopped
  • 1-2 large garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds, toasted
  • 1 1/2 cups rice
  • chicken stock
  • Additons: green beans, bamboo shoots, broccoli, carrots, greens, kale, cabbage, snap peas, collards, chopped peanuts, sobe noodles in place of rice;
  • Chicken:

Instructions

  1. Toss chicken with 3 Tbs cornstarch; shake off excess. Heat 3 Tbs peanut oil in wok over high heat. Add chicken; cook until browned, about 8 minutes. Deglaze with sake as needed. Drizzle with soy sauce and sprinkle on galangal. Set aside; wipe wok clean.
  2. Combine orange juice, soy sauce, vinegar, honey, red pepper, and cornstarch and other sauce ingredients and heat in a sauce pan until thickened. May need to add more corn starch.
  3. Heat wok over medium; add 1 Tbs peanut oil and then all veggies, stirring frequently until softened. Drizzle with soy sauce and a little sake.
  4. Add chicken and mix in.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, serve with the sauce on the side.
