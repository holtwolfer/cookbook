Yelloweye Paillard

  • white wine and/or lemon juice
  • 1 (12-16 oz) yelloweye fillet
  • 1 cup flour
  • butter or olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • 5 oz arugula
  • 2 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1 fresno pepper, sliced

Instructions

  1. Marinate in white wine/lemon juice for 20-30 minutes
  2. Pat fish dry with paper towel.  Season with salt and pepper.  Dredge in flour, pressing to coat well.
  3. Heat olive oil or butter and fry on all sides about 3-4 mins per side.
  4. Salad: Combine arugula, lemon juice, 1/4 cup olive oil, salt, pepper.

Served with

  • salad
  • fish fry sides: slaw, fries or baked, steamed veggies
