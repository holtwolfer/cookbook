Coconut Milk Rice

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups jasmine rice
  • 1 bay leaf

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Additions: zest from 1 lime or lime juice or cilantro
  2. In a medium pot, bring coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt to a boil. Add rice, stir, and cover. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes. Add lime zest and fluff with a fork.
