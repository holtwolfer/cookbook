Crab Rolls

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Thursday, April 15, 2021

Tags

dungeness crab

Served with

  • fries

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1 1/4 cups aioli or mayonnaise
  • 2 large celery ribs, cut into 1/4-inch dice
  • 2 Tbs fresh lemon juice
  • paprika
  • 1 1/4 lbs lump crabmeat, picked over and lightly broken up
  • Salt
  • 8 hot dog buns or 16 mini brioche rolls, split
  • 8 Boston lettuce leaves, sliced
  • Butter the hot dog buns and toast in a non-stick skillet until lightly browned.

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. In a large bowl, mix the mayonnaise with the celery and lemon juice and season with cayenne. Gently fold in the crabmeat and season with salt. Fill the buns with the lettuce and the crab salad and serve.

Crab Rolls
1 1/4 cups aioli or mayonnaise
2 large celery ribs, cut into 1/4-inch dice
2 Tbs fresh lemon juice
paprika
1 1/4 lbs lump crabmeat, picked over and lightly broken up
Salt
8 hot dog buns or 16 mini brioche rolls, split
8 Boston lettuce leaves, sliced
Butter the hot dog buns and toast in a non-stick skillet until lightly browned.
In a large bowl, mix the mayonnaise with the celery and lemon juice and season with cayenne. Gently fold in the crabmeat and season with salt. Fill the buns with the lettuce and the crab salad and serve.
