Hungarian Goulash

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Friday, October 25, 2019

Tags

beef

Served with

  • egg noodles, spaetzle, crusty bread, sour cream, dill, side salad, kale salad, broccoli, brussels sprout hash
  • kraut

Related recipes

  • Dumplings

Ingredients

  • 1 - 1.5 lbs beef (chuck, stew meat, etc), 1 inch cubes
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3-4 large onions, minced (You can't have too many onions!)
  • 2 Tbs vegetable oil
  • 2-3 bell peppers, rough chop
  • 2-3 garlic
  • salt/pepper
  • paprika
  • tomato paste
  • beef stock + red wine + water
  • 2 bay leaves

Instructions

  1. Sautee onions in oil.
  2. Coat the beef in flour.
  3. Add bell peppers, cook a few minutes more.
  4. Add the garlic, salt, pepper, paprika, tomato paste, stir in and let the paste caramelize a little.
  5. Add enough stock and water to cover and bay leaves.
  6. Cover and simmer for at least 4 hours.
  7. Add more liquid if needed.
