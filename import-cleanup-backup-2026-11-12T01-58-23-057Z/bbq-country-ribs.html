BBQ Country Ribs

OneNote Pork section · Original page dated Tuesday, June 16, 2020

Tags

grillbarbecueporksummer

Served with

  • salt potatoes
  • grilled veggies

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • country ribs, bone-in preferred
  • BBQ rub:
  • paprika
  • aleppo pepper
  • salt/pepper
  • sumac
  • bbq sauce, like sweet baby rays

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

set up grill to smoke, sear ribs over higher heat in the middle of the grill and then switch to indirect grilling to slowly smoke.

smoke a while before slathering on bbq sauce

Longer cooking time…..

smoke with smoker box on high, far burner on low, with rub, finish over higher heat, brushed with bbq sauce.

Done at 145 degrees.
