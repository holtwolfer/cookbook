Ramp Chimichurri

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch ramps
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 Tbs sherry vinegar
  • salt/pepper
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. Place just the bulbs into a food processor or blender and process
  2. until minced. Then add the leaves, olive oil, red wine vinegar, salt
  3. and red pepper flakes. Blend until smooth.

Notes and variations
