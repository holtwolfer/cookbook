Pork Loin Roast

OneNote Pork section · Original page dated Tuesday, March 3, 2020

Tags

porkroast

Served with

  • Kevin Belton's red cabbage
  • fried squash
  • roasted potatoes

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 3 lb. boneless pork loin, well trimmed
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbs grainy mustard
  • dash of cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp. chopped rosemary
  • 1 tsp. chopped thyme leaves
  • salt/pepper
  • fresh rosemary sprigs
  • 3 Tbs melted butter
  • 1 Tbs brown sugar

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Preheat oven to 400°F.

In a small bowl, mix together garlic, mustard, rosemary, thyme, 1 1/2 teaspoons salt, and freshly ground black pepper.

Heat a large cast iron skillet and brown the pork loin on all sides.

Rub mixture all over pork loin and place in roasting pan fat-side down. Bake for 30 minutes, then carefully flip the loin and bake until a thermometer inserted into the middle of the meat reads 145°F, about 20 minutes.

Mix melted butter and brown sugar together, then brush on top of the pork loin. Broil for 2 minutes to let caramelize.

Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing into 1/3” pieces. Serve pork warm with extra pan juices.
