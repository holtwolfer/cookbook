Lemon Panko Sauce for Crab

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup panko
  • 1/2 Tbs lemon zest
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • pinch of cayenne
  • 1/4 cup butter

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, mix the panko with the lemon zest, parsley, salt and cayenne.
  2. Melt the butter and pour it into small bowl.
  3. Serve the panko mixture and butter separately, dipping the crab into melted butter and then into the panko.
