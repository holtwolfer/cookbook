Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Fish Rundown
  • 1-2 lbs cod
  • 2 Tbs grapeseed oil (neutral oil)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 large onion, chopped or scallions
  • (1 Scotch bonnet pepper, minced)
  • 2-3 bell peppers, rough chop
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1.5 -2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 cup tomato, chopped (or grape tomatoes, halved)
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • cilantro, chopped for garnish
  • Salt the cod lightly on both sides and let rest refrigerated for about an hour, at least 30 minutes and up to 24 hours.
  • (Alternatively cover the fish in lime juice and let sit for about 30 minutes)
  • Mango Rundown
  • 1-2 lbs cod
  • 2 Tbs grapeseed oil (neutral oil)
  • 1 medium red onion, chopped or scallions
  • (1 Scotch bonnet pepper, minced)
  • 1-2 red bell peppers, rough chop
  • salt/pepper
  • 1.5 -2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 mango, large chop (1.5 cups frozen cubes)
  • 1 lime, juiced

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Heat the oil in a wide sauce pan over medium-high heat and add the garlic, onion, peppers, salt, pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion softens, 5-10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the coconut milk and tomatoes, bring to a boil, cook, stirring occasionally, until reduced by about half.
  3. Rinse the fish and add it to the pot. Adjust the heat to a gentle simmer and cook until the fish is done. Add lime juice, garnish and serve.
  4. cilantro, chopped for garnish
  5. Some mango added early before reduction, the rest added later. Didn't seem to make a difference.
  6. Bok choi, cherry tomatoes, scallions

Notes

Additions: eggplant; bok choi;

Notes: was a little bland, maybe add spice, sugar, puree some of the mango
