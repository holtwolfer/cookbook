Leftovers - Turkey Enchiladas with Red Sauce

Ingredients

  • Turkey Enchiladas with Red Sauce
  • Red sauce:
  • 1-2 tbs butter
  • 4 tbs flour
  • onion powder
  • garlic powder
  • cumin
  • paprika
  • salt/pepper
  • chili powder
  • mexican oregano
  • tomato paste (1/2 jar)
  • 1.5 cups chicken stock
  • Enchiladas:
  • oil
  • leftover turkey, finely chopped
  • scallions, chopped
  • 2 roasted red bell peppers, chopped
  • paprika
  • ancho chile pepper
  • aleppo pepper
  • mexican oregano
  • spike/pepper
  • 2-3 tomatoes, chopped or cherry tomatoes, chopped
  • sauce
  • pepper jack, grated
  • colby jack, grated
  • cilantro
  • medium flour tortillas
  • Red Sauce:  Make roux; mix flour with spices, melt butter in sauce pan, whisk in flour mixture and continue to stir for 2 minutes.  Stir in paste and whisk for about 2 minutes.  Slowly add stock while whisking.  Adjust as needed for a thick sauce.  Remove from heat.
  • Enchiladas:

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in skillet and add the turkey and scallion whites, red pepper, spices. Stir to coat and add a little water or stock if it seems too dry. Mix in a little sauce, tomatoes, scallion greens, and cilantro and finally some of the grated cheese.
  2. Oil outside of large flour tortillas, roll with turkey mixture, place in baking dish. top with more grated cheese and reserved sauce.  bake at 350 for 20-30 minutes to melt cheese, heat through.
  3. Additions: corn, beans, rice
  4. serve with: cilantro, avocado, sour cream, guacamole, rice

Notes and variations
