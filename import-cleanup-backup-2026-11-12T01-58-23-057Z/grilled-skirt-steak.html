Grilled Skirt Steak

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Thursday, October 16, 2025

Tags

grill

Served with

  • rice
  • roasted potatoes
  • zucchini + peppers

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1.5 - 2 lbs skirt steaks (or flat iron / flank / hanger)
  • marinade:
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • olive oil
  • balsamic vinegar
  • soy sauce
  • salt/pepper
  • paprika

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Mix all marinade ingredients in a gallon bag and marinate the steak for 30-35 minutes.
  2. Heat grill to medium high heat or charcoal with hot and cool sides.
  3. rare: 125-130ºF
  4. medium rare: 130-135ºF
Original OneNote text (complete)
1.5 - 2 lbs skirt steaks (or flat iron / flank / hanger)
marinade:
1 clove garlic, minced
olive oil
balsamic vinegar
soy sauce
salt/pepper
paprika
Mix all marinade ingredients in a gallon bag and marinate the steak for 30-35 minutes.
Heat grill to medium high heat or charcoal with hot and cool sides.
rare: 125-130ºF
medium rare: 130-135ºF
served with: rice; roasted potatoes; zucchini + peppers;
tags: grill;
Green Sauce
1/2 cup cilantro, rough chop
1/2 cup parsley, rough chop
2-3 scallions, rough chop
1-2 cloves garlic, rough chop
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp aleppo pepper
salt/pepper
1/2 tsp coriander
1/2 tsp cumin
3 Tbs lemon juice
1/4 cup olive oil (original is 1/2 cup)
Combine everything but olive oil in food processor and  chop. While chopping slowly add the olive oil.
