Pimiento Cheese

From the OneNote Appetizers section · Original page dated Thursday, May 2, 2024

Review before cooking or publishing:
  • No clear preparation instructions found; structured data omitted.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz yellow cheddar, finely shredded (2 cups)
  • One 4-oz jar pimientos, drained and chopped
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 Tbs finely grated onion
  • 1 1/2 tsp hot sauce
  • chives
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Cheddar cheese with pimientos, mayonnaise, onion and Tabasco in a medium bowl. Stir until well incorporated. Season with salt.
