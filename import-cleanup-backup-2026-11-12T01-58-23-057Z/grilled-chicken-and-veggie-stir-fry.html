Grilled Chicken & Veggie 'Stir-fry'
Marinade:
3 Tbs soy sauce
2 Tbs sake
2 Tbs mirin
1 Tbs honey
3 scallions, chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 Tbs ginger
2-3 chicken breasts, butterflied
Veggie sauce:
1 Tbs soy sauce
1 Tbs rice vinegar
1 Tbs mirin
1 Tbs honey
pepper
paprika
Veggies:
snap peas
broccoli
cauliflower
kale
carrots
other stuff
Rice
Used biggest grill basket lined with foil as a grill 'wok' for the veggies.  Drizzled veggies with some oil and then the sauce.  Tossing them on the grill often.  Make sure to get some good smoke going.
Notes: very good with nice smokey flavor.  Good way to use farm veggies.  Ideally would be nice to thicken the veggie sauce a little or have extra for serving?  Would work really well with beef too.  Could try cooking meat on skewers or also wok style to get more of the marinade flavor.
