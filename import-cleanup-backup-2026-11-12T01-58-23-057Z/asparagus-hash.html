Asparagus Hash

Ingredients

  • olive oil and/or butter
  • proscuitto (optional)
  • spring onions/ramps/scallions/shallots
  • salt/pepper
  • lemon juice or sherry vinegar
  • Asparagus, cut into ~2 inch pieces (really thin asparagus worked well)

Instructions

  1. saute prosciutto if using and onion whites over medium heat
  2. add asparagus, saute until just tender, add onion greens and lemon juice or vinegar for last 1-2 mins of cooking
