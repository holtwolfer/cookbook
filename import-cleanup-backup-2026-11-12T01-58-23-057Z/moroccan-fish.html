Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

Moroccan Fish (Swordfish)

  • 1/2 cup cilantro, rough chop
  • 1/2 cup parsley, rough chop
  • 2-3 scallions, rough chop
  • 2 cloves garlic, rough chop
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp aleppo pepper
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 3 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil (original is 1/2 cup)
  • fish

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Finely chop the cilantro, parsley, scallions, garlic, spices, and lemon juice in a food processor. Drizzle the olive oil in with the processor running.
  2. You can use some of the charmoula as a marinade and then grill the fish.
  3. Or put some sauce on a sheet of foil, place the fish on top and cover with more on the top, reserving the rest for serving. Fold into a foil packet and let marinate for 1-4 hours and then grill in the foil, opening it up to start to get some smoke flavor.
