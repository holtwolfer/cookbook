Pulled Pork

Tags

pork

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • 3.5 lb Boston Butt (bone-in pork shoulder)

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    Rub:

    2 tsp salt

    2 tsp pepper

    1-2 Tbs paprika

    2 tsp onion powder

    2 tsp garlic powder

    Wood chip bath:

    1/2 onion, quartered

    2 cloves garlic, smashed

    1 cup cider vinegar

    2 to 3 apple to hickory chips

    water to cover

    Rub the shoulder the night before and keep refrigerated until ready to cook.

    Soak the wood chips for about an hour.

    Preheat the smoker to 275. Fill the water bowl with beer and water. Add chips. When they start to smoke lower the heat to 225 and place the butt on the 2nd highest rack.

    Hit the 165oF stall at about 4 or earlier. Turned the temp up to 235 and then to 275.

    took the butt off at 5:15 and it was tasty and tender, at about 175oF, but needed another hour or so. Next time plan for 8-9 hours.

    Original OneNote text (complete)
    3.5 lb Boston Butt (bone-in pork shoulder)
Rub:
2 tsp salt
2 tsp pepper
1-2 Tbs paprika
2 tsp onion powder
2 tsp garlic powder
Wood chip bath:
1/2 onion, quartered
2 cloves garlic, smashed
1 cup cider vinegar
2 to 3 apple to hickory chips
water to cover
Rub the shoulder the night before and keep refrigerated until ready to cook.
Soak the wood chips for about an hour.
Preheat the smoker to 275.  Fill the water bowl with beer and water.  Add chips.  When they start to smoke lower the heat to 225 and place the butt on the 2nd highest rack.
Notes: outdoor temp at start time: 29oF; about 30 mins to reach 275oF  added chips 15 mins into preheating.  No smoke at 30 mins.  Pork on at 10:05. Three bottles of beer in the water bowl.
Hit the 165oF stall at about 4 or earlier.  Turned the temp up to 235 and then to 275.
took the butt off at 5:15 and it was tasty and tender, at about 175oF, but needed another hour or so.  Next time plan for 8-9 hours.
