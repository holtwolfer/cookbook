Salmon Caper Scrambler

Ingredients

  • butter
  • thinly sliced red onion
  • capers
  • jalapeno or habanero, minced
  • smoked salmon / lox
  • eggs
  • cream cheese or sour cream

Instructions

  1. sautee onion, capers, peppers in butter until just softened.
  2. Add salmon and then eggs
  3. scramble
  4. when eggs are mostly set stir in the cream cheese or sour cream
