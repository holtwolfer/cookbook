Garlicky Kale Parmesan Salad

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (about 3-4 lemons)
  • 2 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 2 Tbs sherry vinegar
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 1/2 cups extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic (1 large clove)
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 1-2 Tbs honey
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp dry mustard
  • 1 bunch kale, torn and stems removed
  • 1 cup freshly grated parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Combine the lemon juice, salt, extra virgin olive oil, garlic and spices in a lidded jar or salad dressing container. Close the lid and shake vigorously. Set aside for at least 20 minutes, so the dressing is infused with garlic flavor.
  2. Tear the kale into bite-sized pieces, discarding any thick stems. Before serving, toss with salad dressing and top with a hearty sprinkle of parmesan cheese.
  3. I think it's a little better when it has some time to soak in the dressing.
