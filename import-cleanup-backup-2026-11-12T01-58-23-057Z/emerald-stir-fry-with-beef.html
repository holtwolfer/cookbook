Emerald Stir-fry with Beef

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Friday, September 20, 2019

Tags

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • Marinade:
    • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
    • soy sauce
    • mirin
    • lime juice
    • avocado oil
    • paprika
    • salt/pepper
    • flank steak/skirt steak
    • Sauce:
    • 1/2 cup mirin or semisweet white wine, like Riesling
    • 1/2 cup orange juice
    • 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
    • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
    • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
    • 2 tablespoons peanut oil
    • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
    • Veggies:
    • 1 bunch tatsoi
    • 1 large carrot, matchsticked
    • 2 small red bell peppers
    • green beans, cut in half
    • (see veggie note below)
    • 4 teaspoons cornstarch dissolved in 1/4 cup warm water
    • 1 teaspoon sesame oil to drizzle

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    1. Start cooking some rice. (cous cous was good too)
    2. Marinate flank steak. Grill (pull off a little under done). Rest. Slice.
    3. Combine mirin and/or white wine, orange juice, soy sauce, rice vinegar, red pepper flakes in a small bowl.
    4. Heat oil in wok. Cook garlic 30 seconds and then add veggies. Cook until starting to soften. Add sauce and continue to cook until veggies are close to done. Add beef. Add cornstarch. Drizzle with sesame oil.

Start cooking some rice.  (cous cous was good too)
Marinate flank steak. Grill (pull off a little under done).  Rest.  Slice.
Combine mirin and/or white wine, orange juice, soy sauce, rice vinegar, red pepper flakes in a small bowl.
Heat oil in wok.  Cook garlic 30 seconds and then add veggies. Cook until starting to soften.  Add sauce and continue to cook until veggies are close to done.  Add beef.  Add cornstarch. Drizzle with sesame oil.
Notes: add some orange marmalade to the sauce; make sure beef is towards the rare side on the grill
veggie note:
-original recipe:
2 cups fresh snow peas (6 ounces)
1 large bunch broccoli (1 1/4 pounds) trimmed and cut into small florets
1 bunch asparagus (1 pound), trimmed and sliced on diagonal into 2-inch pieces
2 cups (8 ounces) frozen shelled edamame
-could also use:
shredded cabbage, broccoli, bamboo shoots, zucchini, sprouts,
original recipe:
In a large wok or very large (14-inch) saute pan, heat 1 tablespoon of oil over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook, stirring, until just browned, about 2 minutes. Transfer the beef to a plate.
Heat the remaining tablespoon of oil over medium heat and cook garlic until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add snow peas, broccoli, asparagus and edamame, raise heat to medium-high, and cook for 3 minutes until vegetables are slightly softened. Add the mirin-soy mixture and cook, stirring, until edamame are cooked and asparagus is crisp-tender, about 4 minutes. Add the beef and dissolved cornstarch and stir to incorporate. Cook until mixture thickens slightly and beef is heated through, an additional 2 minutes. Drizzle with sesame oil and serve.
