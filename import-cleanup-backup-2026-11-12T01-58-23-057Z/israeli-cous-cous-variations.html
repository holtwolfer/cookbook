Israeli Cous Cous Variations

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Cous cous
  • olive oil
  • onion, minced
  • carrot, minced
  • red bell pepper, minced
  • broccoli, stalk minced, florets cut small
  • broccoli raab, chopped
  • 1 1/4 cup Israeli cous cous
  • chicken stock

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Heat oil in sauce pan. Add onions, bell pepper, broccoli stalks, carrots, and cook until softened. Add broccoli florets and broccoli raab. Add cous and a little more oil, stir to coat, and add chicken stock. Bring to boil, cover, lower to simmer. Cook until all stock is absorbed, about 7 minutes.
