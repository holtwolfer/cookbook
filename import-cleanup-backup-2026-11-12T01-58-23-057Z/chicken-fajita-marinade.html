Chicken Fajita Marinade
1 orange, juiced
2 limes, juiced and zested
4 Tbs avocado oil
2 cloves garlic, minced
3 chipotle chilis in adobo
3 Tbs cilantro, chopped
1 tsp cumin
1/2 Tbs paprika
1 Tbs worcestershire sauce
2 Tbs brown sugar
1 tsp salt
tortillas
onions
peppers
tomatoes
cheese
cilantro
avocado
guac
sour cream
Mix all ingredients together, marinate 4 hours to overnight.
