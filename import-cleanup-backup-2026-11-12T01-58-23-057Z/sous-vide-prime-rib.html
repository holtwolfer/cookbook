Sous Vide Prime Rib

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Thursday, December 17, 2020

Tags

beef

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • 3 lb bone-in beef ribeye roast
    • Kosher salt
    • 1 Tbs black peppercorns, coarsely ground
    • 1 Tbs green peppercorns, coarsely ground
    • 1 Tbs pink peppercorns, coarsely ground
    • 1 Tbs dried celery seeds
    • 2 Tbs dried garlic powder
    • 4 sprigs fresh rosemary, finely minced
    • 1 quart beef stock (low-sodium if using store-bought)
    • 2 egg whites
    • Generously season the beef with kosher salt and place in the refrigerator for at least one hour (and up to 12 hours).

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    1. Set Anova Sous Vide Precision Cooker to 132°F (55.5°C).
    2. Place beef in a large Ziplock or vacuum bag, seal, and place in the water bath. Set the timer for six hours.
    3. Finishing Steps
    4. Preheat the oven to 425°F / 218°C. Remove the beef from the bag, reserving the cooking liquid, and pat dry.
    5. Whisk together the peppercorns, celery seeds, garlic powder, and rosemary.
    6. Brush top and sides of roast with egg white, season with salt, and coat with spice mixture
    7. Place beef on a sheet ban with baking rack and roast in the oven for 10-15 minutes, until the crust is dark golden brown. Remove the roast from the oven and let rest for 10-15 minutes before carving.
    8. While the roast rests, in a large sauce pan, bring the sous vide cooking liquid and beef stock to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer until reduced by half. Skim off and discard any solids that float to the top.
    9. Carve the roast, trimming off the rib bones and slicing into six equal portions.
    10. Serve with beef jus.
    11. Cheese Sauce for Veggies
    12. 2 Tbs butter 2 Tbs gluten-free or all-purpose flour salt/pepper 1 cup milk (works best with 2% milk) 1-1/2 cups freshly shredded cheddar cheese (not pre-grated)
    13. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add flour then whisk for one minute. Slowly whisk in milk then season with salt and pepper and switch to a wooden spoon. Stir and cook until thickened, about 4-5 minutes. Turn off heat then add cheese and stir until melted and smooth. Taste then season with additional salt and pepper if necessary. Serve immediately.     14. Yorkshire Pudding
    15. 1 cup regular flour do not use self-rising flour
    16. ½ tsp salt (I use less)
    17. 1 egg
    18. 10 oz milk (1 ¼ cups or ½ English pint)
    19. Oil or pan drippings
    20. Directions
    21. Basic English Batter Recipe:
    22. Sift flour and salt into bowl and make a well in the center. Drop in the egg and half the milk. Mix in flour, a little at a time, using a wooden spoon until a creamy mixture is obtained. Beat for 5-10 minutes until mixture is full of bubbles. Stir in rest of milk and cover and allow to stand or you can use it at once for Y.P. I usually doubled the recipe to make about 16 puddings.

    24. Place a small amount of meat fat or marg/butter in each section of a cupcake pan. Heat pan in oven until fat is sizzling hot. Remove from oven and fill each section about 2/3 full with batter. Cook at 425 degrees about 20-30 minutes. They are done when they are well risen and browned and come out of the pans easily.

Cheese Sauce for Veggies
2 Tbs butter
2 Tbs gluten-free or all-purpose flour
salt/pepper
1 cup milk (works best with 2% milk)
1-1/2 cups freshly shredded cheddar cheese (not pre-grated)
Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add flour then whisk for one minute. Slowly whisk in milk then season with salt and pepper and switch to a wooden spoon. Stir and cook until thickened, about 4-5 minutes. Turn off heat then add cheese and stir until melted and smooth. Taste then season with additional salt and pepper if necessary. Serve immediately.

Yorkshire Pudding
1 cup regular flour do not use self-rising flour
½ tsp salt (I use less)
1 egg
10 oz milk (1 ¼ cups or ½ English pint)
Oil or pan drippings
Directions
Basic English Batter Recipe:
Sift flour and salt into bowl and make a well in the center. Drop in the egg and half the milk. Mix in flour, a little at a time, using a wooden spoon until a creamy mixture is obtained. Beat for 5-10 minutes until mixture is full of bubbles. Stir in rest of milk and cover and allow to stand or you can use it at once for Y.P. I usually doubled the recipe to make about 16 puddings.
To Cook: Oven 425 degrees
Place a small amount of meat fat or marg/butter in each section of a cupcake pan. Heat pan in oven until fat is sizzling hot. Remove from oven and fill each section about 2/3 full with batter. Cook at 425 degrees about 20-30 minutes. They are done when they are well risen and browned and come out of the pans easily.
