Dunlay’s on the Square Hashbrowns

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 2½ lbs russet potatoes shredded, squeezed dry
  • ¾ cup grated or chopped onions
  • 2 cups heavy cream (1 pint)
  • 2 Tbs garlic (original recipe calls for roasted)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 Tbs chopped fresh Parsley
  • 1 tsp thyme (used fresh)
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp Spike or seasoned salt
  • 2 Tbs melted butter for brushing

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Preheat oven to 450ºF and grease a 9x13 baking dish (red oval baking dish)
  2. Mix together all ingredients except for butter and bake for 45 minutes - 1 hour uncovered, brushing with butter halfway through to promote browning.
  3. Allow 15 minutes rest time for potatoes to set up.
  4. Notes: could try half cream, half milk; check tatoes in middle, it was a little undercooked after about 45 minutes of cooking and should have gone longer.
