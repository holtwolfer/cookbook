Lasagna

OneNote Pasta section · Original page dated Wednesday, February 19, 2020

Tags

pasta

Served with

    Ingredients

    Sauce

    • 1 lb Italian sausage
    • 1/4 cup olive oil
    • 1 red onion, chopped
    • 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
    • 4-5 anchovies, minced into paste
    • 1/4 cup white whine
    • 1 jar tomato paste
    • 1 pint jar tomato sauce
    • 2 - 28oz cans crushed tomatoes
    • roasted red peppers

    Eggplant

    • 2-3 large eggplants
    • salt/pepper
    • olive oil

    Spinach/Mushrooms

    • 1 pkg shitaake or cremini mushrooms, chopped
    • 1 lb fresh spinach
    • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
    • olive oil

    Ricotta

    • 15 oz. ricotta cheese
    • 1 lb pumpkin puree
    • 2 eggs
    • grated mozzarella
    • 4 oz. parmesan cheese

    Cheeses & Assembly

    • 16 oz. fresh mozzarella
    • 16 oz. fontina
    • 12 oz lasagna noodles
    • 9x13 pan

    Instructions

    Sauce

    1. Remove sausage from casing and heat in large dutch oven over medium heat until cooked through and then drain the grease. Add the olive oil and onions and cook until tender. Add the garlic and anchovies and cook 1 minute. Deglaze the pan with the white wine, scraping up any brown bits. Raise heat to medium high. Add the paste and let it caramelize a little bit over 2-3 minutes. Add the tomato sauce and canned tomatoes. Bring to a simmer and then reduce heat to low for a slow simmer. Cook for about 1 hour until the sauce has become very thick.

    Eggplant

    1. Preheat oven to 400. Slice the eggplant lengthwise into thin (1/4") slices, slicing the peel off of the ends. Lay out on parchment-lined baking sheets and sprinkle with the salt/pepper and drizzle with olive oil. Roast in the oven for about 20 minutes until very lightly browned, partially dried out, but still soft.

    Spinach/Mushrooms

    1. Heat oil in non-stick pan, add mushrooms and then add spinach a handful at a time, allowing it to cook down.

    Ricotta

    1. Whisk the egg in a bowl, mix together remaining ingredients.

    Notes

    For the perfect lasagna: pre-made meatballs sliced/cut in half would be super duper awesome. Make a meat sauce and the simple red sauce used for chicken parm.

    Could use a little more sauce. Make sure it has time to thicken.
