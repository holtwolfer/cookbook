Pickled Ranch Dip

From the OneNote Appetizers section · Original page dated Monday, March 1, 2021

Ingredients

  • 8 pickle chips or 1-2 spears, finely chopped
  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • 3 Tbs finely chopped fresh dill
  • 3 Tbs thinly sliced fresh chives
  • ½ tsp garlic salt (optional)
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Add the pickles to a bowl, add mayonnaise, dill, chives, garlic salt, onion powder and 1/3 cup buttermilk. Stir to combine, season to taste with salt and chill until ready to serve.
