Greek Lamburgers

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1 lb ground lamb
  • mint, chopped
  • oregano
  • paprika
  • salt/pepper
  • spinach leaves, chopped
  • feta
  • red onion, thinly sliced
  • tomato, sliced
  • lettuce or spinach
  • Sous vide @ 135 for 45 minutes

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Mix fresh mint, oregano, paprika, salt/pepper into the lamb and shape into patties.
  2. Sous vide.
  3. Finish on the grill over very high heat to brown the outsides.
  4. Notes: lamb was very watery and had to be dried by squeezing with paper towels. Finished on cast iron griddle on the grill.
  5. Secret Sauce:
  6. tomato vinaigrette + yogurt = secret sauce

Served with

Greek potato wedges; Greek salad
