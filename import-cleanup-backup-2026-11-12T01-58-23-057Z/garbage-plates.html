    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    1. Use a sauce pan.
    2. Sautee onions in butter, season with salt and pepper
    3. Add the garlic, all the spices, toast for 1 min, then add the tomato paste, and allow it to caramelize a bit. Add the mustard, Worcestershire sauce, Franks and then stock to the right runny consistency
    4. Brown ground beef in a separate skillet and then add to the sauce and simmer over low heat, adding a little more water or stock if needed. Simmer at least 20-30 minutes.
    5. Use immersion blender to get a finer texture similar to triple ground beef.

    17. grapeseed oil

    19. paprika
    20. Preheat air-fryer to 400 degrees.
    21. In a large bowl toss the hashbrowns with oil, sal/pepper, and paprika.
    22. Cook for around 20 minutes tossing a few times.
    23. Air Fryer Fries with Sauce
    24. 1 large russet potato (about 8 ounces), cut into 1/4-inch-thick sticks
    25. 1 ½ tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
    26. 1 ½ teaspoons kosher salt, more as needed
    27. Sauce: (see also remoulade sauce below)
    28. ¼ cup mayonnaise
    29. 2 tablespoons sour cream or plain whole-milk Greek yogurt
    30. 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
    31. ¼ teaspoon smoked paprika
    32. Put potatoes in a large bowl and cover with cold water. Soak for at least 30 minutes (or up to overnight, stored in the refrigerator), then drain and pat very dry.
    33. Heat the air fryer to 350 degrees, if preheating is necessary. Line a rimmed baking sheet with paper towels.
    34. In a dry bowl, toss potatoes with 1 tablespoon oil and 1 1/2 teaspoons salt. Transfer to air fryer and fry at 350 degrees for 10 minutes, tossing halfway. Transfer the potatoes to the baking sheet, spreading them in an even layer, and let cool to room temperature, at least 30 minutes and up to 4 hours. (This step is optional; it gives the fries a slightly crispier exterior. If you want to skip it, keep the potatoes in the fryer and proceed to the next step.)
    35. Turn the air fryer heat up to 400 degrees. Arrange potatoes in the fryer if you’ve taken them out, and drizzle with 1/2 tablespoon oil. Cook for until golden and crisp, about 8 to 10 minutes, tossing or stirring halfway through. Transfer immediately to a serving platter and sprinkle with more salt.
    36. While the fries are cooking, make the sauce: In a small bowl, stir together mayonnaise, sour cream, mustard and paprika. Serve alongside the fries for dipping.

Air Fryer Fries with Sauce
1 large russet potato (about 8 ounces), cut into 1/4-inch-thick sticks
1 ½ tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
1 ½ teaspoons kosher salt, more as needed
Sauce:  (see also remoulade sauce below)
¼ cup mayonnaise
2 tablespoons sour cream or plain whole-milk Greek yogurt
1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
¼ teaspoon smoked paprika
Put potatoes in a large bowl and cover with cold water. Soak for at least 30 minutes (or up to overnight, stored in the refrigerator), then drain and pat very dry.
Heat the air fryer to 350 degrees, if preheating is necessary. Line a rimmed baking sheet with paper towels.
In a dry bowl, toss potatoes with 1 tablespoon oil and 1 1/2 teaspoons salt. Transfer to air fryer and fry at 350 degrees for 10 minutes, tossing halfway. Transfer the potatoes to the baking sheet, spreading them in an even layer, and let cool to room temperature, at least 30 minutes and up to 4 hours. (This step is optional; it gives the fries a slightly crispier exterior. If you want to skip it, keep the potatoes in the fryer and proceed to the next step.)
Turn the air fryer heat up to 400 degrees. Arrange potatoes in the fryer if you’ve taken them out, and drizzle with 1/2 tablespoon oil. Cook for until golden and crisp, about 8 to 10 minutes, tossing or stirring halfway through. Transfer immediately to a serving platter and sprinkle with more salt.
While the fries are cooking, make the sauce: In a small bowl, stir together mayonnaise, sour cream, mustard and paprika. Serve alongside the fries for dipping.
