Beef Spiedies

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Thursday, October 7, 2021

Tags

beef

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • Beef or Lamb Spiedies
    • 1 cup olive oil
    • 1/4 cup white vinegar
    • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
    • 1 clove garlic, chopped
    • 2 Tbs chopped mint
    • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper
    • 1/2 Tbs paprika
    • 1 1/2 tsp dried basil
    • 1 1/2 tsp dried oregano
    • 1 tsp dried thyme
    • 1/2 tsp dried parsley
    • 1/2 tsp onion powder
    • 2 bay leaves
    • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
    • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
    • 1.5 to 2 pounds cubed beef
    • Veggies
    • Green and red bell peppers, cut into small squares
    • Mushrooms
    • Onions, wedged
    • Cherry or grape tomatoes
    • pita bread or hot dog rolls (preferably New England-style)
    • Tzatziki sauce:
    • 1 English cucumber, seeded, chopped
    • 2 cups Greek yogurt
    • 1/2 cup sour cream
    • 1/4 cup mayo
    • 2 Tbs lemon juice
    • 1/2 Tbs dill
    • optional: garlic, mint

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    1. Whisk together the yogurt, sour cream, mayo, lemon juice, dill, garlic, and mint (if using). Stir the cucumber in with a spoon.
    2. Lamb - 145 degrees
