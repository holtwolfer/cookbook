Easy Gravy

  • rosemary garlic mashed potatoes

  • corn

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 4 Tbs butter
  • 1.5 cups stock
  • 4 Tbs flour
  • salt/pepper

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Melt butter in a sauce pan, add the flour and whisk constantly until lightly browned, about 2 minutes. Slowly add the broth, whisking constantly. Bring to a boil, season with salt and pepper.
  2. Add more stock if too thick.
