Brisket

Ingredients

  • Rub:
  • 2 Tbs ground black pepper
  • 1/2 Tbs white pepper
  • 1.5 Tbs kosher salt
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • Spray:
  • ~2 Tbs cider vinegar
  • ~1 cup water

Instructions

  1. Smoked Brisket
  2. 6.5 lb brisket
  3. maple chips
  4. The night before smoking, trim the brisket and make the rub.
  5. On the bottom of the brisket there is a chunk of fat where the point and the flat connect.
  6. Trim it out.
  7. On the day of the smoke remove the brisket, apply the rub and allow it to warm to room temperature over an hour. Meanwhile, heat the smoker to 275oF. (See notes)
  8. Use dry maple chips.
  9. Don't open the door for 3 hours.
  10. After 3 hours check on it and spray. Only spray every 30 minutes.
  11. The stall should occur between 160-170.
  12. When the stall is done, wrap and continue to cook. Remove at 203ish or when it seems very soft, less rubbery, like it would break apart by hand. Remove and place in a cooler until ready to serve. Should rest for at least an hour.
  13. See Franklin BBQ p 183 for slicing instructions
  14. Cut off the burnt end of the flat opposite the point end.
  15. Cut 1/4 inch strips along the flat until you reach the spot where the flat meets the point, where the strip of fat was removed earlier.
  16. Turn the point end 90o so the cut side is facing you.
  17. Pull back on the right side of the point and there should be a slab of fat there to cut off.
  18. Turn 180o so the cut-side is facing away from you.
  19. Slice the point. It may be easier to slice it in half first.

Notes and variations

Served with: smoked beans; potato salad; collards; slices white bread; slaw; salt potatoes;

Highland Springs Brisket

On at 8:30, set to 275, reading about 200

3 hours in the thermometer read 165

sprayed for the first time

4 hours read 169

wrapped at 7 hrs temp 172

temp went up to 190 per smoking thermometer, 183 per meat thermometer, moved to grill to cook at higher temp with the beans, never really went up. Had to remove and let rest with 30 mins before dinner time. Seemed a little undercooked.

not sure if it should have been wrapped earlier.

good idea to move to grill with beans when wrapped… clean up smoker parts, less work later. can still smoke beans more.

2020-03-28 Notes:

Smoker started about an hour ahead of time, set to 275, was holding around 265 after an hour of smoking. Outside temp was 44 degrees. Started at 8:20

Checked at 11:30 and the internal temperature was around 180. The stall should be between 160-170. Let it smoke another 15-20 minutes and then wrapped it and put back on.

Moved to the cooler at about 3:45 and let rest until 5:15.

The fat on the top did not render quite enough. Probably should have left it unwrapped a little longer or smoked longer at a lower temp?

Turned out freakin' awesome otherwise.

Texas Potato Salad

1/2 cup red wine vinegar

1 1/2 tablespoons sugar

Salt and pepper

1 teaspoon yellow mustard seeds

1/2 small red onion, sliced thin

2 jalapeño chiles (1 sliced into thin rings; 1 stemmed, seeded, and minced)

3 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 3/4-inch pieces

6 tablespoons mayonnaise

6 tablespoons yellow mustard

1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

2 large hard-cooked eggs, cut into 1/4-inch pieces

1 celery rib, minced

add dill

Combine vinegar, sugar, 1½ teaspoons salt, and mustard seeds in bowl and microwave until steaming, about 2 minutes. Whisk until sugar and salt are dissolved. Add onion and jalapeños and set aside until cool, 15 to 20 minutes. Strain onion and jalapeños through fine-mesh strainer set over bowl. Reserve pickled vegetables and vinegar mixture separately. Meanwhile, combine potatoes, 8 cups water, and 1 tablespoon salt in Dutch oven and bring to boil over high heat. Reduce heat to medium and simmer until potatoes are just tender, 10 to 15 minutes. Drain potatoes thoroughly, then transfer to large bowl. Drizzle 2 tablespoons reserved vinegar mixture over hot potatoes and toss gently until evenly coated. (Reserve remaining vinegar mixture for another use.) Refrigerate until cool, about 30 minutes, stirring once halfway through chilling. Whisk mayonnaise, mustard, ½ teaspoon pepper, and cayenne together in bowl until combined. Add mayonnaise mixture, reserved pickled vegetables, eggs, and celery to potatoes and stir gently to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cover and refrigerate to let flavors blend, about 30 minutes. Serve. (Salad can be refrigerated for up to 2 days.)
