Chicken Enchiladas with Red Sauce

OneNote Chicken section · Original page dated Saturday, September 28, 2019

Tags

Thanksgivingleftoversturkeymexican

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Chicken:
  • chicken tenders (or breasts)
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • cilantro stems
  • paprika
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • cumin
  • tequila
  • mexican oregano
  • salt/pepper
  • Red sauce:
  • 1-2 tbs butter
  • 4 tbs flour
  • onion powder
  • garlic powder
  • cumin
  • paprika
  • salt/pepper
  • chili powder
  • mexican oregano
  • tomato paste (1/2 jar)
  • 1.5 cups chicken stock
  • tomato roasted on grill
  • more tomatoes, chopped
  • Enchiladas:
  • 2 ears corn, roasted on grill
  • 1/2 red onion, roasted on grill, chopped
  • 2 red bell peppers, roasted on grill, peeled, chopped
  • 2-3 tomatoes
  • sauce
  • queso fresco, grated
  • colby jack, grated
  • cilantro
  • Options: black or pinto beans (could be sauteed with onion and the peppers....)
  • large flour tortillas
  • serve with: cilantro, avocado, sour cream
  • Red Sauce:  Make roux; mix flour with spices, melt butter in sauce pan, whisk in flour mixture and continue to stir for 2 minutes.  Stir in paste and whisk for about 2 minutes.  Slowly add stock while whisking.  Adjust as needed for a thick sauce.  Remove from heat.  Add in tomato chunks.
  • Enchiladas:
  • corn, bell peppers, tomato roasted over high heat.  (Be careful with tomato, half fell through grate when picking up with tongs)

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Grill chicken over medium heat.  Want to keep it nice and moist.  oil grate. in large bowl mix pulled chicken, onion, corn, bell peppers, some sauce, some chopped tomato, queso fresco and colby jack.  Oil outside of large flour tortillas, roll with chicken mixture, place in baking dish. top with more grated cheese and reserved sauce.  bake at 400 for 10-15 minutes to melt cheese, heat through.
  2. Turkey Enchiladas with Red Sauce
  3. tomato paste (1/2 jar)
  4. 1.5 cups chicken stock
  5. leftover turkey, finely chopped
  6. 2 roasted red bell peppers, chopped
  7. 2-3 tomatoes, chopped or cherry tomatoes, chopped
  8. medium flour tortillas
  9. Red Sauce:  Make roux; mix flour with spices, melt butter in sauce pan, whisk in flour mixture and continue to stir for 2 minutes.  Stir in paste and whisk for about 2 minutes.  Slowly add stock while whisking.  Adjust as needed for a thick sauce.  Remove from heat.
  10. Heat oil in skillet and add the turkey and scallion whites, red pepper, spices. Stir to coat and add a little water or stock if it seems too dry. Mix in a little sauce, tomatoes, scallion greens, and cilantro and finally some of the grated cheese.
  11. Oil outside of large flour tortillas, roll with turkey mixture, place in baking dish. top with more grated cheese and reserved sauce.  bake at 350 for 20-30 minutes to melt cheese, heat through.
  12. Additions: corn, beans, rice
  13. serve with: cilantro, avocado, sour cream, guacamole, rice
