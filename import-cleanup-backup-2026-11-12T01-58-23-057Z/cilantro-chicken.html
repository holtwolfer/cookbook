Cilantro Chicken

OneNote Chicken section · Original page dated Thursday, February 25, 2021

Tags

AsianThaistir-fry

Served with

  • coconut milk rice

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 3 chicken breasts, thinly sliced
  • marinade:
  • avocado oil
  • rice wine vinegar
  • lime juice
  • soy sauce
  • fish sauce
  • mirin
  • sake to deglaze
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ginger, grated
  • lime zest
  • soy sauce
  • fish sauce
  • lemongrass
  • thai basil
  • galangal
  • cilantro, chopped, reserve some for serving
  • 2 cans coconut milk (or it doesn't make enough snauce)
  • juice of 1 small lime
  • carrots
  • broccoli
  • bamboo shoots
  • bell pepper

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. heat oil in wok, cook chicken in batches until just cooked through, move to bowl
  2. deglaze with marinade or sake while cooking and after
  3. add veggies and cook until just cooked through deglazing with sake if needed
  4. add small amount of oil and then lemongrass, ginger, soy sauce, fish sauce, galangal, bangkok spice and caramelize a little before adding coconut milk, lime juice, most of the cilantro and basil, reserving some to add later
  5. bring to a simmer and then add the chicken and veggies back and simmer briefly
  6. Heat peanut oil in wok, cook chicken in a couple of batches, removing to bowl covered in foil.  Season lightly with pepper and galangal and drizzle with soy sauce while cooking.
  7. Deglaze wok with sake, let it burn off a little, add garlic, ginger, soy sauce, fish sauce, thai basil, galangal, simmer a few minutes then add coconut milk, lime zest, some of the cilantro and lime juice.  Bring to light boil, add chicken and stir over low heat for a few minutes.
  8. 1 1/2 cups jasmine rice
  9. Additions: zest from 1 lime or lime juice or cilantro
  10. In a medium pot, bring coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt to a boil. Add rice, stir, and cover. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes. Add lime juice and cilantro and fluff with a fork.

Coconut Milk Rice
1 can coconut milk
1 1/4 cup water
1 tsp sugar
1/4 tsp salt
1 1/2 cups jasmine rice
1 bay leaf
juice of 1 lime
cilantro
Additions: zest from 1 lime or lime juice or cilantro
In a medium pot, bring coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt to a boil. Add rice, stir, and cover. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes. Add lime juice and cilantro and fluff with a fork.
