Pork Carnitas

OneNote Pork section · Original page dated Tuesday, May 5, 2020

Tags

Mexicanporktacosslow cooker

Served with

  • pickled red onions
  • sour cream
  • salsa verde

Ingredients

Carnitas

  • 1 pork loin roast (usually shoulder or butt or country ribs)
  • 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion
  • 4 Tbs orange juice, orange reserved
  • 2 Tbs lime juice
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 tsp marjoram
  • 2 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1-2 cups water

Salsa Verde

  • 8-10 tomatillos
  • 1-2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 bunch cilantro
  • 1/2 tsp coriander

Pickled Onions

  • 1 medium red onion
  • 6 slices jalapeno
  • 1/4 cup cider vinegar
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • salt/white pepper
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp sugar

Instructions

Carnitas

Cut the pork into big chunks and brown in the slow cooker. (Cook with any bones as well)

Process the onion, garlic, oj, lime juice.

Add the spices to the slow cooker to toast for 30 seconds, then add the onion puree and mix everything together. Add the 2 cups chicken stock and water to mostly cover the pork.

Bring to a boil and then switch to the high setting. Cook 4-6 hours, stirring and flipping the pork occasionally.

Remove the pork from the cooker and shred. Heat a skillet and brown the meat a little, spooning in some of the cooking liquid.

Salsa Verde

Cook the tomatillos and garlic cloves in boiling water for about 10 minutes, until the color darkens and they are softened. Reserve about 1/4 cup of the cooking liquid in the pan for pickled onions.

Let cool a little and then process with remailing ingredients.

Pickled Onions

Heat the vinegar, lime juice and salt/pepper, sugar to a boil. Pour over the onions and peppers.
