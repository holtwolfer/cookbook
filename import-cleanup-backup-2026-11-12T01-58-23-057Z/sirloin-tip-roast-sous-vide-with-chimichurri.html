    Original OneNote text (complete)
    Prep:
Roast garlic
Chimichurri:
1 cup finely chopped parsley leaves
1/3 cup olive oil
3 Tbs lemon juice
1 Tbs minced garlic
1 tsp paprika
salt/pepper
Roast:
1.5-2 lb Tri-tip steak/roast
salt/pepper
2 tablespoons soy sauce
6 cloves garlic, pre-roasted, crushed
Set Sous Vide Cooker to 129.2°F / 54°C
Generously season both sides with salt and pepper. Place the tri-tip in a zipper lock bag. Add the soy sauce. Crush the garlic gloves and add whole to the bag.
Add the zipper lock bag to the water bath using the water immersion method to remove all air. Sous vide for 2 hours.
Remove and dry tri-tip thoroughly. Rest for 10 minutes.
Heat a cast-iron skillet over or high heat.  Sear steak until golden brown, about 1 minute per side.
