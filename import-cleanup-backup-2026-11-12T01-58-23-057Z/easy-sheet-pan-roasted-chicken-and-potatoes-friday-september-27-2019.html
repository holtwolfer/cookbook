Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 4 bone-in chicken breasts
  • chimichurri rub
  • olive oil
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • small potatoes, cut into halves or quarters
  • green goddess spice blend
  • dried mustard
  • paprika
  • salt, pepper
  • olive oil
  • preheat oven to 425.

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. rub the chicken breasts with the chimichurri mix, marinate in a gallon bag with lemon juice and olive oil.
  2. Spread potatoes on parchment lined sheet pan. Season and toss to coat using the parchment paper.
  3. roast the potatoes for 10-15 minutes and then add the chicken breasts to the sheet pan, spreading the potatoes out to make room.
  4. roast for about 30 minutes, until the chicken is cooked.
