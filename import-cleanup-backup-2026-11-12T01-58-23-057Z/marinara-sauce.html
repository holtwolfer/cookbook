Marinara Sauce

OneNote Pasta section · Original page dated Tuesday, March 3, 2020

Tags

sauceLidiasummerfreezer saucetomato picktomatoestomato weekendpasta

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • salad
  • bread dip
  • meatballs

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 3/4 cup olive oil
  • ~1/4 cup garlic, minced
  • 14 lb tomatoes, seeded, rough chop
  • juice from tomatoes (put seeds in sieve over a measuring cup, we got about 1.5 cups)
  • pinch crushed red pepper flakes
  • salt
  • bay leaf
  • parm rind
  • 50 fresh basil leaves, chopped

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Heat oil over medium-high heat in a large stock pot.  Add garlic and crushed red pepper and cook until browned, about 2 mins.

Carefully add tomatoes and juice and season with salt.  Cover and cook over high heat stirring occasionally to bring up to a boil.  When tomatoes are covered with liquid and it keeps boiling when you stir it, remove cover, about 30 mins.  (middle picture)

Heat lowered to medium and cooked another 45 mins stirring often.

Basil added and sauce pureed with hand mixer.

Salt added to taste.

Meatier version:  Started with red bell pepper and garlic.  After blending added uncooked sausage, mushrooms and bigger pieces of bell pepper.  Note:  if using non-paste tomatoes it needs more time to cook down.
