Smash Burgers

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef
  • 4 burger buns
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • canola oil
  • salt/pepper
  • American, cheddar, smoked gouda, brick cheese
  • Toppings:
  • lettuce
  • tomato
  • pickles / pickle relish
  • onion
  • ---put down onions and jalapenos on griddle and add burger on top, smash---
  • Divide the meat into 6 'pucks' about 2.5 inches wide, 2 inches high.
  • Butter top and bottom of the buns and toast on griddle or under broiler.
  • Oil griddle or cast iron pan with a paper towel and heat over medium high.

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Season burgers with salt and pepper, place in pan seasoned side down.
  2. Using a solid spatula and meat tenderizer for extra weight, smash the burgers to about 4 inches wide, 1/2 inch thick.
  3. When they are nicely browned, flip, add onions (optional) and cheese slices
Original OneNote text (complete)
1.5 lbs ground beef
4 burger buns
2 Tbs butter
canola oil
salt/pepper
American, cheddar, smoked gouda, brick cheese
Toppings:
lettuce
tomato
pickles / pickle relish
onion
---put down onions and jalapenos on griddle and add burger on top, smash---
Divide the meat into 6 'pucks' about 2.5 inches wide, 2 inches high.
Butter top and bottom of the buns and toast on griddle or under broiler.
Oil griddle or cast iron pan with a paper towel and heat over medium high.
Season burgers with salt and pepper, place in pan seasoned side down.
Using a solid spatula and meat tenderizer for extra weight, smash the burgers to about 4 inches wide, 1/2 inch thick.
When they are nicely browned, flip, add onions (optional) and cheese slices
Served with:  fries; special sauce;

Special Sauce
1/2 cup mayo
3 Tbs sour cream
2 Tbs ketchup
1 Tbs chili sauce
1 Tbs mustard
1 Tbs pickle relish
pinch paprika
Combine all ingredients and whisk until smooth, combined.
Air Fryer Sweet Potato Fries
2 large sweet potatoes, cut into thin strips
2 Tbs canola oil
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp salt
(cayenne pepper)
Preheat air fryer to 400.
Toss fries with all ingredients to coat.  Fry at 400 for 10-12 minutes.
