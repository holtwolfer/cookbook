Fischbrötchen: German Fish Sandwiches

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Thursday, August 13, 2020

Tags

GermanSitkafishwhite fishcodbasssandwichtartar sauce

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

Fischbrötchen

  • 2 eggs (1 hard-boiled)
  • 4 cornichon pickles
  • 1/2 apple, not too sweet
  • 2 Tbs mayo
  • 2 Tbs sour cream
  • 1/2 cup whole milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 Tbs pickle juice
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/3 cup breadcrumbs (panko + pretzel)
  • 2 fillets Pacific cod
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons butter (ghee)
  • 2 brioche rolls (or pretzel rolls)
  • 2 large leaves butter lettuce

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. MAKE REMOULADE
  2. Boil one egg until hard (10-12 minutes) and peel while under cold water. Finely dice with cornichons. Remove core and finely dice the apple. Combine mayo, yogurt, pickle juice, lemon juice and season to taste with salt and pepper. Stir in parsley.
  3. COOK FISH
  4. Whisk the second egg with milk and a pinch of salt, transfer to a deep plate or shallow bowl. Pour flour and breadcrumbs onto two separate plates. Dust the fish in flour, then coat in egg mixture, and finally coat with breadcrumbs.
  5. Heat butter in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the fish for 4 to 5 minutes on each side. Transfer to a clean plate lined with paper towel.
  6. BUILD SANDWICHES
  7. Lightly toast brioche in the skillet or in a toaster. Build sandwiches with lettuce, fish, and remoulade. Serve the remaining remoulade sauce on the side.
