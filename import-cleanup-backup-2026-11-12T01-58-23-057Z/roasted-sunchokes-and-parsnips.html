Roasted Sunchokes and Parsnips

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Roasted Sunchokes & Parsnips
  • sunchokes, sliced lengthwise into 2-3 segments
  • parsnips, cut into chunks
  • butter
  • salt/pepper
  • fresh thyme
  • Preheat oven to 400

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Heat butter in a cast iron skillet. Saute sunchokes until lightly browned on both sides, add in parsnips and cook a few minutes more. Move skillet to oven and roast for 15-20 minutes. Return to stove and toss with a little more butter and fresh thyme. Sesaon with salt and pepper.
