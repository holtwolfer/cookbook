Simple Sauce, Drop Meatballs, Veggie Spaghetti

OneNote Pasta section · Original page dated Thursday, March 19, 2020

Tags

ItalianPasta

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • side salad

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Simple Sauce, Drop Meatballs, Veggies Spaghetti
  • Sauce
  • 3-4 large garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 4-6 anchovy filets, minced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Two 28-ounce cans tomatoes, 1 crushed, 1 whole
  • 1 large red bell pepper cut into chunks
  • 1 medium broccoli head, cut into small florets
  • Meatballs:
  • 1 sleeve square saltines (~15)
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 oz Parmesan cheese, grated (1/2 cup)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • salt/pepper
  • Sauce:
  • Have the tomatoes ready to go.

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a medium saucepan (blue dutch oven) over medium heat. Add the garlic, achovies, red pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until it begins to brown, about 2 minutes.

Add the crushed tomatoes and break up the whole tomatoes by hand as you add them. Slosh a little water around to rinse the cans and add to the sauce (~1/4 to 1/2 cup). Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer, 45 mins to 1 hour.  Add the meatballs. With about 30 minutes cooking time left add the red peppers and the broccoli.

Meatballs:

Crush the saltines. Combine saltines and milk in large bowl and let sit for 5 minutes for saltines to soften. Mash with fork until smooth paste forms. Mix in the garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt, pepper. Mix in the meat. Form into meatballs and refrigerate until ready to drop in the sauce.
