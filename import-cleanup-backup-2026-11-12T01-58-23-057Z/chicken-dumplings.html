Chicken & Dumplings

OneNote Chicken section · Original page dated Thursday, December 19, 2019

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 3 chicken breasts
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • prosciutto, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • rosemary
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 cups chicken stock ---always need to add a lot more stock before/while cooking dumplings
  • 2-3 carrots, chopped
  • 2-3 celery stalks, chopped
  • 1-2 leeks, chopped
  • (other root veggies)
  • kale
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2/3 cup flour
  • 1 cup white wine
  • frozen peas
  • Dumplings:
  • 3/4 cup buttermilk (~3/4 Tbs lemon juice, up to 3/4 cup with milk)
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1-1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 Tbs butter
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • chopped chives
  • pepper

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Simmer the chicken in stock for 30-45 minutes until it can be easily shredded. Remove and shred.
  2. Heat olive oil in dutch oven and cook onion and prosciutto until lightly browned. Add garlic and cook another minute. Add carrots and celery and other root veggies. Cook until lightly browned. Add the flour and stir for 1-2 minutes. Add the wine, cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the broth and season with salt and pepper. Add chicken back in and simmer, covered for 15 minutes. NOTE- tends to burn on the bottom of the pot if not stirred a lot.
  3. Whisk the buttermilk and egg together.
  4. In the food processor, combine the flour, baking powder and soda, and salt. Pulse a few times to mix. Scatter the butter in pieces evenly over the flour and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse meal and the largest chunks of butter are 1/4 inch. Transfer to a large mixing bowl and add the buttermilk, parsley, and chives, folding in until just combined.
  5. Drop ~1 Tbs sized dumplings into the stew, leaving a little space in between. Cover and cook until a cake tester comes out clean. Around 10-15 minutes over low heat. - be careful of sticking to the bottom of the pot.
