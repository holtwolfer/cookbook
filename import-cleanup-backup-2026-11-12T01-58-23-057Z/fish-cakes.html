Fish Cakes

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Thursday, May 28, 2020

Tags

white fishNYTblack basscod

Served with

  • salad w/ lemon vinaigrette
  • remoulade sauce
  • fries
  • baked potato

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 2 Tbs olive oil, more for frying
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • 1 lb black bass
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs white wine
  • 1 lb russet potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced (2 cups)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 Tbs roughly chopped cilantro
  • 3 Tbs roughly chopped basil
  • 1 scallion, white and green parts, finely chopped
  • 1 serrano chile, seeded and minced (used shishito)
  • Pinch cayenne (sub paprika)

Finely grated zest of 1 small lime (skipped)

  • ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 Tbs chopped lime pickle or preserved lemon (optional)
  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • Lime wedges, for serving
  • 1 egg + 1 yolk
  • 1 Tbs dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbs mayo
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 2 tsp old bay
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 2 pkgs halibut burger
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 3 Tbs Dijon- or Creole-style mustard
  • ¼ cup white-wine vinegar
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1-2 tsp cajun seasoning
  • 1 cup grapeseed oil
  • 1 Tbs prepared horseradish
  • 1 clove garlic, rough chop
  • 3-4 scallions, rough chop
  • 2 stalks celery, rough chop
  • 1/2 bunch of parsley, rough chop
  • 2 Tbs ketchup
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Lemon Vinaigrette
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 4 Tbs freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp sumac
  • 1/2 tsp green goddess spice blend

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. In a large skillet over medium heat, heat olive oil. Add garlic and cook until golden brown at the edges, about 2 minutes.
  2. Season fish with 1 teaspoon salt and the pepper, add to pan and let cook for 1 minute. Add white wine and 2 tablespoons water. Turn heat to low, cover and cook until fish is just barely cooked through, 8 to 10 minutes. Move fish to a plate, keeping any liquid and garlic in skillet.
  3. In the same skillet over high heat, add potatoes, remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt and water to just cover. Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer, cover and cook until tender, about 15 minutes.
  4. Flake fish.
  5. Drain potatoes and garlic, and place in a large bowl. Roughly mash potatoes and garlic, then add flaked fish, eggs, cilantro, basil, scallion, chile, cayenne, lime zest and panko, and combine. Season with salt if needed. Cover and chill for at least 3 hours.
  6. Place flour on a plate. Form generous 1/4 cup fish patties about 1/2 inch thick. Dip patties into flour to lightly coat each side.
  7. In a large, preferably nonstick skillet, heat 1/8 inch of olive oil over medium heat. Cook fish cakes until golden brown, about 5 to 8 minutes each side, adding more oil as needed. Move to a paper-towel-lined plate.
  8. If using lime pickle or preserved lemon, stir into mayonnaise. Serve fish cakes with the mayonnaise (with or without pickles) and lime wedges.

Baltimore-style Fish Cakes

  1. 1/2 bunch of parsley, finely chopped

dash of hot sauce

  1. 1 sleeve ritz crackers, crushed into crumbs
  2. ghee
  3. canola oil
  4. Mix egg, mayo, mustard, pepper, Old Bay, worceshershire, hot sauce, pepper, and parsley together until frothy.
  5. Add fish to the bowl and sprinkle on the cracker crumbs.
  6. Pour egg mixture over top.
  7. Gently toss to mix together.
  8. Form cakes.
  9. Heat small amount of ghee and oil in a skillet and sautee the cakes turning several times until golden brown, about 8 minutes total cooking time.
  10. If they brown too quickly lower the heat and cover with foil to make sure they cook through.

Rémoulade Sauce

  1. salt/pepper
  2. In small food processer, chop the garlic, scallions, celery, parsley. Add the egg yolk, mustard, vinegar, paprika, ketchup, worcestershire, salt/pepper and process until smooth. Slowly add the oil in a fine stream while mixing. Chill in refrigerator. It will thicken.
  3. salt/pepper
  4. 6 Tbs olive oil
  5. In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon zest, lemon juice, sugar, mustard, and fine sea salt, whisking until the sugar and salt are dissolved. Add 3 tablespoons of the oil in a slow stream, whisking constantly until the dressing is well blended. Season with fine sea salt and freshly ground black pepper. If desired, whisk in the remaining oil in a slow stream, whisking constantly. DO AHEAD: The vinaigrette can be prepared ahead and refrigerated, in an airtight container, up to 3 days.

Lemon Vinaigrette
1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
4 Tbs freshly squeezed lemon juice
2 tsp sugar
1 tsp Dijon mustard
1/2 tsp sumac
1/2 tsp green goddess spice blend
salt/pepper
6 Tbs olive oil
In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon zest, lemon juice, sugar, mustard, and fine sea salt, whisking until the sugar and salt are dissolved. Add 3 tablespoons of the oil in a slow stream, whisking constantly until the dressing is well blended. Season with fine sea salt and freshly ground black pepper. If desired, whisk in the remaining oil in a slow stream, whisking constantly. DO AHEAD: The vinaigrette can be prepared ahead and refrigerated, in an airtight container, up to 3 days.
