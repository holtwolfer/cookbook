Quick-Pickled Radishes

Tags

Served with

    Ingredients

    • 1-2 ruby heart radishes, matchsticked
    • 1/2 cup white wine vinegar
    • 1 Tbs sugar
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1 bay leaf
    • 1 lime, juiced
    • 1 lime
    • 1 bay leaf
    • 3 pieces of lime peel
    • 6 Tbs cider vinegar
    • 6 Tbs white vinegar
    • 2 Tbs water
    • 4-5 slices jalapeno
    • dash of crushed red pepper
    • 1 Tbs sugar

    Instructions

    1. Put radishes in a jar, bring remaining ingredients to a boil and pour over the radishes
