Quinoa

Ingredients

  • 1 cup quinoa
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 stalk celery, minced
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • 1 roasted red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 tsp lemongrass
  • 2 small tomatoes or cherry tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp aleppo pepper
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 1 tsp ras en hanout
  • Cook the quinoa

Instructions

  1. Sautee celery and onion in oil. Add red peppers. Then lemongrass and spices. Finish with tomatoes for last 2-3 minutes. Add a little more oil if needed. Stir into the quinoa.
