Glazed Carrots and Parsnips

Ingredients

  • Glazed Carrots and Parsnips
  • 2-3 large carrots, sliced or cut into chunks
  • 1-2 large parsnips, sliced or cut into chunks
  • 2-3 Tbs butter
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1 Tbs honey
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • rosemary or other fresh herb
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook carrots and parsnips in the butter in a non-stick skillet for a few minutes and then add the remaining ingredients. Simmer over medium heat until most of the liquid has evaporated, creating a glaze and the veggies are tender, about 10 minutes. Add more liquid if needed to finish cooking.
