Cherry Bourbon Glaze Beef Skewers

Tags

green egg

Served with

  • roasted potatoes
  • side salad
  • grilled veggies

Ingredients

  • Beef & Veggies
  • 1½ lbs New York strip steak, cut into 1-inch cubes (used a sirloin)
  • 15 oz cremini mushrooms
  • 1 large red onion, sliced in large pieces
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • ¼ cup beef rub (see recipe below)
  • Marinade
  • 1 cup dry red wine
  • ½ cup beef broth
  • ½ cup diced red onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 8 stems fresh thyme (didn't use)

Instructions

  1. In a 1-gallon resealable bag, combine all of the marinade ingredients.
  2. Cherry-Bourbon Glaze
  3. 1 Tbs butter
  4. ¼ cup finely diced red onion
  5. 1 clove garlic, minced
  6. 1 tsp paprika
  7. 1/2 cup ketchup
  8. 2 Tbs apple cider vinegar
  9. 2 Tbs bourbon
  10. ¼ cup cherry jam
  11. 1/2 Tbs molasses
  12. 1/2 Tbs dark brown sugar
  13. salt/pepper
  14. Heat the butter in a sauce pan and then add the onion. Sauté for 5 to 7 minutes until the onions are softened but not caramelized. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the remaining ingredients and bring to a simmer. Let simmer 10-15 minutes. Transfer to a measuring cup and using an immersion blender, blend the sauce. Allow to cool and then store in the refrigerator. (can be made in advance)
  15. Beef Rub Ingredients (skipped)
  16. ⅔ cup kosher salt
  17. 2 tsp paprika
  18. ⅔ cup coarse ground pepper
  19. 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  20. ⅔ cup granulated garlic
  21. In a small bowl, combine all of the ingredients. Store in an airtight container for up to 6 months.
  22. Makes 2 cups (480 ml) dry rub
  23. Grilling the beef:
  24. Grill over medium heat with lots of smoke, brushing the glaze on and rotating.
