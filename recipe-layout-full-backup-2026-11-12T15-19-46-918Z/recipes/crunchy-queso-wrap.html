Crunchy Queso Wrap

Tags

beef

Served with

    Ingredients

    • Crunchy Queso Wraps
    • For the Filling
    • 1.5 lb ground beef
    • ¼ cup yellow onion, chopped
    • taco seasoning
    • For Assembly
    • 1 can refried beans
    • ⅔ cup sour cream
    • 10 burrito-size (10-inch) flour tortillas
    • ¾ cup nacho cheese
    • 1 cup grated cheese - oaxaca, colby jack, jack, cojita
    • 1 cup thinly sliced romaine lettuce
    • ¾ cup pico de gallo, drained or taco sauce
    • 10 tostada shells
    • avocado oil

    Instructions

    1. Make taco meat. Make sure it is on the dry side.
    2. Prepare the assembly line:
    3. On a large flat surface, set out the flour tortillas.
    4. Spread thin layer of refried beans about the size of the tostada.
    5. Add ½ cup filling to the center of one tortilla
    6. Add layer of grated cheese
    7. Spread with 3 Tbs queso over the filling.
    8. Top the mixture with a tostada, pressing it slightly so the meat mixture is evenly distributed.
    9. Spread 2 scant tablespoons of the spicy sour cream on top of the tostada.
    10. Top evenly with a heaping ¼ cup shredded lettuce, then 3 Tbs drained pico de gallo.
    11. Enclose the filling by folding over one flap of the tortilla “border” to cover the filling, repeating the pleat every inch or two. The tortilla should fully enclose your filling, but an opening smaller than 1 inch at the center is just fine.
    12. Heat 1 Tbs oil in the skillet over medium, then carefully add the wrap, setting it seam side down. Cook until golden and crisp, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
