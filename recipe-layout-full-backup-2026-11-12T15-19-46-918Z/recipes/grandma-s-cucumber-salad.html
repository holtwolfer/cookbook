Grandma's Cucumber Salad

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Miracle Whip
  • 2 Tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • cucumber, thinly sliced
  • Peel, slice and salt the cucumbers.  Let set.  Drain.  Add dressing with pepper.
