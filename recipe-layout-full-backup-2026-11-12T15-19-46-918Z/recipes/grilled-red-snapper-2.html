Grilled Red Snapper

Ingredients

  • 4 red snapper filets
  • 1-2 lemons, juiced
  • salsa Lizano

Instructions

  1. Heat grill to medium.
  2. Brush the fish with olive oil.
  3. Add fish filets skin-side down
