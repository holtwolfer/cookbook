Ingredients

  • Salmon:
  • salmon steaks
  • salt/pepper

White sauce

  • greek yogurt

dijon mustard

  • olive oil
  • rice or white wine vinegar or lemon juice
  • white wine

dill

  • pepper
  • cure with lemon juice and then season with salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Season salmon steaks with salt & pepper, drizzle with olive oil.
  2. Grill over low heat with lots of alder smoke. Finish with higher heat to sear.
  3. 120-125 degrees
