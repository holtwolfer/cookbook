Fettuccine with Eggplant, Tomato, and Blue Cheese

Tags

pastaitalianvegetarian

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • simple salad with vinaigrette

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant, diced into 3/4-inch cubes
  • 4 Tbs olive oil, divided
  • Salt for cooking water
  • 1 lb fettuccine
  • 1/2 medium or large red onion, diced
  • 1-2 red bell peppers, diced (used an anaheim)
  • 1 large clove garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbs sherry vinegar or red wine vinegar
  • 2 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 3 large Roma tomato, diced
  • 8 oz (1 cup) tomato sauce
  • 1/2 jar tomato paste
  • 6-8 large white button mushrooms, diced (used most of a regular package)
  • crushed red pepper to taste
  • Handful fresh basil leaves, sliced into thin strips
  • 1 Tbs minced fresh flat-leaf parsley (used arugula)
  • 8 oz blue cheese (plus more for topping), cut or crumbled into small chunks
  • 2 to 3 Tbs extra-virgin olive oil
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Generously salt the water and add the fettuccine. Slightly under cook the pasta, 1-2 minutes less then the package directions suggest.

While the pasta is cooking, heat extra large carbon steel pan over medium-low heat and cook the onion in the remaining tablespoon of olive oil until golden. Add peppers and eggplant, cook until eggplant is softened and a little browned. Add the garlic, stir, and cook for 1 minute. Add the vinegar to deglaze. Add the tomato, tomato sauce, paste, mushrooms, crushed red pepper, and most of the basil (reserve a few strips for topping). Continue to cook over low heat to thicken/blend flavors.

Using a pasta grabber, transfer the pasta directly into the sauce and toss gently; reserve the pasta water. Add the parsley (or arugula), more basil, blue cheese, and the extra-virgin olive oil to the pasta and sauce, tossing again to combine. Add 2-4 tablespoons of the reserved pasta water and allow the dish to cook for 1-2 minutes; the cheese should melt down and mix with the liquid to create a luxurious sauce. Taste and adjust the seasonings as necessary with salt, pepper, and/or more crushed red pepper. Serve hot, with the remaining basil and additional blue cheese on top.
