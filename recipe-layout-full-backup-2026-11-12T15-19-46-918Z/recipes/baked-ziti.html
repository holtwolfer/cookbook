Baked Ziti

Tags

Italianpastafood lab

Served with

  • garlic bread

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ziti
  • Sauce:
  • butter
  • 1 jar tomato paste
  • 2 (28-oz) cans tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • dried oregano
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 12 oz ricotta cheese
  • 3 oz parmesan
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • spinach
  • eggplant
  • 3 Tbs parsley, minced
  • 3 Tbs basil, minced
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 lb fresh mozzarella pearls

Instructions

Make the sauce.

Preheat oven to 400 degrees.

Place pasta in a large bowl and cover by 3 or 4 inches with hot, salted water and let sit for 30 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes to avoid sticking. Drain.

Pour half of the sauce into a large bowl, add the ricotta, half the parm, the eggs, cream, and half the herbs and stir to combine. Add the pasta, along with half the mozzarella and stir. Transfer to a 9 x 13 baking dish and top with the remaining sauce and mozzarella.

Cover tightly and bake for 45 minutes. Remove foil and bake for another 15 minutes until cheese is lightly browned. Uncover and finish under broiler to melt/brown the mozzarella. Remove from oven, sprinkle with remaining parm and let cool for 10 minutes. Sprinkle with remaining herbs and serve.

Meatballs

1 sleeve saltines (20ish), crushed

salt & vinegar chips, crushed

1 cup milk

1 lb ground beef

1 lb ground pork or Italian sausage

1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated

1 tsp garlic powder

1 tsp dried oregano

1 tsp dried basil

1 Tbs soy sauce

1/2 tsp white pepper

1/2 tsp black pepper

Preheat oven to 400 degrees.

Combine saltines and milk in large bowl and let sit for 5 minutes for saltines to soften. Mash with fork until smooth paste forms.

Add Parmesan, garlic powder, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper to saltine mixture and mix. Add beef, pork, soy sauce and combine with hands. Divide meat mixture into 24 scant ¼-cup portions. Roll portions between your wet hands to form balls. Place meatballs on a sheet pan and then bake until cooked through and slightly browned.

About 35-40 minutes.
