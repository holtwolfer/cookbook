Cranberry Relish

Ingredients

  • A recipe from uncle Joe and aunt Julie
  • 2 cups cranberries
  • 1 small onion
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • 2 Tbs fresh horseradish
  • Grind cranberries and onion in food processor until finely chopped. Add remaining ingredients. Mix well and freeze.
