Farmer's Salad

Ingredients

  • Farmer's Salad
  • 1 pint cherry or grape tomatoes, halved
  • 1⁄2 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 1⁄2 cups olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 1⁄2 lb. fingerling or small waxy potatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
  • 4 slices whole grain bread, cut into 1" pieces
  • 1 small white onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup pickled beets from a jar, drained
  • 1 1⁄2 tbsp. whole grain mustard
  • 1 Tbs honey
  • 1 small shallot, roughly chopped
  • 8 oz. mixed greens
  • 1 cup pickled red cabbage from a jar, drained
  • 4 eggs hard-boiled, peeled, and quartered
  • 2 Tbs roughly chopped basil
  • 1 Tbs roughly chopped mint
  • 1 Tbs roughly chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Toss tomatoes, 1⁄4 cup vinegar, 2 Tbs olive oil, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Boil potatoes in salted water until tender, 6-8 minutes. Drain and thinly slice.
  2. Heat 1⁄3 cup oil and garlic in a 12" skillet over medium-high heat. Cook until soft, about 1 minute. Add bread, salt, and pepper; cook until crisp, 7-9 minutes. Transfer croutons to a plate.
  3. Wipe skillet clean and heat 1⁄4 cup oil over medium heat. Add onion; cook until slightly caramelized, 5-7 minutes. Add sliced potatoes, salt, and pepper; cook until potatoes are golden, 10-12 minutes.
  4. Puree remaining 1/4 cup vinegar with beets, mustard, honey, shallot, salt, pepper, and 3 tbsp. water in a blender until smooth. With the motor running, drizzle in 1/2 cup oil; puree until emulsified.
  5. To serve, spread mixed greens on a large platter. Arrange tomatoes, potatoes, croutons, cabbage, and eggs in alternating rows over greens. Drizzle about 1⁄4 cup dressing over the top and sprinkle with basil, mint, and parsley. Serve additional dressing on the side.
  6. Vinaigrette: sherry vinegar, olive oil, mustard, honey, salt, pepper, scapes/scallions/shallots/chives
