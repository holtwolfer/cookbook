Garlic Shrimp

Tags

surf & turfscampi

Served with

  • steak

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • 1-2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 bay leaf

pinch crushed red pepper

  • shrimp
  • salt/pepper
  • parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Season shrimp with paprika.
  2. Melt butter in carbon steel pan. Sautee garlic, bay leaf and crushed red pepper for 2 minutes, until fragrant. Cook shrimp, about 1 minute per side, until very lightly browned, seasoning with salt and pepper. Sprinkle on parsley, remove from heat, serve immediately.
