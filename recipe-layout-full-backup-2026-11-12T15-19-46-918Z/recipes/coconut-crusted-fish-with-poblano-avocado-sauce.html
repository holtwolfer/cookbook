Coconut-Crusted Fish with Poblano Avocado Sauce

Tags

mahi mahiwhitefishsalmon

Served with

  • veggie rice

Ingredients

  • Coconut Crusted Fish with Poblano Avocado Sauce
  • Sauce:
  • 1 poblano (check heat)
  • jalapeno (didn't use)
  • cilantro (~ half a bunch)
  • 1/4 cup coconut milk
  • 1 small clove garlic
  • 1 avocado
  • 1/2 lime juiced
  • 1 Tbs avocado oil
  • salt
  • Fish:
  • 1/4 cup coconut
  • 1/2 cup panko
  • 1 Tbs flour
  • cilantro
  • 2 avocados
  • 5-6 medium tomatillos
  • 2 large cloves garlic
  • 1 leek or scallions or onion
  • 2 avocados
  • cilantro
  • sugar
  • Coconut Milk Rice
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Combine ingredients in small food processor and blend until smooth.
  2. 2 Tbs butter, melted
  3. salt, pepper
  4. Preheat oven to 425.
  5. Combine the coconut, panko, flour, butter. Place fish on parchment lined sheet pan and season with salt and pepper. Bake for 5 mins and then remove from oven. Coat the top of each piece of fish and pat down. Return to the oven and bake until flaky.

alternate sauce

  1. garlic
  2. sour cream
  3. tomatillo salsa
  4. sour cream and salsa added in small amounts to get right flavor/consistency
  5. (see ninfas sauce recipe. could also use tomatillos and green tomatoes)

Tomatillo - Avocado Salsa

  1. cumin

coriander

  1. 1 Tbs lime juice
  2. salt
  3. roast the garlic and tomatillos on the grill until charred, let cool in food processor.
  4. Process with onions and then add avocado and remaining ingredients
  5. 1 bay leaf
  6. In a medium pot, bring coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt to a boil. Add rice, stir, and cover. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes. Add lime zest and fluff with a fork.

Notes

Additions: zest from 1 lime or lime juice or cilantro
