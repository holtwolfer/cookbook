Spring (Summer) Rolls

Tags

AsianThaiappetizer

Ingredients

  • Veggie Spring Rolls
  • spring roll wrappers
  • 1-2 carrots, julienned
  • 8 oz shitake mushrooms, thinly sliced
  • peanut oil
  • sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • 1/2 package cellophane rice noodles
  • microgreens/ sprouts
  • mint, chopped
  • scallions, thinly sliced
  • peanut sauce or sweet chili sauce for dipping

Instructions

  1. Put noodles in hot water and soak for 5 minutes, drain, rinse with cold water. Lay out on cutting board and cut into 3-4 inch lengths.
  2. Heat peanut oil in non-stick pan and sautee the carrots and mushrooms until just tender. Turn off heat and drizzle with a little sesame oil and soy sauce.
  3. Soak spring roll wrapper in hot water for 1 minute and then build the roll. Place some noodles at one end of wrapper, top with carrot mixture, greens, mint, scallions. Start rolling over the filling. Roll a couple of times and then fold in the sides and continue to roll up.
