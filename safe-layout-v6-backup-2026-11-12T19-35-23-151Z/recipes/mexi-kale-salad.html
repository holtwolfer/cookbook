Mexi-Kale Salad

Ingredients

  • Juice of 2 limes (about 1/4 cup)
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1-2 green onion, trimmed, sliced
  • salt/pepper
  • 16 oz. kale
  • 3/4 cup raw pumpkin seeds, toasted
  • 1 cup radishes, thinly sliced
  • 2/3 cup crumbled queso fresco

Instructions

  1. Puree lime juice, olive oil, honey, and onion in blender. Season with
  2. salt.
  3. Toss kale, pumpkin seeds, radishes, half the cheese, and vinaigrette in
  4. large bowl; season with salt and pepper. Garnish with remaining
  5. cheese.
