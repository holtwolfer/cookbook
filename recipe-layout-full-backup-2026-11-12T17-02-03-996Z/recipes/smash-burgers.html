Smash Burgers

Tags

burgerfood labair fryerbar food

Served with

  • fries
  • special sauce
  • burgers

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef
  • 4 burger buns
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • canola oil
  • salt/pepper
  • American, cheddar, smoked gouda, brick cheese
  • Toppings:
  • lettuce
  • tomato
  • pickles / pickle relish
  • onion
  • ---put down onions and jalapenos on griddle and add burger on top, smash---
  • Divide the meat into 6 'pucks' about 2.5 inches wide, 2 inches high.
  • Butter top and bottom of the buns and toast on griddle or under broiler.
  • Oil griddle or cast iron pan with a paper towel and heat over medium high.

Instructions

  1. Season burgers with salt and pepper, place in pan seasoned side down.
  2. Using a solid spatula and meat tenderizer for extra weight, smash the burgers to about 4 inches wide, 1/2 inch thick.
  3. When they are nicely browned, flip, add onions (optional) and cheese slices
