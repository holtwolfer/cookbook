Tomato Basil Chicken Breasts

Ingredients

  • 3 small boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • salt/pepper
  • ¼ cup flour
  • 3 Tbs butter, divided
  • ghee
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 2 Tbs diced shallots (about 1 medium shallot)
  • 12 oz assorted tomatoes, small ones halved and medium and large cut in wedges
  • 1 Tbs capers in brine, drained
  • ½ tsp red wine vinegar
  • balsamic vinegar
  • basil leaves (large leaves torn), for serving

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken breasts all over with salt and pepper. Add the flour to a shallow bowl or plate. Dredge the chicken by placing it in the flour, then turning to coat all sides evenly.
  2. Heat 2 Tbs of butter and the olive oil in a medium skillet over medium. When the mixture is bubbling, shake off any excess flour from the chicken and add to the skillet. Cook, undisturbed, until golden brown, about 4 minutes. Flip the pieces over and continue cooking until cooked through, about 4 more. Transfer the chicken to two serving plates.
  3. Add the shallots to the pan and cook, stirring, until softened and lightly caramelized, about 2 minutes. Stir in the tomatoes and ⅓ cup water (this helps create more sauce and helps the tomatoes cook more quickly). Simmer until the sauce is reduced by about half, about 3 minutes.
  4. Stir in the capers, the remaining 1 Tbs butter and the vinegar. Turn off the heat and season with salt.
  5. To serve, top the chicken with the basil leaves and the tomato sauce and season with black pepper.

Served with

  • crusty bread
  • zucchini/squash
  • orzo w/ bell pepper + spinach
  • rice
  • pasta
