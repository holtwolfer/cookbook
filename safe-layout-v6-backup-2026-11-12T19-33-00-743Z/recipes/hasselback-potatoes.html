Hasselback Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 4-5 medium potatoes
  • salt
  • 1 bunch of chives, minced
  • 4 Tbs butter

Instructions

  1. Cut the slits in the potatoes. Season with salt. Work chives into the slits. Top with 1 Tbs of butter and wrap up in foil. Gill over the coals until soft.
