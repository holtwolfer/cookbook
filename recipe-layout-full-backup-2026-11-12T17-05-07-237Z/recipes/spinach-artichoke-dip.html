Spinach Artichoke Dip

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 10-12 artichokes in seasoned oil, chopped
  • spinach, chopped
  • 1 cup parmesan, grated
  • 1/2 of a fresh mozzarella, broken into small pieces
  • 1/4 cup cream cheese
  • 1/4 cup mayo
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 tsp dry mustard
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp aleppo pepper
  • salt/pepper
  • dash of franks

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375.
  2. Heat butter in small cast iron skillet. Sautee shallots until soft, 2-3 minutes. Add artichoke chunks and then spinach and garlic and seasonings. Cook until spinach is wilted and there is not much liquid. Let cool a little.
  3. Mix together the cream cheese, mayo, sour cream. Stir this mixture and the cheeses into the skillet. Transfer to oven and cook about 30 minutes until lightly browned and bubbling.
