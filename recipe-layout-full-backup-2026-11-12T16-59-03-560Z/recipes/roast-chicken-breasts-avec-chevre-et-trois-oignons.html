Roast Chicken Breasts Avec Chèvre et Trois Oignons

Related recipes

  • Wild Rice

Ingredients

  • Roast Chicken Breasts with Chèvre et Trois Oignons
  • 2 Tbs olive oil 1 large leek, rinsed and thinly sliced crosswise (white and a little pale green part only) (3/4 cup) 1 large shallot, minced (1/4 cup) 2 cloves garlic, minced 1 Tbs fresh fines herbes, or 1 teaspoon dried fines herbes, crushed 4 oz creamy goat cheese (chèvre) 4 medium bone-in chicken breasts 2 Tbs olive oil

Instructions

  1. In a medium skillet, cook the leeks, shallots, and garlic in 2 tablespoons olive oil until tender but not brown. Add fines herbes; cook and stir about 30 seconds more. Cool slightly. In a bowl, combine leek mixture and goat cheese.
  2. Cut a pocket into each breast half by making a horizontal slice from one end of the breast to the other, cutting close toward the other side of the breast, but not entirely through. Spoon filling into pockets. Use wooden toothpicks to close the pockets. Season the breasts with salt and pepper; brush with the 2 tablespoons olive oil. Transfer to a flameproof, broiler-proof baking pan large enough to hold the breasts in one layer.
  3. Bake in a 350 oven for 35 to 40 minutes or until chicken is done (170°F). Remove pan from oven. Adjust oven rack to broiling position. Broil chicken breasts about 5 minutes or until skin is crisp and nicely brown.
