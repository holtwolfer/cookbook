Pork Chops with Plum Sauce

Tags

grillplumspork blade steakpork steakpork

Served with

  • salt potatoes
  • corn

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry red wine
  • 2 tsp grated peeled ginger
  • 1/4 cup instant flour (such as Wondra) or cornstarch
  • salt/pepper
  • 4 1/2-inch-thick pork chops or pork blade steaks
  • 1 orange
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 8 large sprigs fresh mint
  • 2 large plums, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • Peanut oil, for frying
  • For Frying:

Instructions

Whisk 1 tablespoon wine, the ginger and 2 tablespoons ice water in a large bowl. Whisk in the flour and 1 teaspoon salt. Add the pork, turning to coat; refrigerate while you prepare the sauce.

NOTES… use a mix of wine and pomegranate juice, made sauce ahead without butter, left on stove, reheated at dinner time and added butter then. Put ginger into sauce and also rubbed pork steaks with some ginger, salt and pepper and grilled over charcoal . Used more plums. Added fig sweetener in place of some of the honey. dash of balsamic could be good. did not need corn starch for sauce.

Remove the zest from the orange in wide strips using a vegetable peeler. Put the zest in a sauce pan and squeeze in the juice. Whisk in the vinegar, honey and the remaining 3/4 cup plus 3 tablespoons wine. Add the mint and cook over medium-high heat until syrupy, 10 to 15 minutes. Add the plums and cook until slightly soft, about 4 minutes. Remove from the heat and discard the mint. Swirl in the butter, if desired, and season with salt.

Heat 1/4 inch peanut oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the pork until golden and crisp, 4 to 5 minutes per side. Transfer to a paper towellined plate to drain. Season with salt and serve with the plum sauce
