Meatballs

Tags

pasta

Served with

    Ingredients

    • 1 lb ground pork
    • 1 lb ground beef
    • salt
    • 1 cup buttermilk (1 Tbs lemon juice, bring up to 1 cup with 2% milk)
    • 1/4 oz packet of unflavored gelatin
    • 4 slices of white bread, crust removed
    • 2 tsp soy sauce
    • 2-4 anchovies, mashed to a paste
    • 2 eggs
    • 3 oz (about 1.5 cups) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
    • 3 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
    • 1 cup canola oil for frying

    Instructions

    Blend the pork, beef, and salt together in a large with your hands and let rest in the fridge.

    Pour the butter milk into a bowl and sprinkle the gelatin over it. Allow 10 mins to hydrate. Add the bread and soak for 10 minutes, until fully absorbed.
