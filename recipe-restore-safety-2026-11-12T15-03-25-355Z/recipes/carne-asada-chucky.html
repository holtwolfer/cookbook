Carne Asada - Chucky

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chuck roast, sliced into 1/2-inch steaks
  • oil or fat for greasing the grill
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • For the tacos:
  • flour tortillas
  • refried beans
  • salsa
  • guacamole
  • queso fresco
  • onion / cilantro / tomato

Instructions

  1. Slice beef against the grain.
  2. Clean and oil grill grate, heat to medium-high - high.
  3. Salt meat on top side just before putting on the grill. Grill until drops of moisture form on top side and then flip and grill for less time on other side. Remove from grill, cover and let rest 5 minutes before chopping. Chop and serve quickly.

Served with

  • rice
  • corn on the cob
