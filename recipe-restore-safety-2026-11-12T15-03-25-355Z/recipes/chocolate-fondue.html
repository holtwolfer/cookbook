Chocolate Fondue

Notes and variations

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 lb premium semisweet chocolate (recommended: Lindt or Callebaut)
  • 1 Tbs pure vanilla extract
  • 1 Tbs brandy
  • Strawberries, bananas, blueberries, angel food cake, apples, pineapple, brownies, marshmallows, cookies or any other dipping item of your choice

Instructions

  1. Heat cream in fondue pot and then add in chocolate and vanilla and whisk until melted.
