Baked Potatoes

Ingredients

  • baking potatoes
  • Toppings:
  • salt / spike
  • butter
  • sour cream
  • cheese
  • scallions / chives
  • broccoli / cauliflower

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450. Pierce the skin of the potatoes several times with a fork. Bake for 45 mins to 1 hr depending on size.
