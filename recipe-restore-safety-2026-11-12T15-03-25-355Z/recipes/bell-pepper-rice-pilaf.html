Bell Pepper Rice Pilaf

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups basmati rice
  • 2 Tbs oil or butter
  • 1 large red onion, chopped
  • 1 large red bell pepper, seeded, chopped
  • 1 large yellow bell pepper, seeded, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp aleppo pepper
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 Tbs brown sugar
  • 1 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1 Tbs lime juice
  • Cook the rice.

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a sautee pan over medium heat and cook onion for 2-3 minutes, add the bell pepper, cook another 5 minutes or so until mostly cooked but still has some crunch, add the cumin, aleppo pepper, salt/pepper, brown sugar, balsamic vinegar and soy sauce. Cook for a few minutes and lower the heat to low for a few more to let it thicken a bit. Mix into the rice. Add lime juice.
  2. Bell Pepper Rice Pilaf
  3. 6 Tbs (3/4 stick) butter
  4. 1 large red onion, chopped
  5. 1 large red bell pepper, seeded, chopped
  6. 1 large yellow bell pepper, seeded, chopped
  7. 2 1/3 cups long-grain white rice
  8. 2 tsp ground cumin
  9. 1 tsp chili powder
  10. 3 3/4 cups chicken broth
  11. 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  12. Melt butter in heavy large pot over medium heat. Add onion and bell peppers and sauté until tender, about 12 minutes. Add rice, cumin and chili powder; stir 1 minute. Add broth and bring to boil, stirring occasionally. Cover pot. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until rice si tender and broth is absorbed, about 25 minutes. Season pilaf to taste with salt and pepper. Sprinkle with cilantro and serve.
