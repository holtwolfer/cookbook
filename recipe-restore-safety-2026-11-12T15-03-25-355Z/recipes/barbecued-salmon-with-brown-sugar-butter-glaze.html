Barbecued Salmon with Brown Sugar Butter Glaze

Ingredients

  • 1 center-cut salmon fillet (2 pounds), preferably skin-on
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ¼ cup mirin
  • ¼ cup sake
  • 3 Tbs sesame oil
  • 2 Tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 scallions, trimmed and thinly sliced (set aside 3 tablespoons scallion greens for garnish)
  • 1 Tbs minced fresh peeled ginger
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ½ stick (4 Tbs) unsalted butter
  • ¼ cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 Tbs sriracha
  • ½ tsp freshly grated lemon zest
  • 1 Tbs fresh lemon juice, or to taste
  • 1 Tbs cornstarch
  • 3 tablespoons tamari or soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling

Instructions

  1. For the Marinade:
  2. Sauce:

Reserved marinade from above

  1. 2 Tbs hot water
  2. pepper
  3. Soak the cedar plank in water for at least 1 hour, preferably longer.
  4. Make the marinade: Place the soy sauce, mirin, sake, sesame oil, Worcestershire sauce, scallions, ginger, and garlic in a mixing bowl and whisk to mix. Pour the marinade over the salmon in a ziploc bag. Let the salmon marinate in the refrigerator for 2 to 4 hours.
  5. Pull the salmon out, reserving the marinade.
  6. Salmon:
  7. Set up your grill for indirect grilling and heat to medium-high (400 degrees).
  8. Arrange the salmon fillet on the plank and place the plank in the center of the grill away from the heat. Add wood chips or chunks to the fire. Indirect grill the salmon until browned and cooked through, 20 to 30 minutes basting 2-3 times with the sauce towards the end of cooking. Cook the salmon to about 125 degrees.
  9. Meanwhile make the sauce: Pour the marinade into a saucepan. Boil until reduced by half. Whisk in the butter, sugar, lemon zest and juice, and black pepper. Thicken with about 1 Tbs of cornstarch whisked into about 2 Tbs hot water.
  10. Serve the salmon on the plank. Brush one final time with the barbecue sauce and sprinkle with scallion greens. Serve the remaining barbecue sauce on the side.

Grilled Broccoli

  1. 1 head broccoli, approximately 2 pounds, cored and cut into 1-inch florets

Kosher salt, to taste

  1. 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh parsley
  2. Flaky sea salt (optional)
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together the tamari or soy sauce with the vinegar. Add the olive oil while whisking vigorously. Add the broccoli and toss to coat. Sprinkle lightly with kosher salt.
  4. Place a grill basket on the grill and add the broccoli to it. Grill, tossing frequently, until the florets are crisp at the edges and tender within, with just a little bit of bite to them, approximately 10 to 12 minutes. If you don’t have a grill basket, lay the florets out on the grill in a single level and use tongs to turn them often. More work, same result.
  5. Transfer the cooked broccoli to a platter, drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with parsley and, if using, a pinch or two of flaky sea salt.

Served with

  • Israeli couscous with red onion/carrots/bok choi
  • kale salad
  • roasted potatoes
  • hasselback potatoes
