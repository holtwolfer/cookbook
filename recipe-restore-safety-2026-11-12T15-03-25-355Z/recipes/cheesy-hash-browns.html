Cheesy Hash Browns

Ingredients

  • Cheesy Hash Browns Kinda Like Dunlays
  • 2 lbs potatoes, shredded
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • 1 onion, shredded
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup colby jack
  • 1/2 cup cheddar
  • 1/4 cup parmesan
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • salt/pepper
  • spike
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Preheat oven to 450

Instructions

  1. Shred the potato and onion onto a cloth napkin, and squeeze out excess water.
  2. Mix sour cream, cheese, spices together. Blend into the potato and onion mixture.
  3. Melt butter in cast iron skillet and then add the potato mixture. Pour in heavy cream and mix in.
  4. Bake for 35-45 minutes.

  24. PARMESAN POTATOES
  25. 2#                                Frozen shredded hash browns-thawed completely
  26. 1 Med                          Onions sliced
  27. 2 tsp                            Salt
  28. ½ tsp                           Pepper
  29. 1½ cups                       1/2 & 1/2
  30. ¼ #                              Butter
  31. ¼ #                              Parmesan cheese, shredded
  32. Paprika
  33. Directions:
  34. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
  35. Spray a 9x13 cake pan with food release
  36. Saute onions over medium heat in the butter in heavy pot.
  37. Add 1/2 & 1/2 and warm.
  38. Add potatoes and cook, stirring until the cream is absorbed and thickens slightly.
  39. Add half the parmesan cheese and stir till it melts
  40. Spread into a 9x11 cake pan. Top with remainder of cheese
  41. Sprinkle lightly with paprika. Cover with foil.
  42. Bake 30 minutes. Uncover and run under broiler to slightly brown.
