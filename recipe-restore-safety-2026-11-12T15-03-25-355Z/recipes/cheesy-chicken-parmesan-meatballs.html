Cheesy Chicken Parmesan Meatballs

Ingredients

  • This one-dish dinner takes inspiration from chicken Parmesan, but eliminates the fuss of breading and frying chicken cutlets. Instead, chicken meatballs are the star, delivering an equally comforting and satisfying meal. The unexpected secret to these tender meatballs is tofu, which keeps them juicy. Simply press pieces of tofu between your fingertips to create small crumbles that resemble ground meat. The addition of ricotta creates a creamy texture, as well as great flavor. The meatballs are simmered in marinara sauce with red bell peppers, which infuse the sauce with fresh flavor and natural sweetness. Sharp and tangy provolone completes the dish, although mozzarella could be used for milder flavor. Enjoy over buttered egg noodles, or with crusty bread to sop up the sauce.
  • ¾ cup crumbled firm tofu (about 4 ounces)
  • ½ cup ricotta (about 4 ounces)
  • ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan (about 1 1/2 ounces)
  • ¼ cup plain bread crumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 Tbs minced garlic
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 lb ground chicken (or turkey)
  • 2 Tbs extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 medium red bell peppers, cored and sliced lengthwise 1/2-inch-thick
  • 3 cups marinara sauce (about 24 ounces)
  • 6 oz sliced provolone cheese, and/or fresh mozzarella
  • cooked egg noodles or other pasta, for serving
  • chopped parsley, for garnish (optional)
  • Heat oven to 375 degrees.

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine tofu, ricotta, Parmesan, bread crumbs, egg, garlic, oregano, salt/pepper, and mix until well combined. Add chicken and gently mix to incorporate. Lightly wet hands to prevent meatball mixture from sticking, then roll into 24 (1 1/2-inch) golf ball-size rounds.
  2. In a Dutch oven, heat oil over medium. Add bell peppers, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5 minutes. Add marinara sauce and meatballs, and stir gently to coat the meatballs in the sauce.
  3. Bring to a boil, cover and transfer to the oven to bake until meatballs are cooked through, 20 minutes.
  4. Top with provolone slices, slightly overlapping, and bake until cheese melts, about 3 minutes longer.
  5. Serve meatballs, peppers and sauce over egg noodles. Garnish with parsley.
  6. 2 (28-oz) cans of San Marzano whole peeled tomatoes
  7. 1 onion, peeled and cut in half
  8. Combine the tomatoes, their juices, the butter and the onion halves in a saucepan. Add a pinch or two of salt.
  9. Place over medium heat and bring to a simmer. Cook, uncovered, for about 45 minutes. Stir occasionally, mashing any large pieces of tomato with a spoon. Add salt as needed.
  10. Discard the onion before tossing the sauce with pasta. This recipe makes enough sauce for a pound of pasta.

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • pasta
