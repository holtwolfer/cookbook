Collards

Ingredients

  • 1-2 Tbs butter
  • 1/2 medium red onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 smoked ham hock or smoked turkey
  • 2-3 small garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 2 pts chicken broth
  • 1-2 large bunches fresh collard greens, washed, trimmed, and cut into ~1 inch squares
  • 3 Tbs apple cider vinegar
  • 1 Tbs sugar
  • 1 Tsp paprika
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Melt butter over medium heat and add onion and ham to stockpot, and sauté until tender, about 8 minutes. Add garlic and sauté 1 minute. Stir in broth, collard greens, apple cider vinegar, sugar, paprika, salt/pepper. Cook over low heat 2 hours or until desired degree of tenderness.
