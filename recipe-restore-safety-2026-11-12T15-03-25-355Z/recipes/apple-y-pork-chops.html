Apple-y Pork Chops

Ingredients

  • 3 pork chops
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • salt/pepper
  • rosemary and/or sage
  • apple cider
  • cider vinegar
  • calvados

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350.

Season pork chops with salt/pepper, rosemary and sage.

Heat cast iron pan over medium heat. Melt butter and throw in garlic slices and then sear both sides of the chops, about 4 minutes per side. Lower the heat and add apple cider, cider vinegar, calvados. Move the pan to the oven to finish cooking. Cook chops to around 140.

Finish on the stove-top spooning the pan sauce over the chops.

Served with

  • mashed potatoes
  • caramelized onions
  • fried apples
  • green beans
  • brussel sprouts
