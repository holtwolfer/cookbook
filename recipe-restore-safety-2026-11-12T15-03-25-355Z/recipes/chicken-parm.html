Chicken Parm

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, butterflied and pounded to 1/8-inch thick
  • 150 grams (1 cup) plain breadcrumbs
  • 25 grams salt and vinegar potato chips, not kettle cooked or thick cut
  • 3 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried parsley
  • 1.5 tsp garlic powder
  • grated parmesan
  • 2/3 cup flour
  • 3 large eggs, beaten
  • 4 Tbs unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 Tbs olive oil, divided
  • 1 cup red sauce, divided, recipe follows
  • sliced fresh mozzarella
  • Italian fontina, shredded
  • Chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley OR basil, for garnishing
  • Red Sauce
  • 3-4 large garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 4-6 anchovy filets, minced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Two 28-ounce cans tomatoes, 1 crushed, 1 whole
  • One 2-inch Parmesan rind

Instructions

  1. Combine the breadcrumbs, potato chips, oregano, parsley and garlic powder in a gallon zip-top bag and crush into a course meal.
  2. Set up breading station with a piece of parchment for the uncooked and one for cooked chicken.
  3. Unwrap the chicken and blot well with paper towels then dredge the chicken first in the flour (shake off the excess), then dip in the beaten eggs (again, drain the excess), and finally coat with the breadcrumbs before placing on a piece of parchment paper.
  4. Position an oven rack in the top third of the oven and crank the broiler to high.
  5. Melt 2 Tbs of the butter with 1 Tbs of the olive oil in a sauté pan over medium-high heat. When the butter foams, add the breaded chicken to the pan, but don’t overcrowd. Cook until crisp and golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer to a second piece of parchment, wipe out the pan and repeat with the remaining butter, oil and chicken.
  6. Spread 3/4 cup of the red sauce into the bottom of a a 9-by-13-inch broiler-safe baking dish or pan and broil until the sauce bubbles and begins to caramelize, about 2 minutes.
  7. Remove the dish from the oven and arrange the chicken pieces in a single layer. Dot the chicken with the remaining red sauce, leaving bare spots to retain some crispy bits. Scatter the fontina and mozzarella over the sauce.
  8. Return to the broiler until the cheese is bubbly and beginning to brown, about 5 minutes in our broiler but check after 2.
  9. Garnish with chopped flat-leaf parsley or basil.
  10. Have the tomatoes ready to go.
  11. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a medium saucepan (blue dutch oven) over medium heat. Add the garlic, anchovies, red pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until it begins to brown, about 2 minutes.
  12. Add the crushed tomatoes and break up the whole tomatoes by hand as you add them. Then submerge the Parmesan rind in the sauce. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer, until the sauce is almost dry, about 45 mins to 1 hour.
  13. Discard the Parmesan rind or feed it to the dog. Use the sauce immediately or store in an airtight container in the fridge.

  23. Cream Citrus Butter Sauce
  24. 1 small shallot, minced
  25. 1/2 cup butter (1 stick), cut into pieces
  26. 2 Tbs whipping cream
  27. juice of 1 small (mandarin) orange
  28. Heat wine and shallot in a saucepan. Bring to boil and reduce over medium-high heat to 1 Tbs.
  29. Reduce heat to low and whisk in butter, a few pieces at a time, until sauce is smooth and all butter is incorporated. Whisk in cream and lemon/orange juice. Serve immediately or keep warm with a double boiler over hot water.
  30. Cut the butterflied and pounded breasts into 6 to 8 even pieces. Season with the salt on both sides and place on a rack set inside a sheet pan (technically we use "half" sheet pans for this). Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  31. Combine the breadcrumbs, potato chips, oregano, parsley and garlic powder in a gallon zip-top bag and crush into a course meal.
  32. Set up a breading station with three loaf pans: place the flour in the first pan, the beaten eggs in the second, and the breadcrumb mixture in the third. Set a clean wire rack inside a sheet pan to hold the breaded pieces.
  33. Unwrap the chicken and blot well with paper towels then dredge the chicken first in the flour (shake off the excess), then dip in the beaten eggs (again, drain the excess), and finally coat with the breadcrumbs before placing on the rack.
  34. Position an oven rack in the top third of the oven and crank the broiler to high. Line a clean pan with paper towels and have it standing by.
  35. Melt 2 tablespoons of the butter with 1 tablespoon of the olive oil in an 11-inch sauté pan over medium heat. When the butter foams, add half the breaded chicken to the pan, but don’t overcrowd. Cook, undisturbed, until crisp and golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer to the clean rack, wipe out the pan and repeat with the remaining butter, oil and chicken.
  36. Spread 3/4 cup of the red sauce into the bottom of a a 9-by-13-inch broiler-safe baking dish or pan and broil until the sauce bubbles and begins to caramelize, about 2 minutes.
  37. Remove the dish from the oven and arrange the chicken pieces in a single layer. Dot the chicken with the remaining red sauce, leaving bare spots to retain some crispy bits. Scatter the fontina and mozzarella over the sauce.
  38. Return to the broiler until the cheese is bubbly and beginning to brown, about 1 minute to 1 minute and 30 seconds.
  39. Garnish with chopped flat-leaf parsley.
