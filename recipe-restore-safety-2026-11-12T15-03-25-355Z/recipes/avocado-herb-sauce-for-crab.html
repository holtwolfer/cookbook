Avocado Herb Sauce for Crab

Notes and variations

Ingredients

  • Avocado Herb Sauce for Crab
  • 1 avocado
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbs mayo
  • 1 Tbs lime juice
  • 1/2 tsp champagne vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp hot sauce

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a food processor and blend to a smooth puree.
