Brussel Sprout Hash

Ingredients

  • 6 Tbsp butter
  • 1/2 lb shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbs apple cider vinegar
  • 4 tsp sugar
  • 1 1/2 lbs brussels sprouts
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup water

Instructions

Melt 3 Tbsp butter over medium heat.  Add shallots, sprinkle with coarse kosher salt and pepper.  Sautee until soft and golden, about 10 min.  Add vinegar and sugar.  Stir until brown and glazed, about 3 min.

Halve brussel sprouts, lengthwise.  Cut lengthwise into 1/8 inch slices.  Heat oil over medium-high heat.  Add sprouts.  Season with salt and pepper.  Sautee until brown at edges, about 6 min.  Add water and 3 Tbsp butter.  Sautee until most of the water evaporates and sprouts are tender but still bright green, about 3 minutes.  Add shallots.
