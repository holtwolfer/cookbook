Braised Beets

Ingredients

  • beets, cubed
  • butter
  • wine - red or white
  • salt/pepper
  • balsamic vinegar
  • balsamic glaze
  • stock
  • tarragon or other fresh herbs
  • blue cheese

Instructions

  1. melt butter in a large sauce pan. Add beets, wine, salt/pepper, balsamic vinegar, glaze, and stock to cover. Cover and simmer for about 20 minutes until the beets are tender, adding fresh herbs at the end of cooking.
  2. Serve with crumbled blue cheese.
