Chili Oil Sauce (Chile de Árbol in Olive Oil)

Recipe from Noemy

Remove the stems from the dried chile de árbol peppers.

Grind the chiles

Place the chiles into a grinder (a coffee grinder works very well) and grind them until you obtain chili flakes or a coarse powder, depending on your preference.

Combine with olive oil

Transfer the ground chiles into a pot.

Cover completely with olive oil.

Ingredients

  • A general guideline is to use approximately double the volume of olive oil compared to the chiles.

Instructions

  1. For example:
  2. For one box of chiles, you may add about 250 ml to 500 ml (¼ to ½ liter) of olive oil.
  3. Heat the mixture
  4. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil and cook for approximately 10–15 minutes, stirring constantly.
  5. Do not be afraid of heating the olive oil — only small quantities will be used later, and this step helps infuse the flavor.
  6. Important precaution
  7. Make sure absolutely no water enters the mixture.
  8. Water will reduce the preservation time and can spoil the sauce.
  9. Handling after cooking
  10. The mixture will be extremely hot. Handle carefully to avoid burns.
  11. Storage
  12. Pour the hot chili oil into clean containers with lids.
  13. Over time:
  14. The chili flakes will settle at the bottom.
  15. The infused oil will remain on top.
  16. Adjusting strength
  17. Depending on how strong the chiles are, you may:
  18. Add more olive oil to reduce intensity, or
  19. Leave it as is for a stronger flavor.
