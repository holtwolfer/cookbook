Salmon BLTs

Tags

gertrude'sbawlmersandwichsauce

Served with

  • fries
  • potato wedges
  • smashed potatoes
  • pesto aioli
  • green sauce

Ingredients

  • Salmon BLTs a la Gertie

salmon, cut into bun-sized portions

  • salt/pepper
  • flour
  • 2 eggs

parmesan cheese, grated

  • herbs, finely chopped

bacon

  • olive oil
  • tomato
  • lettuce
  • pesto aioli
  • pretzel rolls
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves (stems removed)
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 Tbs minced garlic (3 cloves)

Instructions

  1. Cook bacon. Preheat oven to 350.
  2. Whisk eggs, parmesan cheese, and herbs together. Season salmon with salt/pepper. Heat olive oil in cast iron skillet. Dredge the salmon in the flour and then coat in the egg mixture and transfer to hot skillet. Cook on both sides until lightly browned. Transfer to oven to finish cooking??

Pesto Aioli

  1. 1 Tbs lemon juice
  2. pinch salt
  3. Put of all the ingredients into a blender or food processor.
  4. Pulse for 3 minutes, occasionally scraping the sides down, until smooth.
  5. Transfer to an air tight container or covered bowl and refrigerate until needed.
