Sheet-pan Balsamic Salmon

Ingredients

(just used the glaze)

  • 4 (5 ounce) Keta fillets
  • 1½ lb cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed and cut into 1 - 1½ inch pieces
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1 Tbs honey
  • 1 Tbs dijon mustard
  • 1 - 2 Tbs chopped parsley
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425° F.
  2. ROAST VEGGIES
  3. On a parchment paper lined sheet pan, toss tomatoes, green beans, garlic, 3 tbsp oil, and salt & pepper to taste. Roast for 15 minutes.
  4. MAKE GLAZE
  5. Whisk together 1 tablespoon oil, balsamic vinegar, honey and mustard in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 7-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until the mixture thickens.
  6. PREP SALMON
  7. Drizzle 1 tbsp oil on the salmon fillets, sprinkle with salt & pepper, and lightly brush on the glaze.
  8. ROAST SALMON
  9. Once the vegetables have roasted for 15 minutes, push them to the side and place the salmon fillets on the sheet pan. Return to the oven and roast for and additional 8-10 minutes.
  10. SERVE
  11. Transfer the salmon and vegetables to serving dishes or plates, drizzle with reserved balsamic glaze and season with salt & pepper if desired. Top with chopped parsley.
  12. Sarah’s Pro-Tip: The sheet pan needs to be large enough for everything to fit in a single layer. If the vegetables get too crowded, divide this recipe onto two pans.
  13. Beverage Pairing: Serve this sheet pan balsamic salmon with a chilled glass of chardonnay.
