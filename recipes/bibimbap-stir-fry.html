Bibimbap Stir-Fry

Ingredients

  • Chicken Marinade:
  • 1 chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1 apple (or pear), grated, with juice
  • 1/2 white onion, chopped
  • 1 Tbs ginger
  • 2 Tbs brown sugar
  • 1 Tbs honey
  • 2 Tbs sesame seeds
  • 2 Tbs sesame oil
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 2 scallions, chopped
  • 1 bottle sprite or ginger ale!
  • peanut oil
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 bunch swiss chard, stalks and leaves separated, chopped
  • 1 kohlrabi, match-sticked
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1 Tbs mirin
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 portabella mushrooms, large chop
  • 3-4 scallions, chopped
  • pepper
  • eggs
  • gochujang
  • 2 small cucumbers
  • 2 radishes
  • 2 cups rice
  • 2 cups stock
  • 2 cups water

Instructions

  1. Slice chicken in thin pieces, mix marinade ingredients and marinate 1 hour to overnight.
  2. Cook the chicken, add veggies, then soy/mirin, etc.
  3. Serve over rice, topped with eggs and gochujang or ssam sauce.
  4. boil, heat to low 7 mins not cooked through
  5. thin out with 3 tbs water, add 2Tbs sesame oil, 1 tsp sugar, whisk
  6. garlic scallion sauce
  7. 1/2 cup water, add 3 minced scallions, 3 tbs soy sauce, 3 garlic cloves, minced, 1 Tbs sugar, mix together
  8. dutch oven, oil, high heat, 3 shredded carrots, 1/3 of sauce, 1-2 mins, reduce liquid/softened, remove
  9. oil, high heat, shimmer, add mushrooms, coat wth oil, add 1/3 of sauce, cook until dry and tender, remove
  10. oil, high heat, 10 oz spinach, chopped, rest of sauce in, 1-2 mins, remove
  11. 10inch nonstick, 2 tsp veg oil, heat low heat 5 mins, add 4 large eggs from bowl, cover, 2 min
  12. 2 Tbs veg oil, 1 Tbs semsame oil dutch oven high heat, add rice, pack down into bottom, 2 mins cook, heat to low, add veggies on top, slide eggs on top.
  13. mix everything but bottom crust, serve with a little of the crusty rice too. topped with pickled stuff.
