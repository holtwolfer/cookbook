Lobster Stock

Ingredients

  • 3 (1 1/2 pound) cooked lobsters (shells)
  • 3 corn cobs
  • 6 Tbs butter
  • 1 cup yellow onion, chopped
  • 1/4 cup sherry
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 4 cups milk
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup white wine

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot and sautee onions until translucent. Add sherry and paprika and cook 1 minute. Add milk, cream, wine, lobster shells and any lobster juices and corn cobs and bring to a simmer. Partially cover the pot and stimmer over very low heat for 30 minutes.

Notes and variations

Source title is Lobster Stock; ingredients include milk and cream.
