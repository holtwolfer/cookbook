Quick BBQ Sauce

Ingredients

  • 4-6 Tbs brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup cider vinegar
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1 Tbs molasses
  • 1 Tbs sorghum
  • dry mustard
  • paprika
  • garlic powder
  • onion powder
  • sumac
  • bay leaves
  • 2-4 cups ketchup + chili sauce

Instructions

  1. Whisk all ingredients together and simmer in a sauce pan to thicken
  2. Additions: 1/4 cup apple cider, honey, worcestershire, bourbon, onions, peppers, celery, mustard, ancho chile powder, cloves, allspice
