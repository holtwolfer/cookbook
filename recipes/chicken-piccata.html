Chicken Piccata

Ingredients

  • 3 chicken breasts
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 Tbs paprika
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 large egg
  • 3/4 cup bread crumbs
  • olive oil
  • 3 Tbs butter
  • 1/3 cup fresh lemon juice (2 lemons, reserve halves)
  • 1/2 cup white wine
  • fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees and line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
  2. Place chicken breasts in a plastic bag and pound out to around 1/4 inch thickness, then season with salt and pepper.
  3. Mix the flour, paprika, salt and pepper on a plate. Beat egg together in a bowl and place breadcrumbs on a second plate. Dip each chicken breast in the flour, then egg and then bread crumbs.
  4. Heat 1 Tbs olive oil in a deep saute pan (carbon steel) over medium heat and cook the chicken breasts until browned, about 2 minutes a side. Place on the sheet pan and bake 5 to 10 minutes.
  5. Wipe out the saute pan with a paper towel and melt 1 Tbs butter over medium heat. Then add the lemon juice, wine and the reserved lemon halves, along with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp pepper. Boil over high heat until reduced by half. Remove from heat and add 2 Tbs butter and mix. Spoon sauce over the chicken breasts to serve.
  6. Serve with: mashed potatoes, corn;
