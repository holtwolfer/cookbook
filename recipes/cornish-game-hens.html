Cornish Game Hens

OneNote Chicken section · Original page dated Saturday, January 9, 2021

Tags

mini-chickens

Served with

  • roasted potatoes
  • mashed potatoes

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1/2 Tbs dried basil
  • 1 Tbs poultry seasoning
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs olive oil
  • 4 Cornish game hens, about 1 1/2 pounds each, rinsed and dried thoroughly
  • 1 lemon, in 1/8s
  • 2 stalks celery, large chop
  • 4 garlic cloves, cut in half
  • 1 onion, quartered or in 1/8s
  • Preheat oven to 375 degrees F.

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. In a small mixing bowl, stir together salt, pepper, dried basil and poultry seasoning. Rub olive oil all over the hens and season each with the salt mixture. Place the hens on a baking sheet pan with plenty of space between them. Loosely stuff the cavities of each hen with some celery, lemon, garlic and onion.
  2. Roast the hens for about 1 hour or until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh registers 165 degrees F and the juices run clear. Remove the hens from the oven, loosely tent with foil and let rest 10 minutes before carving or serving.

Cornish Game Hens
1/2 Tbs dried basil
1 Tbs poultry seasoning
salt/pepper
2 Tbs olive oil
4 Cornish game hens, about 1 1/2 pounds each, rinsed and dried thoroughly
1 lemon, in 1/8s
2 stalks celery, large chop
4 garlic cloves, cut in half
1 onion, quartered or in 1/8s
Preheat oven to 375 degrees F.
In a small mixing bowl, stir together salt, pepper, dried basil and poultry seasoning. Rub olive oil all over the hens and season each with the salt mixture. Place the hens on a baking sheet pan with plenty of space between them.  Loosely stuff the cavities of each hen with some celery, lemon, garlic and onion.
Roast the hens for about 1 hour or until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh registers 165 degrees F and the juices run clear. Remove the hens from the oven, loosely tent with foil and let rest 10 minutes before carving or serving.
