Grilled Glazed Ham Kabobs

Ingredients

  • Grilled Glazed Ham, Pineapple, Pepper Kabobs
  • 1 ham steak
  • 1 pineapple, cored and cut into big chunks
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2-3 small oranges, cut into wedges
  • maraschino cherries
  • Glaze:
  • 1/4 cup orange juice
  • 1 Tbs cherry liquid
  • 1 can pineapple juice
  • 1 Tbs Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 cup cider vinegar
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp molasses
  • 1 Tbs cornstarch/water

Instructions

Heat grill.

Add orange, cherry and pineapple juices to the pan and reduce by 1/2.

Mix in the mustard, vinegar, brown sugar, soy sauce, molasses, stir 1 minute, reduce heat to low. Cook until thick, sticky, and bubbly. Probably need to add the cornstarch/water to thicken. It will also thicken a bit more as it cools. You want it to be thick to coat the kabobs and not drip into the grill.

Grill over medium heat and brush with glaze.

Parmesan, Herb Smashed Potatoes:

drizzle/brush with olive oil or butter. sprinkle with some grated parmesan cheese and some chopped herbs like chives, thyme, parsley.

Add sour cream and chives and cheddar or other baked potato toppings to make them like potato skins

Preheat oven to 425.

Parboil the potatoes until just tender when stabbed with a fork.

Remove from water to a lined baking sheet and smash them down flat with a potato masher. Let them cool/dry.

Bake for 20-30 minutes until they are lightly browned and have a little crisp to them.

Glazed Ham with Pineapple

4 Tbs butter

1 can pineapple rings, reserving liquid

1/4 cup orange juice

1 Tbs cherry liquid

1 Tbs Dijon mustard

1/4 cup cider vinegar

1/4 cup brown sugar

1 tsp soy sauce

1 tsp molasses

1-2 oranges, sliced (optional)

salt/pepper

Heat a large cast iron skillet set over medium-high heat, and melt 2 Tbs of butter. Add ham steaks and cook on each side, until lightly browned, about 2-3 minutes a side. Transfer to plate.

Add 2 Tbs of butter to skillet. Add pineapple and orange slices (if using) and saute until lightly browned, about 4 to 5 minutes per side. Remove the pineapple to the plate.

Add orange, cherry and pineapple juices to the pan and reduce by 1/2.

Heat oven to 200 degrees.

Mix in the mustard, vinegar, brown sugar, soy sauce, molasses, stir 1 minute, reduce heat to low. Cook until thick, sticky, and bubbly. Remove from heat and stir in 1-2 Tbs butter. Transfer ham steaks back into saute pan, coating both sides with the glaze. Place pineapple rings on top with a cherry in each. Drizzle some glaze over top and transfer skillet to the oven to heat through.

Served with

  • parmesan smashed potatoes
  • bell pepper rice pilaf
  • orange-spiced couscous
  • asparagus
  • Israeli couscous
  • sauteed apples
  • scalloped potatoes
