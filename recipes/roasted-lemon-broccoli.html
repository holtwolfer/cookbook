Roasted Lemon Broccoli

Ingredients

  • Roasted Lemon Broccoli
  • 1 head of broccoli, cut into florets
  • 2 Tbs olive oil (maybe a little more)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • pinch of crushed red pepper
  • aleppo pepper
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 lemon

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425.
  2. Microwave oil, red pepper and garlic for about 1 min to infuse the oil. Place broccoli on parchment-lined sheet pan, drizzle the oil on, season with aleppo pepper, salt/pepper and use parchment paper to toss to coat broccoli. Halve the lemon, cut half into slices and put on top of the broccoli. Roast for about 20 minutes until lightly browned and tender. Remove from oven and squeeze other half of lemon over the top.
  3. Notes: add grated parmesan before roasting
