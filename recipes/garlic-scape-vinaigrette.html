Garlic Scape Vinaigrette

Ingredients

  • 10 chopped garlic scapes
  • 10 basil leaves
  • 1 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 2 Tbs lemon juice
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender until fully mixed. Use with salads, or as an oil dip for fresh bread.
