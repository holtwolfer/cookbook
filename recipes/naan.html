Naan

Ingredients

  • 600g bread flour
  • 7g (1 packet / 2 tsp) instant or rapid rise yeast
  • 12g kosher salt
  • 24g sugar
  • 360g full-fat yogurt
  • 8 Tbs (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted

Instructions

  1. Combine flour, yeast, salt, and sugar in the bowl of a stand mixer and whisk until combined.
  2. Add the yogurt and knead with dough hook on low speed until the dough comes together into a smooth ball. It should stick slightly to the bottom of the bowl. Add a little more yogurt or milk if necessary.
  3. Continue to knead until slightly elastic, about 5 minutes. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and allow to rise at room temp until roughly doubled in size, about 2 hours.
  4. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface. Using a bench scraper, cut into 12 even pieces.
  5. Roll each piece a ball, place on a floured surface, leaving space between balls and cover with a floured cloth. Allow to rise at room temp until doubled in volume, about 2 hours.
