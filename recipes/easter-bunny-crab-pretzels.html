Easter Bunny Crab Pretzels

Ingredients

  • Pretzels:
  • 1 1/2 cups warm (110 to 115 degrees F) water
  • 1 Tbs sugar
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 package active dry yeast
  • 22 oz all-purpose flour, approximately 4 1/2 cups
  • 2 oz unsalted butter, melted
  • vegetable oil, for pan
  • 10 cups water
  • 2/3 cup baking soda
  • 1 large egg yolk beaten with 1 Tbs water
  • pretzel salt
  • Crab Topping:
  • 3 ounces cream cheese
  • 1 tablespoon Old Bay Seasoning
  • 2 teaspoons onion powder
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon Tabasco sauce

Instructions

  1. add a little mustard
  2. 8 oz crab claw meat
  3. 6 oz can jumbo lump crab
  4. 6 oz can white crab meat
  5. shredded cheddar and colby cheeses
  6. Pretzels (Alton Brown):
  7. Combine the water, sugar and kosher salt in the bowl of a stand mixer and sprinkle the yeast on top.
  8. Allow to sit for 5 minutes or until the mixture begins to foam. Add the flour and butter and, using the dough hook attachment, mix on low speed until well combined.
  9. Change to medium speed and knead until the dough is smooth and pulls away from the side of the bowl, approximately 4 to 5 minutes.
  10. Remove the dough from the bowl, clean the bowl and then oil it well with vegetable oil. Return the dough to the bowl, cover with plastic wrap and sit in a warm place for approximately 50 to 55 minutes or until the dough has doubled in size.
  11. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees F.
  12. Line 2 half-sheet pans with parchment paper and lightly brush with the vegetable oil. Set aside.
  13. In the meantime, turn the dough out onto a slightly oiled work surface and divide into 12 ( was 9, but that made really poofy pretzels) equal pieces. Roll out each piece of dough into a 24-inch rope. Make a U-shape with the rope, holding the ends of the rope, cross them over each other and press onto the bottom of the U in order to form the shape of a pretzel. Place onto the parchment-lined half sheet pan.
  14. Return to the half sheet pan, brush the top of each pretzel with the beaten egg yolk and water mixture and sprinkle with the pretzel salt. Bake until dark golden brown in color, approximately 12 to 14 minutes. Transfer to a cooling rack for at least 5 minutes before serving.
  15. Crab Topping and Finishing:
  16. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Leave cream cheese at room temperature to soften up.
  17. Place cream cheese in a large mixing bowl and add Old Bay, onion powder, Worcestershire sauce and Frank's Red Hot and blend until smooth. Fold in crabmeat last to avoid breaking it up too much. Place mixture in a plastic bag with one corner cut off. Pipe mixture onto pretzels.
  18. Cover the crab dip with shredded cheese. Bake until cheese is melted and slightly browned.
