Grilled Adobo Fish

Ingredients

  • 1 lime, juiced (about 2 Tbs)
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1-2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (1 1/2- to 2-pound) black rockfish
  • 1-2 lemons

Instructions

  1. FOR THE ADOBO:
  2. ½ tsp dried oregano
  3. 1/2 Tbs homemade sazón
  4. FOR THE FISH:
  5. 1-2 small oranges
  6. Lime wedges, hot sauce and chopped cilantro leaves, for garnish
  7. Mix all adobo ingredients together in a small bowl.
  8. Marinate the fish in lemon and orange juice for about 30 minutes.
  9. Grill fish over medium heat with peach chips in foil 'boats' with the adobo poured over the top.

Served with

  • grilled corn
  • couscous
