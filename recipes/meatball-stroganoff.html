Meatball Stroganoff

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1.5 lb lean ground round steak
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2/3 cup fresh bread crumbs
  • 1 ¼ cup drained yogurt
  • 4 Tbs grated onion (1 small onion)
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs paprika
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • ¼ lb mushrooms, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup chopped onions
  • 1-2 cloves chopped garlic
  • 1 tsp worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 cup sherry wine
  • 1 jam jar tomato paste
  • 4 Tbs flour
  • 3-4 Tbs sour cream
  • (don't use parsley)
  • In large bowl, mix the bread crumbs, 1/4 cup of yogurt and grated onion. Add the meat and egg and season with salt and pepper. Mix well with large fork, but do not overmix.
  • Shape the mixture into balls about 2 inches in diameter.

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Sprinkle a sheet of parchment paper with paprika and roll the meatballs, turning gently to coat well.
  2. Heat the butter in wide, blue dutch oven and cook the meatballs, turning gently and shaking the pan until they are nicely browned. Scatter the mushrooms, chopped onions and garlic around the meatballs. Shake the skillet to distribute the ingredients evenly. Cover and simmer for about 5 minutes. Shake the skillet a few times.
  3. In a small bowl, mix the sherry, flour and 2-3 Tbs tomato paste. Blend well. Add to the skillet, cover and simmer for 10 minutes, shaking often. Deglaze with more sherry in needed. Add worcestershire sauce.
  4. Stir in the remaining yogurt, and sour cream and bring to just a simmer over low heat. Do not overboil.
