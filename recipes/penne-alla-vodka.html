Penne alla Vodka

OneNote Pasta section · Original page dated Tuesday, November 17, 2020

Tags

pasta

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • Penne with Vodka Sauce
    • This works well with 1/2 the original recipe and almost a whole lb of pasta - add it and see how it looks. Mommers said use less cream.
    • 1 cups onion, chopped
    • 1 pkgs prosciutto, chopped
    • 1 Tbs garlic, minced
    • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper
    • 1 cans (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
    • 1 cups vodka
    • 1 cup peas
    • 1/2 cup cream
    • 1/3 cup basil, chopped
    • 1 box penne, prepared according to box

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    Heat olive oil in red dutch oven, add crushed red pepper, sautee onions until softened.

    Add prosciutto and brown lightly, add garlic, and cook 1-2 mins.

    Deglaze with 1/2 cup of vodka, letting alcohol cook off.

    Add tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, add ¼ cup vodka, and cook 10 mins at simmer.

    Add remaining ¼ cup vodka, simmer until alcohol is burned off. Add peas and cream, simmer 2 mins,

    Stir in penne, add basil and serve.
