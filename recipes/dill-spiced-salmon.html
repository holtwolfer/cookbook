Dill-Spiced Salmon

Tags

Marcus off-duty

Ingredients

  • 4 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 Tbs dijon mustard
  • 2 Tbs fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 4 1/2 tsp aleppo
  • 1 Tbs coriander seeds, finely ground
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, finely ground
  • salt/pepper
  • 4 (6 oz) salmon filets
  • 1 Tbs butter

Instructions

  1. In a food processor blend 2 Tbs olive oil, lemon juice, mustard, dill, garlic, aleppo, coriander, cumin and 1/4 tsp pepper into a paste. Transfer to a bowl.
  2. Season the salmon with salt & pepper.
  3. Heat 2 Tbs olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. When hot, add salmon, skin side down and brush with half of the paste. Cook for about 4 minutes and then add the butter. Continue to cook, spooning oil/butter over the salmon for 2 minutes.
  4. Salmon should be cooked most of the way through.
  5. Flip the salmon and brush the skin side with paste. Cook for just a few seconds and remove to let rest for a few minutes before serving.
