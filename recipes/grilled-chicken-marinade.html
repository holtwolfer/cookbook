Grilled Chicken Marinade

Ingredients

  • ½ cup oil
  • ½ cup balsamic vinegar
  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/8 cup lemon juice
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp fresh rosemary
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 6 chicken breasts or 3.5 lb chicken

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a large ziplock bag. Add chicken to the bag and marinate overnight.
