Fried Apples

Ingredients

  • 3 large apples, peeled, cored, cut into thick slices
  • 4 Tbs butter
  • 2 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 Tbs sugar
  • 2 Tbs brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ginger
  • 1/2 tsp sage

Instructions

  1. Melt butter in cast iron skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add apple slices and lower heat to medium low.
  3. Cook apples until starting to get tender and lightly browned.
  4. Add the remaining ingredients and continue to simmer, stirring often until apples are very tender and browned and there is a thick sauce.
