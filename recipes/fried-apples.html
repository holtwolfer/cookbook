Fried Apples

Tags

apple

Served with

    Ingredients

    • 3 large apples, peeled, cored, cut into thick slices
    • 4 Tbs butter
    • 2 tsp fresh lemon juice
    • 2 Tbs sugar
    • 2 Tbs brown sugar
    • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
    • 1/4 tsp ginger
    • 1/2 tsp sage

    Instructions

    Heat butter over medium heat in carbon steel or cast iron skillet.

    Add apples, lemon juice and then remaining ingredients. Cook, stirring frequently until softened, lightly browned.
