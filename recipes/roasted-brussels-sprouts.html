Roasted Brussels Sprouts

Ingredients

  • brussels sprouts, halved (or quartered if super big)
  • olive oil
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400.
  2. Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper on parchment paper.
  3. Roast for 15-20 minutes.

Notes and variations

Variations:

Spices: paprika; sumac; Aleppo pepper;

Vinegar: balsamic; apple cider; sherry; balsamic glaze;
