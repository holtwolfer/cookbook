Roasted Brussels Sprouts

Tags

Served with

    Ingredients

    • brussels sprouts, halved (or quartered if super big)
    • olive oil
    • salt/pepper

    Instructions

    Preheat oven to 425.

    Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper on parchment paper. Roast for 30-40 minutes.

    Variations/additions:

    Spices: paprika; sumac; aleppo pepper;

    Vinegar: balsamic; apple cider; sherry;
