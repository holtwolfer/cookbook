Chicken Chimichangas

Tags

Mexican

Served with

  • sour cream
  • guacamole
  • salsa
  • avocado slices

Related recipes

  • Guacamole

Ingredients

  • Boiled Chicken
  • some combination of:
  • 1 white onion, halved
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bay leaf
  • salt
  • 6 black peppercorns
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • celery
  • stock
  • lime peel

Instructions

  1. bring to a simmer over medium heat. Add chicken, bring back to simmer, cook 30 minutes. Turn off heat and let chicken cool in the broth. Remove chicken and shred.
  2. If making salsa verde, boil the tomatillos with the chicken.
  3. 2 bell peppers (roasted, chopped)
  4. 1-2 poblano peppers (roasted, chopped)
  5. 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  6. salsa verde (recipe below, or jar)
  7. large flour tortillas
  8. neutral oil for frying
  9. Sauté onion and garlic in oil until tender, add peppers, pinto beans, salsa and spices and then chicken and stir well and cook until warmed.
  10. Put a large spoonful of chicken filling and some cheese in the middle of a flour tortilla and fold all sides in to form a square.
  11. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  12. Fry the chimichangas until lightly browned, about 2 minutes per side.
  13. Remove to a plate lined with paper towel.
  14. To Marinate and Grill:
  15. Mix all marinade ingredients in a gallon bag and marinate the chicken for at least 4 hours or overnight. Grill chicken over low, indirect heat to avoid drying out, and grill the peppers over the flames on the outside. Put peppers in a a paper bag and let cool, peal skin off, deseed and chop. Let chicken cool, covered in foil and shred with 2 forks.
