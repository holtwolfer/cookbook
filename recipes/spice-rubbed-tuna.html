Spice-rubbed Tuna

Ingredients

  • 2 albacore tuna filets
  • 2 Tbs olive/canola oil

Garnish: 4 lemon wedges

  • 6 Tbs aged balsamic vinegar
  • 3 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove (peeled and halved)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 Tbs coriander (ground)
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • pepper

Balsamic reduction

  • 1/2 cup roughly chopped parsley
  • 1/2 lb. Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock
  • 5 oz Greek yogurt (2/3 cup)
  • 2 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin?

Instructions

  1. Place the balsamic vinegar, lemon juice, and garlic in a small saucepan over medium-low heat.
  2. Simmer until the mixture reduces by half.
  3. Cream out with 1-2 Tbs butter. Turn off the heat and reserve until needed. This sauce does not have to be hot for serving. The reduction will thicken slightly as it cools.

Rub

  1. In a small bowl, combine the salt, coriander, paprika. Rub tuna filets.
  2. Evenly coat the tuna steaks with the freshly ground black pepper, and gently press it in, so that it adheres to the surface, being careful not to smash the flesh.
  3. Place a cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add the oil and swirl to coat the pan. When you see small wisps of smoke, add the tuna to the pan and sear 1 minute on first side, 30 seconds for the other 2 sides.
  4. Remove to a cutting board, let rest a few minutes. Slice crosswise in about 1/2 inch slices. Serve with a small amount of sauce drizzled alongside. Garnish with lemon wedges.

Mashed Potatoes

  1. 2/3 cup olive oil
  2. Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  3. 3 cloves garlic

milk

  1. butter
  2. Purée parsley, 1/3 cup oil, salt, and pepper in a food processor until smooth; set aside.
  3. Bring a 2-qt. saucepan of salted water to a boil. Add potatoes and garlic to pan; boil until tender, 18–20 minutes, then drain and transfer to a big bowl.
  4. Add stock, salt, and pepper; mash until smooth and keep warm.

Smoky Roasted Cauliflower

  1. salt/pepper
  2. 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  3. Preheat oven to 425.
  4. Mix yogurt, oil, spices in a bowl.
  5. Place cauliflower on a parchment lined sheet pan. Toss with yogurt mixture to coat. Roast for 35-40 minutes until tender and lightly browned

Served with

  • garlic mashed potatoes with herbs, salt potatoes, smoky roasted cauliflower, side salad w/ vinaigrette
  • couscous
