Cauliflower and Carrots with Parmesan Panko

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower and Carrots with Parmesan Panko
  • 1 small head cauliflower cut into florets
  • 2-3 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 1/2 cup panko
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • salt/pepper
  • olive oil

Instructions

  1. Mix the panko, parmesan, salt/pepper together and add olive oil, mixing it in, until the texture is that of wet sand.
  2. (Optional to speed up cooking steam the veggies until almost cooked)
  3. Heat oil in a skillet and cook veggies until tender, stir in the panko mixture and heat for just a few minutes, mixing.
