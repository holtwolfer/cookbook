Cornmeal Crusted Black Bass

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs black bass (or other whitefish - yelloweye, cod)
  • 1 lemon, cut in half
  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 1-2 Tbs olive oil for frying
  • salt/pepper

For serving

  • 2 to 3 Tbs unsalted butter, melted and warm
  • lemon wedges

Instructions

  1. Cut bass into smaller pieces, dry with paper towel and squeeze lemon juice over both sides.  Let sit and warm to room temperature, 20-30 minutes, flipping them occasionally.
  2. Season with salt, pepper, paprika (or sumac) on both sides and then press into corn meal to coat.  Heat oil over medium high heat until smoking.  Cook fish until browned, about 2-3 minutes per side.  Temp should be around 120.  Remove to plate with paper towels.
  3. Tomatoes
  4. Cut thick slices of tomato. Season with salt and pepper and drizzle with olive oil on both sides. Press into cornmeal to coat and fry in olive oil over medium-high heat, about 1-2 minutes per side to just brown. Serve drizzled with balsamic reduction.

Served with

  • healthy lady garlic fries
  • peas
  • tomato slices that were drizzled with oil, coated with cornmeal and fried, drizzled with balsamic reduction
  • rolls
  • grits
  • collards
  • couscous
  • glazed carrots
