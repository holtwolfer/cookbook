Loaded Baked Potato Soup

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs russet potatoes
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 8 slices thick cut bacon
  • 1 large yellow onion
  • 3 stalks celery
  • 3 large carrots
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 3 Tbs butter
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 32 oz chicken broth
  • 2 cups half & half
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 bunch chives

Instructions

  1. Wash all of your vegetables
  2. Cut bacon into 1 inch squares with kitchen shears. Cook in a large pot over low heat, rotating occasionally. They'll overlap at first but will shrink down as they cook. Remove and set aside on a paper towel once crisp. Leave 2 Tbs of drippings in the pot.
  3. While the bacon cooks, peel the potatoes and cut them into 1 inch cubes. Add to a stock pot and cover the potatoes with 1 inch of water. Add salt and boil gently for 10-15 minutes or until very fork tender. Drain and set aside.
  4. Dice the onion, celery, and carrot and add to the pot with the bacon drippings and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  5. Add garlic and butter and cook 1 more minute.
  6. Whisk in flour and cook for another minute.
  7. Slowly add the chicken broth. Use a wooden spoon to loosen any bacon remnants from the bottom of the pot, this will add flavor.
  8. Slowly add the half & half and let it simmer.
  9. Stir in potatoes, then mix in sour cream, and pepper.
  10. Remove from the heat. If desired, blend half of the batch with an immersion blender or transfer to a blender in batches. This will thicken the soup.
  11. Sprinkle with shredded cheese and stir until combined. Make sure the base of the soup isn't too hot or the cheese won't melt creamy and smooth. The soup will continue to thicken as it sits.
  12. Garnish with chives and bacon, serve!
