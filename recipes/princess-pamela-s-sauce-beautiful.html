Princess Pamela's Sauce Beautiful

Ingredients

  • 8 Tbs peach preserves
  • 1 lemon, juiced (about ¼ cup)
  • 3 Tbs brown sugar
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 1 Tbs vegetable or other neutral-tasting oil
  • 1 Tbs vinegar
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • salt/pepper and Worcestershire sauce, to taste

Instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, combine preserves, lemon juice, sugar, butter, oil, vinegar, paprika and ½ cup water and stir to combine. Cook on medium until thickened, stirring occasionally. Season to taste with salt, pepper and Worcestershire sauce.
