Oven Ribs

Tags

pork

Served with

  • fries
  • salt potatoes
  • corn
  • slaw
  • bread + butter
  • baked beans

Ingredients

  • (we have 2 racks in freezer… 1.5 racks might be best, but probably do 2 next time for leftovers)

Instructions

Rub in a rib rub and some lemon juice

Cut rack in 3 smaller sections

Place on AL foil curved section up

Put 2 ice cubes on ribs and cover with AL foil

Place ribs on cookie sheet rib side up and bake 275 3 1/2 to 4 hours

Let cool completely

Put back on

AL foil and pour off liquid carefully or ribs will fall off bone

Put in fridge until ready to grill

Put on something to grill so they don't fall apart and coat with the sauce you are using. Grill maybe 10 minutes

Rub:

brown sugar

paprika

smoked paprika

smoked salt

celery salt

pepper

dry mustard

onion powder

sumac

poultry seasoning

bbq sauce to serve
