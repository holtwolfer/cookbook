Panko Salmon

Tags

salmoneasyweeknightsheet-panone-pot

Served with

  • roasted potatoes
  • couscous
  • roasted green beans
  • roasted broccoli & zucchini

Ingredients

  • salmon filet
  • Lemon juice

sumac

  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1-2 Tbs Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp dill
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 cup panko
  • olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375.
  2. Squeeze lemon juice on salmon, season with salt/pepper, sumac
  3. mix sour cream, dijon, dill.  season salmon with salt and pepper, rub sour cream mixture on.  mix panko, lemon zest, olive oil until it has the consistency of wet sand and top salmon.  bake on a lined sheet pan for about 30 minutes until temperature is around 125.

Notes

Additions: lemon zest, chives
