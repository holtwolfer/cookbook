Green Beans with Vinaigrette

Ingredients

  • Green Beans with Vinaigrette
  • 1 lb green beans
  • 1-2 Tbs sherry/red wine/balsamic vinegar
  • 1 Tbs Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 3-4 Tbs olive oil
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Whisk all vinaigrette ingredients together. Steam green beans and then toss with vinaigrette
