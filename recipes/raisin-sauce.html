Raisin Sauce

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup raisins
  • 1 1/3 cup cold water
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 4 tsp cornstarch
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1 Tbs vinegar
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp dry mustard

Instructions

  1. Cook raisins in water until tender. Add remaining ingredients. Bring to a boil and simmer for a few minutes.
