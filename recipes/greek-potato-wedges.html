Greek Potato Wedges

Ingredients

  • 4-5 medium potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 3 Tbs olive oil
  • 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • salt/pepper
  • 3 Tbs lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees.
  2. Heat olive oil, garlic, oregano, paprika, salt/pepper in glass measuring cup in the microwave for 30 seconds. Whisk in lemon juice.
  3. Toss potatoes with half of the vinaigrette on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast for 45 minutes. Toss with remaining vinaigrette.
  4. Notes: could add some parmesan
