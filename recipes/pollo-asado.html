Pollo Asado

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 3 lbs chicken breast
  • 1/2 cup Salsa Lizano
  • 1/2 white onion, roughly chopped
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 Tbs vinegre de frutas
  • 2 tsp paprika

Sazon Completa

  • dried garlic
  • dried onion
  • cumin
  • oregano
  • salt/pepper

Rice

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1.5 cups water and/or stock
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 tsp annatto powder
  • 2 tsp salsa lizano
  • bay leaf

Instructions

Chicken

  1. Butterfly chicken breasts or pound them so that they are thin.
  2. Mix all the ingredients of the marinade and some sazon completa together and marinate chicken 3 hours to overnight.
  3. Preheat your grill with the lid closed on medium-low heat.
  4. Raise the grill to medium temperature, Remove the chicken from the marinade pan and place it on the grill.
  5. Grill turning for grill marks and flipping.

Rice

  1. Heat oil in the pot, add annatto powder and salsa. Stir briefly until sizzling.
  2. Stir in rice to coat.
  3. Add water or stock and the bay leaf.

Served with

  • rice
  • brazi bites
