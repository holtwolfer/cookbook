Tandoori Black Cod with Mango Salsa

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Wednesday, December 11, 2019

Tags

black codsablefishwhite fishyellow fish

Served with

  • couscous
  • mexi-kale salad

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 2-3 black cod fillets
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 tsp sumac
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 4-5 green cardamom pods, crushed and finely chopped, discarding the shell
  • 1 large clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 semi-ripe mango, peeled and finely diced (peach also works)
  • 2 Tbs red onion, minced (1/4 small)
  • 2 Tbs cilantro, minced
  • 1 avocado, chopped
  • spinach

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!-REMEMBER IT NEEDS TO MARINATE OVERNIGHT-!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

Black Cod

  1. 1/3 cup Greek yogurt (can also add a little sour cream)

Salsa

  1. 1/2 tsp sea salt
  2. juice of 1/2 lemon
  3. zest from 1/2 lemon
  4. In a bowl, whisk together the marinade ingredients. Rub all over fish and marinate overnight.
  5. Preheat oven to 400 degrees.
  6. To make the mango relish, combine the mango, red onion, cilantro, salt, avocado, and lemon juice together in a bowl and stir together. Let sit for 15-20 minutes, stirring a few times.
  7. Place fish on a lined baking sheet and spread any remaining marinade on the top. Bake for about 20 minutes and finish under the broiler for 2-3 minutes. Final temperature should be around 135 degrees.
