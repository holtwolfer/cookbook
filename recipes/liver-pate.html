Liver Pate

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken livers, well-trimmed
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 3 Tbs olive oil
  • 1-2 shallots thinly sliced
  • 5 sage leaves
  • 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs bourbon
  • 3 Tbs fresh lemon juice
  • 2 sticks unsalted butter, at room temperature
  • 2 Tbs chopped brined green peppercorns, plus 1/2 Tbs brine from the jar
  • Rye crackers, for serving

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, refrigerate the livers in the milk overnight.
  2. Drain the livers and pat dry. In a large nonstick skillet, heat 2 tablespoons of the olive oil. Add the onion and 5 sage leaves and cook over moderate heat until deep golden, about 8 minutes. Stir in the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Scrape the mixture into a food processor.
  3. In the same skillet, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil. Season the livers with salt and pepper and cook over moderately high heat until cooked through, about 12-15 minutes. Add the bourbon; cook until almost evaporated, 1 minute.
  4. Add the livers to the food processor and add the lemon juice; pulse to finely chop. With the machine on, add the butter, 1 tablespoon at a time, until the pâté is very smooth. Stir in the green peppercorns and brine. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Transfer the pâté to 2 bowls. Cover and refrigerate until chilled. Garnish with sage and serve with rye crackers.
