Orange-Spiced Couscous

Ingredients

  • 1 small carrot, diced
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  • (onion, diced)
  • 1 cup fresh orange juice
  • 1/2 cup stock
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • dash of red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp sumac
  • 1 can chick peas
  • 1/4 cup craisins
  • 1.5 cups couscous
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Combine the orange juice, cinnamon, cumin, red pepper, coriander, craisins, and water in a saucepan and cook over high heat until the mixture boils. Stir in the couscous, cover and remove from heat. Let sit for about 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and season with salt/pepper.
  2. Additions: onion/scallion/shallot/scapes, roasted red pepper, bell pepper, tomato, herbs
