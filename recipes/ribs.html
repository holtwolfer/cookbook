Ribs

Ingredients

  • Wood chip bath

Instructions

  1. Smoker preheated to 250oF. Outside temperature was around 40oF.
  2. Water bowl filled with beer.
  3. Start time: 10:25 am
  4. It took almost an hour after putting the ribs on to get back up to temp.
  5. Sprayed for the first time at noon.
  6. Sprayed every 20-30 minutes. Smoker temp was reading on the low side all day.
  7. Tried wrapping 2 racks in foil with a little apple juice to steam cook them. They ended up incredibly tender, but lacking the flavor of the others. The real technique would be 3 hours smoking, 2 hours in foil on smoker and then 1 hour on the grill being brushed with bbq sauce.
  8. Pulled the ribs around 5pm for about 6.5 hours of cooking time. Perfectly cooked.
  9. Mop Sauce
  10. 2 cups vinegar
  11. 1/2 cup water
  12. 1/2 cup dijon mustard
  13. 1 tsp salt
  14. Whisk all ingredients together.
  15. 12:30 on
  16. country ribs
  17. 1 rack ribs
