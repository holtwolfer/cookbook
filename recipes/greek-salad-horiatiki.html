Greek Salad (Horiatiki)

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs roughly chopped fresh parsley, plus more for garnish
  • 2 medium vine-ripened tomatoes, cut into 1 ½" pieces
  • 1 small cucumber, peeled, halved lengthwise, and sliced crosswise into ¼" pieces
  • ½ medium white onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 Tbs red wine vinegar
  • ⅛ tsp dried oregano, plus more
  • salt/pepper
  • 6 oz feta, cut into thick slabs
  • 8 oz kalamata olives

Instructions

  1. Combine parsley, tomatoes, cucumbers, and onions in a bowl. In a small bowl, whisk together oil, vinegar, and oregano; season with salt and pepper and pour over cucumber mixture. Toss. Transfer salad to a serving bowl and top with feta and olives. Garnish with more oregano; season with pepper.

Put the chicken bag in a shallow dish and marinate, in the refrigerator, for at least 12 hours and up to 24 hours.
