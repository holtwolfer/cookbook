Mexican Lasagna

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef
  • taco seasoning
  • onion
  • red bell pepper
  • corn
  • spinach
  • zucchini
  • black beans
  • refried beans
  • corn tortillas
  • roasted tomato salsa
  • scallions
  • 14 oz can roasted tomatoes
  • small can tomato sauce
  • pepperjack cheese
  • colby jack cheese
  • sour cream
  • guac-cilantro, avocado

Instructions

  1. Cook beef + seasoning, onions, set aside
  2. Sautee veggies, corn, black beans, tomatoes
  3. Spread refried beans on tortillas and line baking dish, layer with beef, veggies, cheese, salsa another layer of tortillas and refried beans, beef, veggies, cheese, salsa
  4. back at 375 until bubbly and melty
