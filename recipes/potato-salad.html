Potato Salad

Ingredients

  • (This is 1/2 of the original recipe from Serious Eats)
  • 2 lbs russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 3/4 inch cubes
  • kosher salt
  • 2 Tbs sugar, divided
  • 3 Tbs rice wine vinegar, divided
  • 2-3 ribs celery, finely diced (about 1/2 cup)
  • 1/2 medium red onion, finely diced (about 1/4 cup)
  • 2 scallions, green parts only, thinly sliced (about 1/4 cup, optional)
  • 2 Tbs cup fresh parsley leaves, washed and minced (optional)
  • 1/4 cup chopped cornichons (see notes) or pickle relish
  • 1 Tbs dijon mustard (more or less to taste)
  • 1/2 - 3/4 cups mayonnaise
  • pepper
  • Hard boil 3-4 eggs. Cover with water, bring to a boil, turn off heat and let sit for 10 mins.

Instructions

  1. Add 2 quarts water to a large saucepan. Add potatoes, 1 Tbs kosher salt, 1 Tbs sugar, and 1 Tbs vinegar. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce to simmer, and cook, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are tender, about 10 minutes. Drain potatoes and transfer to rimmed baking sheet. Spread into even layer, then sprinkle with 1 Tbs vinegar. Allow to cool to room temperature, about 30 minutes.
  2. In a large bowl, combine remaining 1 Tbs sugar, remaining 2 Tbs vinegar, celery, onion, scallion (if using), parsley (if using), pickles, mustard, and mayonnaise. Stir with rubber spatula to combine. Fold in potatoes and eggs. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Cover and rest in fridge for at least 1 hour and up to 3 days before serving.
