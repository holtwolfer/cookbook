Sushi

Tags

Japanese

Served with

  • miso soup
  • salad
  • dumplings

Ingredients

  • Rice (Sumeshi)
  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 3 cups water
  • 6 Tbs rice vinegar
  • 3 Tbs sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Rolling / sides
  • nori
  • gari (pickled ginger)
  • wasabi

Seafood

  • salmon

tuna

  • 1/4 lb sashimi grade tuna
  • smoked salmon / cream cheese
  • crab
  • krab

Veggies

  • avocado

cucumber

  • scallions
  • carrots, matchsticked

Yield: 6 cups

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 2 Tbs sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup rice vinegar
  • Rice:

Instructions

  1. In a fine strainer, rinse the rice with cold running water, until water runs clear. Pour rice into pot with 2 cups cold water; let sit 30 minutes.
  2. Bring rice to a boil over medium-high heat; immediately turn heat to low, and cover. Cook 10 minutes, remove from heat and let sit, covered, 15 minutes. In a small saucepan, dissolve the sugar and salt in the rice vinegar over a low flame. Pour evenly over rice and fold gently with a wooden spoon to mix. Leave rice in front of a fan, or fan with a piece of cardboard for 15 minutes or until rice is just above room temperature.
  3. Philadelphia Roll: Place and pack rice on the nori leaving a 1 inch strip on one end. On the other end lay out a strip of thinly sliced smoked salmon, then cream cheese and scallions. Roll and cut.
  4. Tuna Roll: Place a strip of tuna on the rice and roll then cut.
  5. California Roll: Lay out a strip of fake crab, avocado, cucumber

Spicy tuna

  1. mix chopped tuna with mayo and siracha
