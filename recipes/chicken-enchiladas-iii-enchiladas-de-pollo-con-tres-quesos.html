Chicken Enchiladas III - Enchiladas de Pollo con Tres Quesos

Tags

Pati JinichMexican

Ingredients

  • 16 corn or flour tortillas
  • Colorado chile red salsa
  • 4 cups shredded chicken
  • 1 cup crema or sour cream
  • 2 cups Oaxaca or mozzarella (8 oz)
  • 2 cups jack
  • 1 cup cojita
  • 1 avocado for garnish
  • Salsa
  • 2 lbs tomatoes or 1 (28 oz) can tomatoes
  • 2 dried colorado chiles ( sub sun-dried tomatoes / roasted red peppers / bell pepper )
  • 2 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1/2 cup chopped white onion
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs vegetable oil
  • With canned tomatoes and no dried chiles:
  • Process tomatoes, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, bell pepper, onion, salt/pepper in food processor.

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a sauce pan and add the pureed sauce. Simmer 5-10 minutes to blend flavors.
  2. 1 white onion, halved
  3. 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  4. bring to a simmer over medium heat. Add chicken, bring back to simmer, cook 30 minutes. Turn off heat and let chicken cool in the broth. Remove chicken and shred.
  5. add zucchini / spinach / ramps / chives / seasoning
  6. Pour about 1 cup of salsa into a 9x13 baking dish, spread evenly
  7. Add about 1/4 cup of chicken to each tortilla and roll.
  8. Pour remaining salsa over tortillas and top with cream and then the cheeses
  9. bake for 15-20 minutes
  10. Serve with: avocado; salsa; guacamole;
