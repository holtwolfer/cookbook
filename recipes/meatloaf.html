Meatloaf

Ingredients

Glaze:

  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 1/2 cup chili sauce
  • 2 Tbs cider vinegar
  • 1 Tbs balsamic glaze
  • 1-2 tsp soy sauce

Meatloaf:

  • 2 tsp vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup greek yogurt
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp sage
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 tsp dried thyme powder
  • dash hot sauce (Franks)
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 2/3 cup (15) saltines, crushed
  • 1/3 cup minced fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 pkg bacon (Nueskes)

Instructions

For the Glaze:

  1. Combine all ingredients in a sauce pan, whisk, set aside (1/2 goes on meatloaf, other 1/2 is thickened for a side sauce).

For the Meatloaf:

  1. Line a 9x13 pan with foil.  Preheat oven to 375oF.
  2. Heat oil in a small skillet and sauté the onion and garlic until softened, about 5 mins.
  3. In a large bowl whisk the eggs, yogurt, mustard, worcestershire, salt, pepper, paprika, oregano, sage, thyme and hot sauce.
  4. Add the meat, crackers, parsley and onion/garlic and mix together well with a big fork.  The mix should not stick to the sides, if it does add a little milk at a time until it doesn't stick.
  5. Move the mixture to the 9x13 pan and form into a loaf shape, roughly the size of bread loaf pan.
  6. Brush with half the glaze
  7. Drape the bacon over top of the loaf, overlapping slightly and tucking the ends under.
  8. Bake until the bacon is crisp and center temp is 160, about 1 hour 20 minutes.  Let cool 10 minutes.
  9. Simmer the glaze over medium heat to thicken slightly and serve on the side.
  10. -If making ahead, put in cold oven, heat to 375, bake for 1.5 hours.

Served with

  • mashed potatoes
  • celery root puree
  • peas
