Bluephies Chicken Enchiladas

OneNote Chicken section · Original page dated Wednesday, August 19, 2020

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 3 chicken breasts, butterflied
  • smoked Spanish paprika
  • ancho chile powder
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 poblano pepper, diced
  • 1 medium red onion, diced
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, diced
  • 1 tsp mexican oregano
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 Tbs Mae Ploy
  • 10 oz spinach
  • chopped tomato
  • 8 oz cream cheese
  • 8 oz goat cheese
  • flour tortillas
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • grated cheese to top
  • grated red pepper to top
  • Preheat Oven to 350
  • Season the chicken and grill.  Shred

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Heat oil in large skillet and cook peppers and onion until softened.  Then add garlic, thyme, oregano, mae ploy, cook 1-2 mins while mixing well.  Add spinach and cook until wilted.  Lower heat and blend in the cream cheese and goat cheese.
  2. Lightly butter one side of tortilla, add filling and roll with buttered side out.  Place in 9x13 pan.  When all enchiladas are done put sliced tomato on top and cover with a little shredded cheese.  Bake for 10-15 mins until cheese is melted and tortillas are lightly browned.
  3. If refrigerated overnight 4:45 at 350
  4. Boiling chicken breast
