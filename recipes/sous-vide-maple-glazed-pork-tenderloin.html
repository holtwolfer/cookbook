Sous-vide maple glazed pork tenderloin

Ingredients

  • Sous-vide Maple-Glazed Pork Tenderloin
  • 2 12- to 14-ounce pork tenderloins
  • 2 tsp crumbled dried sage leaves
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 6 Tbs pure maple syrup
  • 6 Tbs apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • sage or rosemary, finely chopped

Instructions

Seal and sous vide at 140 for 1 hour

Whisk together glaze ingredients

Brown pork in cast iron skillet with butter. Add in the glaze mixture and cook over high heat, turning the pork in the glaze.

Served with

  • fried apples
  • roasted tatos
  • mashed
  • cauliflower
