Roasted Salmon with Jalapeño, Honey and Lime

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Tuesday, March 16, 2021

Tags

nyt

Served with

  • rice
  • couscous
  • snap peas
  • broccoli

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • ¼ cup honey
  • 4-6 mini bell peppers, thinly sliced crosswise into rings
  • 2 scallion whites
  • 2 Tbs apple cider vinegar or rice wine vinegar
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce or tamari
  • 4 (6-oz) skin-on salmon fillets, about 1-inch thick
  • olive oil, for brushing
  • salt, to taste
  • ½ lime, for serving
  • chopped fresh cilantro, for serving (optional)
  • scallion greens
  • steamed rice, for serving

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees.
  2. While the oven heats, combine the honey, mini bells, scallion whites, vinegar and soy sauce in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to low and simmer for 10 minutes. Set aside.
  3. Pat the salmon fillets dry using paper towels. Brush a 9-by-9-inch baking pan (or other similar-size baking pan or dish) with olive oil, then place the fillets in the pan, arranging them evenly without crowding. Brush the fillets generously with olive oil and sprinkle with salt. Pour the glaze and jalapeños over the salmon, spooning some of the glaze onto the fish as it collects in the bottom of the pan.
  4. Roast the salmon for 6 minutes, then baste with the glaze. Return to the oven and roast until the salmon is cooked through but still slightly rare in the center, another 6 to 8 minutes.
  5. Spoon the glaze in the pan over the salmon, then squeeze the lime over the salmon, and sprinkle with salt and cilantro, if using. Serve hot with rice on the side, pouring any glaze that remains in the pan over the rice.

Notes

NOTES: glaze was runny, honey flavor was too strong; maybe try honey + balsamic reduction;
