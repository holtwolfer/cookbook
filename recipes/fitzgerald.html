Fitzgerald
2 oz. gin
3/4 oz. lemon juice
3/4 oz. simple syrup
2 dashes Angostura Bitters
Add all ingredients to a cocktail shaker with ice and shake for eight to 10 seconds. Strain up into a cocktail glass or coupe, and garnish with a lemon peel.
