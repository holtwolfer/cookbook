Calvados chicken and 'easy' calvados chicken

OneNote Chicken section · Original page dated Friday, September 20, 2019

Review needed: Multiple Calvados chicken variants exist; compare before consolidating.

Tags

FrenchLa Bonne Femmefall

Served with

  • roasted veggies
  • mashed potatoes

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Calvados Chicken
  • olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • 3 chicken breasts
  • 1-2 leeks, chopped
  • 1 apple, peeled, chopped
  • 1 head of garlic
  • apple cider vinegar
  • half & half
  • calvados
  • apple cider
  • 1-2 Tbs butter

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Season chicken with salt/pepper. Heat oil in small Dutch oven. Brown chicken on both sides. Add leaks, apples, vinegar. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes. When chicken is basically cooked through add the calvados and cider and cook 10-15 minutes to reduce. Add half & half and reduce heat to low. Allow to thicken. Finish by stirring in butter.
  2. 'easy' calvados chicken
  3. 2 apples, thinly sliced
  4. brussel sprouts, halved
  5. onion, cut into chunks
  6. herbs... sage or rosemary or thyme
  7. onions, cunt into chunks
  8. Preheat oven to 375.  Brussels sprouts and apple slices placed in red oval baking dish, sprouts to one side, apples and onions on the other.  Chicken placed on apples, seasoned with salt, pepper, paprika.  Sprouts seasoned with salt and pepper.   Everything drizzled with olive oil and then apple cider and stock added to bottom.
  9. Tatos and onions placed on baking sheet lined with parchment.  Season with salt and pepper, paprika, mustard powder.  Add a few dollops of dijon and drizzle with olive oil.  Toss together using the parchment paper until coated.
  10. Halve the squash, scoop out seeds, add a dollop of butter to each half.
  11. when chicken is done remove chicken and sprouts.  Pour all of some (to taste) of the liquid in the dish into a sauce pan, add calvados and more stock if needed and bring to a boil and allow to reduce a little and burn off the alcohol.  Off the heat stir in a little butter and then mix in half and half.
  12. original calvados chicken recipe
  13. pearl or cipollini onions, whole
  14. roast the chicken breasts and onions with a little oil in a dutch oven, uncovered
  15. 1/2 cup chicken stock
  16. reduce to 1/3 of a cup
  17. add 3/4 cup half and half
  18. potatoes, cut into pieces
  19. sweet potatoes, whole (they were very small)
  20. beets, cut into pieces
  21. leeks, sliced lengthwise
  22. oven at 375. garlic wrapped in foil, all other veggies except kale oiled and placed on a cookie sheet lined with parchment paper. cook ~45 minutes adding the kale for the last 10 minutes. Chop up veggies and mash in the roasted garlic.
  23. used blue paella like dutch oven for 3 hens.  Put tatoes, carrots, celery root, small onions (quartered) around.  stirred up veggies with salt, pepper, paprika, olive oil (also on hens), added some chicken stock and roasted at 400 degrees for a little over an hour.  Should have flipped birds halfway through.
  24. Took veggies and birds out of pan, heated pan on stove top and added pan drippings, apple juice and calvados, reduced, stirred in some skim milk.  (should be half and half or cream)
