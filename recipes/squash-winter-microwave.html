Squash, winter, microwave

Ingredients

  • Squash, winter, microwave
  • winter squash
  • 2-4 Tbs butter
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut squash in half, scoop seeds out. Put a little water in a casserole and place squash halves cut-side down. Cover with plastic wrap and nuke for 12 minutes. Scoop out into a bowl with butter, mash with the back of a spoon, mix in salt and pepper to taste.
  2. Additions: cayenne pepper, paprika, sage
