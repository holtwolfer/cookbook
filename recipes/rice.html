Rice

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Thursday, March 19, 2020

Tags

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • onion, chopped
    • celery
    • garlic
    • tomato paste
    • paprika
    • coriander
    • red/green bell pepper, cut into squares
    • red onion, cut into small squares
    • 1 cup brown rice
    • 2 cups stock

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    1. Heat oil in sauce pan.  Sautee onion, celery until softened.  Add spices and garlic.  Add paste and cook 1-2 mins.  Add peppers and red onion, mix in.  Add in rice, stir/cook 1 minute.  Add stock, bring to boil, reduce heat, cook until rice is done.
