Sous Vide Potatoes

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Russett Burbank Potatoes
  • Olive Oil
  • Garlic Powder
  • Onion Powder
  • Paprika Powder
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • Rosemary Flakes
  • Parsley Flakes
  • Step 1

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Cut potatoes into 1 inch cubes.
  2. Step 2
  3. Marinade with all the other ingedients and mix well.
  4. Step 3
  5. Put potatoes in sous vide bag and cook at 83 deg Celsius for 1hr
  6. Step 4
  7. Remove potatoes from bag and dab dry to remove any moisture.
  8. Step 5
  9. Sear on a pan or grill oven until golden and cruchy. Serve hot!
  10. Finishing Steps
  11. Step 0
  12. Sprinkle some paprika powder to taste and parsley flakes after plating.
