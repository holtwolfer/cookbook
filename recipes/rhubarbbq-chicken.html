RhubarBBQ Chicken

Ingredients

  • For the Barbecue Sauce
  • 5 stalks rhubarb, chopped
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 cup sugar (1/2 cup + date syrup + honey + balsamic glaze)
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1-2 tsp crushed red chilies, or to taste
  • 1 + 1/2 tsp ground mustard
  • paprika
  • aleppo pepper
  • citrus pepper
  • spike
  • sumac
  • 1 tsp coarse salt
  • dash of : soy sauce
  • For the Chicken
  • 5 chicken leg quarters
  • 1/2 gallon milk
  • 1 + 1/2 Tbsp coarse salt
  • 1 tsp crushed red chilies
  • Rinse chicken and pat dry.  Loosen skin so marinade can sneak under it.  In a large lidded container or stockpot, combine milk, salt, and chilies, and stir until salt is dissolved.  Marinate chicken in milk mixture overnight in refrigerator.
  • Roughly chop the rhubarb, onion, and bell pepper.  Combine all sauce ingredients in a pot and heat until boiling, cover and reduce to a low simmer for 30-45 minutes.
  • Using an immersion blender puree sauce until smooth and thick.  If sauce remains too thin after pureeing, return to heat and cook until sauce reaches desired consistency.

Instructions

  1. Grill chicken on indirect heat on a covered grill maintained at ~300°F.  Grill skin-side down first, for about 15 minutes.  Flip chickens, baste with barbecue sauce (baste under the skin too), then continue to cook for another 30 minutes.  Baste chickens once more about 15 minutes prior to pulling the chicken off the grill.  Ensure chicken has reached an internal temperature of 170°F before removing from grill.
  2. Serve with extra barbecue sauce and sprinkled with fresh herbs.

Served with

  • salt potatoes
  • corn
  • grilled veggies
