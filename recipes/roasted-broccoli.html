Roasted Broccoli

Ingredients

  • 1 (1- to 1 1/2-lb) head broccoli, cut into 2-inch florets, stems reserved for vinaigrette
  • 2 Tbs extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 large garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • salt/pepper
  • BROCCOLI VINAIGRETTE
  • 1/3 cup plus 1 Tbs extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 cup chopped yellow onion (from 1 small onion) Subbed shallot / leek / 1 scallion
  • 1 cup peeled and thinly sliced broccoli stems
  • 1 large garlic clove, thinly sliced
  • 1 Tbs red wine vinegar
  • 1 Tbs water
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • Pinch of poppy seeds

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss together broccoli florets, olive oil, garlic, salt, and crushed red pepper on a large rimmed baking sheet; spread in a single layer. Roast in preheated oven until broccoli is tender and edges are browned, about 20 minutes.
  2. Make the broccoli vinaigrette
  3. While broccoli roasts, heat 1 Tbs oil in a medium skillet over medium. Add onion, and cook, stirring often, until onion is tender and beginning to brown, about 15 minutes. Stir in sliced broccoli stems and garlic, and cook, stirring occasionally, until broccoli is tender, about 8 minutes. Transfer stem mixture to a blender, and add vinegar, 1 Tbs water, Dijon, salt, pepper, and remaining 1/3 cup oil. Secure lid on blender, and remove center piece to allow steam to escape. Place a clean towel over opening. Process on high speed until smooth, about 1 minute.
  4. To serve, transfer vinaigrette to a serving platter, spreading around platter with a spoon. Top with roasted broccoli mixture, and sprinkle with poppy seeds.
