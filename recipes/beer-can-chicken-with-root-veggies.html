Beer Can Chicken with Root Veggies

Ingredients

  • Beer-Can Chicken & Roasted Root Veggies
  • 1 (4 to 5 lbs) roasting chicken
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced and some slices of peel
  • 1 can of beer
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • herbs - mix of fresh or dry - rosemary, thyme, sage
  • 1 head garlic, top cut off
  • 2 tablespoons (1/4 stick) butter, melted
  • 1 large yellow onion, cut into chunks
  • 3-4 carrots cut into chunks
  • 1-2 sweet potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1-2 medium potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1-2 beets, cut into chunks
  • 1 small celeriac, cut into chunks
  • 1 daikon radish, cut into chunks
  • 1 bulb of fennel, tops removed, and cut into wedges
  • olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • paprika

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F. And make sure the oven racks are in the lowest positions.
  2. Pour out about 1/2 of the beer. Add garlic, herbs, lemon peel, lemon juice to the can and place the can in the stand.
  3. Toss all of the veggies with oil, salt/pepper, paprika on parchment paper-lined sheet pan and place the beer can/stand in the center. Cut the top off the head of garlic and drizzle with olive oil. Place on the sheet-pan.
  4. Remove the chicken giblets. Rinse the chicken inside and out and pat dry. Liberally salt and pepper the inside of the chicken. Place the chicken on the beer can/stand. Brush the outside of the chicken with the butter and sprinkle again with salt and pepper. Tuck the wings behind the body.
  5. Roast the chicken for 1 to 1.5 hours (check at around 45 minutes), or until the juices run clear when you cut between a leg and thigh. Remove the chicken and vegetables to a platter and cover with aluminum foil for about 20 minutes. Slice the chicken onto a platter and serve it with the vegetables.

Served with

  • squash, biscuits or rolls
