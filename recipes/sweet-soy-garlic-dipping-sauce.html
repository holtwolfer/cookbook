Sweet Soy-Garlic Dipping Sauce

Ingredients

  • 5 mins
  • 5 mins
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 3 medium cloves garlic, grated on a microplane grater (about 3 teaspoons)
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced scallions
  • 1/4 cup roughly chopped cilantro
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame seed oil

Instructions

  1. Serves
  2. 8 servings
  3. Makes
  4. 1 cup
  5. Save
  6. Print
  7. Cook Mode (Keep screen awake)
  8. Whisk all ingredients together in a small bowl until sugar is dissolved. Serve with fried chicken.
