Marcella Hazan’s Tomato Sauce

OneNote Pasta section · Original page dated Tuesday, March 31, 2020

Tags

NYTMaracella HazanItalianpastasauceweeknighteasy

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • Our Version of Marcella Hazan’s Tomato Sauce
    • 2 x 28 oz canned tomatoes (whole and/or crushed)
    • 4-5 Tbs butter
    • 1/2 onion, peeled
    • 1-2 bay leaves
    • 1-2 parmesan rinds
    • salt to taste
    • 1 jar tomato paste
    • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
    • anchovies

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    Combine the tomatoes, their juices, the butter, onion half, bay leaves, and parmesan rinds in a Dutch oven. Add a pinch or two of salt. Rinse the tomato cans with ~1/4 cup of water and add to the sauce. Adjust the thickness with water as desired throughout cooking.

    Place over medium heat and bring to a simmer. Cook, uncovered, for about 45 minutes. Stir occasionally.

    Discard the onion, bay leaves and parmesan rinds before serving.
