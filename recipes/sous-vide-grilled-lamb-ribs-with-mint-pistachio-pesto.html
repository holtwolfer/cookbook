Sous Vide Grilled Lamb Ribs with Mint Pistachio Pesto

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Lamb:
  • 1 rack lamb ribs
  • salt, pepper
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  • fresh rosemary, minced (reserve the stems)
  • Sauce:
  • 1 cup packed fresh mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup shelled unsalted pistachios
  • 1/2 cup packed fresh parsley leaves
  • 1/2 cup sliced scallions
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • NOTES: subbed scapes for garlic, chives for scallions

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. In a food processor, pulse the mint, pistachios, parsley, scallions, lemon juice, and garlic until they form a paste. With the processor running, drizzle in 4 tablespoons olive oil. Continue to process until pesto is smooth. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  2. Lamb:
  3. Rub the ribs with salt, pepper, minced garlic, minced rosemary. Vacuum seal.
  4. Sous vide at 134 degrees for 18 hours (note: we did something like 4 hours and they were delicious, but a little tough)
  5. Heat the gas grill to super hot, with searing station on.  Get the smoke box going with wood chips, and if possible, some rosemary.
  6. Grill the ribs just long enough to brown on both sides, about 3-4 mins per side.

Served with

roasted potatoes
