Quick Gravy

OneNote Pork section · Original page dated Friday, September 20, 2019

Tags

mashed potatoes

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • 1 demiglace from world market + 1.5 cups water, heated in microwave (classic french demi glace)
    • 4 Tbs butter
    • 1.5 cups stock
    • 4 Tbs flour
    • salt/pepper

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    Melt butter in a sauce pan, add the flour and whisk constantly until lightly browned, about 2 minutes.

    Slowly add the broth, whisking constantly. Bring to a boil, season with salt and pepper.

    Add more stock if too thick.

    Original OneNote text (complete)
    1 demiglace from world market + 1.5 cups water, heated in microwave (classic french demi glace)
4 Tbs butter
1.5 cups stock
4 Tbs flour
salt/pepper
Notes: original recipe used 2.5 cups of stock, didn't thicken (maybe fake butter is the problem?)
Melt butter in a sauce pan, add the flour and whisk constantly until lightly browned, about 2 minutes.
Slowly add the broth, whisking constantly.
Bring to a boil, season with salt and pepper.
Add more stock if too thick.
Additions: onions/scallions/leeks/shallots - sautee in the butter and then add the flour
