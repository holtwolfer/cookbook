Linguine with White Clam Sauce

Ingredients

  • 6 Tbs extra virgin olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 anchovies, sliced
  • white wine
  • ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  • 1 lb linguine

Instructions

  1. check out recipe in the Ridge folder

Linguine with White Clam Sauce

  1. 36 littleneck clams, scrubbed

small container seafood stock

  1. ¼ tsp dry oregano

medium container chopped clams

  1. ¾ cup chopped fresh Italian parsley

cherry tomatoes

  1. 3 Tbs butter
  2. 1/2 a baguette, sliced down the middle, buttered
  3. Bring a large pot of water to boil for pasta.
  4. In a large straight-sided skillet (wide blue creuset pot), heat 6 Tbs olive oil over medium heat. Add garlic and anchovies and cook 1-2 minutes.
  5. Add the clams to the skillet, along with the red pepper and oregano. Add seafood stock and white wine. Bring to a simmer and cover until clams open, about 5 to 7 minutes. As the clams open, remove to a bowl.
  6. Meanwhile, add linguine to pasta water.
  7. Toast the baguette in the oven, finishing under the broiler for 2-3 minutes to toast.
  8. When all the clams are out, increase heat to high and add ½ cup of the parsley. Cook until reduced by half. Add cherry tomatoes and chopped clams with juices. Cook until tomatoes burst.
  9. Remove some of the cooking liquid and cream out with butter for a soppin' sauce.
  10. When the linguine is al dente and the sauce is reduced, add the pasta directly to the sauce and add the butter. Cook and toss until the pasta is coated with the sauce. Add more parsley and place clams in.

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • crusty bread
  • salad
