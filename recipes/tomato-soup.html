Tomato Soup

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs butter
  • 1 medium shallot, diced
  • 2 medium carrots, diced
  • 1-2 stalks of celery, diced
  • 2 Tbs flour
  • 1 (28-oz) can tomatoes
  • 5 Tbs stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/4 tsp ground celery seed
  • 1/4 cup 2% milk

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter and add the shallot, carrots, and celery. Cook until starting to softened but not browned.
  2. Add the flour and stir until very lightly browned.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes and juices, stock, sugar, salt/pepper, celery salt and bring just to a boil and then reduce heat to low.
  4. Simmer for about 30 minutes stirring often.
  5. Stir in the milk and remove from the heat. Use a hand blender to puree.
