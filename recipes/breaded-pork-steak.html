Breaded Pork Steak

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 2-3 pork steaks, deboned and trimmed
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 2 eggs
  • 1.5 cups bread crumbs
  • olive oil and/or ghee for frying

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Preheat oven to 350oF.

Place steaks in a ziploc bag and pound them out. Season with salt and pepper. Dredge in flour seasoned with paprika then whisked eggs, then bread crumbs. Heat enough olive oil to cover the bottom of a carbon steel pan. Brown the steaks on each side, transfer to a parchment-lined sheet pan (about 2 minutes per side).

Move steaks to the oven to finish cooking.
