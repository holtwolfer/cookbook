Brown Sugar Carnitas

Tags

PatiMexicantacosslow cookerpork

Served with

    Ingredients

    • 2 lb pork shoulder roast (or combo with country ribs), cut into 3 inch chunks
    • salt
    • 2 Tbs 1/4 cup water
    • 2 Tbs 1/4 cup orange juice
    • 2 Tbs 1/4 cup milk
    • 1/2 Tbs brown sugar
    • 1/4 white onion
    • red bell pepper
    • roasted red pepper
    • 1 garlic clove
    • 1 bay leaf
    • tortillas
    • pickled jalapenos or sweet peppers
    • guacamole
    • raw salsa verde
    • sour cream
    • tomatoes
    • beans
    • cilantro rice

    Instructions

    Season meat with salt and brown in slow cooker

    Puree the water, oj, milk, brown sugar, onion, garlic until smooth

    Reduce heat to low and add the pureed mix and bay leaves
