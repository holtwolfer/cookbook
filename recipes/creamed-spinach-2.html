Creamed Spinach

Ingredients

  • Creamed Spinach
  • 2 ½ lbs spinach in bulk, or two 10-ounce plastic wrapped bags
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 1 Tbs flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ tsp freshly grated nutmeg
  • salt/pepper
  • milk or cream?
  • If bulk spinach is used, pick it over to remove and discard any tough stems and blemished leaves. Rinse the spinach thoroughly and shake off excess water. There should be about 2 pounds cleaned weight, or 16 cups.

Instructions

  1. Bring enough water to a boil to cover the spinach when it is added and stirred down. Add spinach and cook 2 minutes and drain. Run under cold running water until chilled. Empty the spinach into a deep colander and press to extract most of the water.
  2. Put the spinach into the container of a food processor or an electric blender and blend thoroughly. There should be about 1 3/4 cups.
  3. Melt the butter in a saucepan and add the flour, stirring with a wire whisk. Add the milk, stirring rapidly with the whisk. Add nutmeg, salt and pepper to taste. Cook, stirring, about 5 minutes and add the spinach. Stir to blend. Heat and serve.
