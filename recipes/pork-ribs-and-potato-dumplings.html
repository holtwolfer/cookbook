Pork Ribs and Potato Dumplings

Tags

pork

Served with

    Related recipes

    • Sauerkraut and Ribs

    Ingredients

    • Dumplings
    • 3 lbs potatoes
    • 1/2 cup farina
    • 4 eggs
    • 2 tsp salt
    • 4 cup flour
    • Makes 28 dumplings.

    Instructions

    Boil potatoes until tender.  Peel and rice.  Allow to cool completely.  Add farina, egg, salt.  Blend well.  Add enough flour to make a medium stiff dough.  Roll into ~1.5" diameter balls.  Flatten like a pancake onto flour and roll into a smooth ball.  Drop into boiling water, reduce heat to low, and cook uncovered for 20 mins, turning the dumplings occasionally.  Remove from water.  Pour a little melted butter over the dumplings and serve with pork ribs and sauerkraut.
