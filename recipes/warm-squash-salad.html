Warm Squash Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 (1 1/2-pound) butternut squash, peeled and 3/4-inch) diced
  • olive oil
  • 1 Tbs pure maple syrup
  • salt/pepper
  • 3 Tbs dried cranberries
  • 3/4 cup apple cider or apple juice
  • 2 Tbs cider vinegar
  • 2 Tbs minced shallots
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 oz baby arugula, washed and spun dry
  • 1/2 cup walnuts halves, toasted
  • 3/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan
  • 3-4 slices proscuitto, chopped
  • (grilled chicken)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F.
  2. Place the butternut squash on a sheet pan lined with parchment. Add 2 tablespoons olive oil, the maple syrup, 1 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon pepper and use the parchment paper to toss. Roast the squash for 15 to 20 minutes, turning once, until tender. Add the cranberries to the pan for the last 5 minutes.
  3. While the squash is roasting, combine the apple cider, vinegar, and shallots in a small saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, until the cider is reduced to about 1/4 cup. Off the heat, whisk in the mustard, 1/2 cup olive oil, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon of pepper.
  4. Place the arugula in a large salad bowl and add the roasted squash mixture, the walnuts, and the grated Parmesan. Spoon just enough vinaigrette over the salad to moisten and toss well. Sprinkle with salt and pepper and serve immediately.

  7. 3 chicken breasts, butterflied or chicken tenders
  8. 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  9. 1/4 cup apple cider
  10. 1/4 cup olive oil
  11. 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  12. 1/2 Tbs dried sage
  13. salt/pepper
