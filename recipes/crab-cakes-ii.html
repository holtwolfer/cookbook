Crab Cakes II

Ingredients

Crab Cakes

  • 2 slices bread, toasted/dried out, grated/crumbled
  • 1 small sleeve ritz crackers
  • scallions
  • parsley (optional)
  • 1/2 stick butter
  • 2 Tbs flour

~ 1/4 cup milk

  • 1 Tbs lemon juice
  • mace

old bay

  • 1 lb crab meat + 2 5 oz cans lump

ghee

  • olive oil
  • toast the bread to dry out
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 3 Tbs Dijon- or Creole-style mustard
  • 2 Tbs cup white-wine vinegar
  • 2 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 cup grapeseed oil
  • (2 Tbs grated horseradish, fresh or prepared, did not use)
  • 1 clove garlic, rough chop
  • 3-4 scallions, rough chop
  • 2 stalks celery, rough chop
  • 1/2 bunch of parsley, rough chop
  • 2 Tbs ketchup
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce

Instructions

  1. melt the butter and whisk in the flour, followed by the milk to make a creamy sauce. Add lemon juice, mace, old bay, scallions/parsley. Add the 2 cans of lump. Grate the bread into the mixture and adjust with the crackers. Add the 1 lb crab and mix gently. Adjust again with crackers if needed to make firm cakes.
  2. Heat ghee and oil in a cast iron or carbon steel pan. Cook crab cakes about 4 mins per side until nicely browned and cooked through.

Rémoulade Sauce

  1. salt/pepper
  2. In small food processer, chop the garlic, scallions, celery, parsley. Add the egg yolk, mustard, vinegar, paprika, ketchup, worcestershire, salt/pepper and process until smooth. Slowly add the oil in a fine stream while mixing. Chill in refrigerator. It will thicken

Served with

  • fries
  • salt potatoes
  • roasted
  • remoulade sauce
  • asparagus
  • green beans
