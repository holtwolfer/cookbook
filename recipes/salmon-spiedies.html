Salmon Spiedies

Ingredients

  • salmon
  • 3 Tbs olive oil
  • 2 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1 Tbs rice vinegar
  • oregano
  • paprika
  • basil
  • garlic
  • onion powder
  • cherry tomatoes
  • red onion
  • red and green bell peppers
  • tzatziki

Instructions

  1. Marinate salmon for 1 hour and then grill on foil.

Notes

Notes: Needed more vinegar flavor. Try upping the vinegar without upping the marinating time
