  • 1 large yellow bell pepper, seeded, chopped

  • 1 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch (about 1/4 ounce; 7g)
  • 1 tablespoon (15ml) water
  • 2/3 cup (160ml) pineapple juice
  • 1/3 cup (80ml) rice vinegar
  • 1/3 cup (74g) light brown sugar

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a small sauce pan over medium heat and add garlic, cooking until just fragrant (1-2 min).  Add the sugar, vinegar, lime juice, ginger, (basil), salt, pepper and bring to a boil then reduce heat to low to simmer until syrupy (12-15 min).  Set aside and allow to cool.
  2. Cook the skewers over medium heat on the grill, brushing with the glaze.
  3. For the sauce:
  4. 1 cup peach preserves
  5. 1/4 cup hot Chinese mustard
  6. Mix together.  (From another Neely recipe)

Bell Pepper Rice Pilaf

  1. 1 Tbs soy sauce
  2. 1 Tbs lime juice
  3. Cook the rice.
  4. Heat oil in a sautee pan over medium heat and cook onion for 2-3 minutes, add the bell pepper, cook another 5 minutes or so until mostly cooked but still has some crunch, add the cumin, aleppo pepper, salt/pepper, brown sugar, balsamic vinegar and soy sauce. Cook for a few minutes and lower the heat to low for a few more to let it thicken a bit. Mix into the rice. Add lime juice.

Chinese Sweet & Sour Sauce

  1. 3 tablespoons (45ml) ketchup
  2. 1 tablespoon (15ml) soy sauce
  3. use apple sauce to thicken
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together cornstarch and water. Set aside.
  5. In a medium saucepan, combine pineapple juice, rice vinegar, brown sugar, ketchup, and soy sauce and bring to a boil over medium heat. Stir in cornstarch slurry and cook until thickened, about 1 to 2 minutes. Remove from heat and use immediately or store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
