Pumpkin Bread

Ingredients

  • Downeast Maine Pumpkin Bread
  • 1 (15-oz) can pumpkin puree
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • ⅔ cup water
  • 3 cups white sugar
  • 3 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  • ½ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp ground ginger

Instructions

  1. Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease and flour two 9x5-inch loaf pans.
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and ginger together in a large bowl.
  3. Mix pumpkin puree, eggs, oil, water, and sugar in a separate bowl until well blended.
  4. Stir flour mixture into pumpkin mixture until just blended.
  5. Pour batter into the prepared pans.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean, about 50 minutes.

Notes and variations

Cook’s Note

This pumpkin bread is also delicious with added nuts or chocolate chips. Fold in 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips or chopped toasted pecans, or ½ cup of each, before baking.
