Steak Salad

Ingredients

  • flank, skirt or flat iron steak
  • Marinade:
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp molasses
  • 2 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • salt/pepper
  • Salad:
  • lettuce mix
  • radishes, thinly sliced
  • cherry tomatoes
  • red onion, halved, thinly sliced
  • carrots
  • gorgonzola, crumbled
  • cucumber
  • Dressing:
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp balsamic glaze
  • dash soy or worcestershire

Instructions

  1. Make the marinade and marinate the beef in a ziploc bag for at least 30 minutes, up to 4 hours.
  2. If using tenderloin: sous vide at 124 for 45 minutes
