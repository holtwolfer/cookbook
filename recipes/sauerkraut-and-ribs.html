Sauerkraut and Ribs

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ribs
  • 2 lbs kraut
  • 1 tsp caraway seed
  • 1 medium onion,
  • 2 Tbs browned flour
  • 1/4 cup cold water

Instructions

Put ribs in a dutch oven with enough water to cover.  Simmer until meat is tender, 2-3 hours.  Add kraut, caraway seeds and onion.  Cook 25 mins until kraut is tender. Drain and reserve the liquid. Add 1-2 Tbs butter and 1/3 cup flour to the kraut. Add some liquid back to make a 'gravy'. Bring to a boil. There you go. Done.

Served with

  • brussel sprout hash
  • broccoli
