Marinade
chicken breasts cut into cubes
soy sauce
orange zest
orange juice
sesame oil
honey
rice vinegar
vegetable oil
garlic
white pepper
Sauce
2 Tbs dark soy sauce
4 Tbs Shaoxing wine (see note)
4 Tbs Chinese rice vinegar or distilled white vinegar
6 Tbs chicken stock
8 Tbs sugar
2 tsp roasted sesame seed oil
4 tsp grated zest and 1/4 cup juice from 1 orange
2 Tbs corn starch
8 (2-inch) strips dried orange peel
orange marmalade
4 tsp peanut, vegetable, or canola oil
4 tsp minced garlic (about 2 medium cloves)
4 tsp minced fresh ginger (about one 1-inch piece)
4 tsp thinly sliced scallion bottoms (about 1 scallion)
broccoli
Whisk together all the sauce ingredients.
Steam/cook broccoli in a wok with some orange juice and water, adding as needed.
Cook chicken in wok, adding some of the marinade.  Set aside.
Add the sauce to the wok and cook until thickened.
Add the chicken back to the sauce and cook a few minutes
Stir in the broccoli.
