Tomatillo - Avocado Salsa

Ingredients

  • 5-6 medium tomatillos
  • 2 large cloves garlic
  • 1 leek or scallions or onion
  • 2 avocados
  • cilantro
  • sugar
  • cumin
  • coriander
  • 1 Tbs lime juice
  • salt

Instructions

  1. roast the garlic and tomatillos on the grill until charred, let cool in food processor.
  2. Process with onions and then add avocado and remaining ingredients
