Beer-can Chicken

Ingredients

  • In the Can:
  • 1 clove garlic, sliced
  • lemon peel
  • lemon juice
  • rosemary, rough chop
  • mexican oregano
  • bay leaf
  • (fennel stalk)
  • Chicken:
  • 4 lb chicken
  • salt/pepper
  • sage

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375 or set up grill for indirect grilling.
  2. Rub chicken with salt, pepper and sage.
  3. Roast until the bird reaches 165, about an hour. Remove from oven and let rest 5-10 minutes tented with foil

Served with

  • potatoes
  • fennel
  • onion tossed with salt, pepper, paprika, olive oil and roasted with the chicken
  • Brussels sprout hash
  • rolls
  • biscuits
