Ingredients

Nut Crusted Salmon with Ginger Butter Sauce

  • Sauce:
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ½ cup white wine vinegar
  • ¼ cup chopped shallots
  • 6 black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 Tbs minced ginger
  • ½ cup whipping cream
  • 6 Tbs chilled butter, cut into ½ inch cubes
  • Fish:
  • 1/3 cup blanched slivered almonds
  • 1/3 cup chopped macadamia nuts
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 ½ tsp brown sugar
  • 1 ½ tsp honey
  • 1 tsp fresh lime juice
  • 4 6-7 oz salmon filets
  • An Ina Recipe
  • 1/2 pound French green lentils (lentilles du Puy)
  • 1/4 cup good olive oil, plus extra for salmon
  • 2 cups chopped yellow onions
  • 2 cups chopped leeks, white and light green parts only
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 3/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon minced fresh garlic
  • 1 1/2 cups chopped celery (4 stalks)
  • 1 1/2 cups Homemade Chicken Stock, recipe follows, or good canned broth

Instructions

  1. For the sauce:
  2. Boil first 6 ingredients in medium saucepan until almost all liquid evaporates, about 4 minutes.  Add cream, bring to boil.  Strain into small saucepan. bring sauce to simmer.  Remove from heat.  Add butter a few tablespoons at a time, whisking until melted and smooth.  Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  3. Fish:
  4. Preheat oven to 350.  Pulse first 6 ingredients in food processor until mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs.
  5. Heat large skillet over medium heat.  Sprinkle salmon with salt and pepper.  Cook until browned on 1 side, about 2 minutes.  Turn salmon over.  Top with nut mixture, pressing to adhere.  Bake until salmon is around 125, about 8 minutes?  IF mixture isn’t browned, place under broiler (be careful, it will brown quickly, under 2 minutes).  Meanwhile finish sauce.

Lentils

  1. 1 1/2 cups chopped carrots (3 carrots)

kale

  1. 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  2. 2 tablespoons good red wine vinegar
  3. Place the lentils in a heat-proof bowl and cover with boiling water. Set aside for 15 minutes, then drain.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a saute pan, add the onions, leeks, thyme, salt, and pepper and cook over medium heat for 10 minutes, until the onions are translucent. Add the garlic and cook for 2 more minutes. Add the drained lentils, celery, carrots, chicken stock, and tomato paste.
  5. Cover and simmer over low heat for 20 minutes (count on a little more time), until the lentils are tender. Add the vinegar and season, to taste.
