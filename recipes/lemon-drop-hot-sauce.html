Lemon Drop Hot Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbs vinegar
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a sauce pan, add the lemon drops, bell pepper, and garlic. Sautee for 4-5 minutes until tender. Add salt, water, sugar, bring to a simmer and cook until everything is soft enough to blend. Add the lemon juice, vinegar, season with salt/pepper and use the hand blender to blend into a smooth sauce.
  2. Note: can be thickened with xanthan gum, but was not required

Notes and variations

Lemon Drop Hot Sauce

grapeseed oil

lemon drop peppers, chopped
