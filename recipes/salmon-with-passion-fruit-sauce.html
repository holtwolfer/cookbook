Salmon with Passion Fruit Sauce

Tags

Colombiansalmontropical

Served with

  • coconut rice
  • steamed broccoli & carrots
  • brazibites
  • plantains

Ingredients

Passion Salmon

  • 1 1/2 lb salmon
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 stalk fresh lemon grass, minced and some big chunks
  • 28 oz passion fruit pulp
  • 2 Tbs lemon juice
  • cornstarch to thicken
  • Coconut Milk Rice
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Heat sugar, pulp, lemon grass and lemon juice in sauce pan, keep at a light simmer to thicken, 45 mins to an hour, adding sugar if needed. Thicken with cornstarch mixed with some of the mixture.
  2. I thought it was a little too tart.
  3. Could also add coconut milk, thicken with sour cream and/or yogurt.
  4. Grill salmon - with some sauce as glaze?
  5. 1 bay leaf
  6. In a medium pot, bring coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt to a boil. Add rice, stir, and cover. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes. Add lime zest and fluff with a fork.

Notes

Additions: zest from 1 lime or lime juice or cilantro

Coconut Milk Rice
1 can coconut milk
1 1/4 cup water
1 tsp sugar
1/4 tsp salt
1 1/2 cups jasmine rice
1 bay leaf
Additions: zest from 1 lime or lime juice or cilantro
In a medium pot, bring coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt to a boil. Add rice, stir, and cover. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes. Add lime zest and fluff with a fork.
