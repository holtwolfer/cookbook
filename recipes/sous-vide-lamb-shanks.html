Sous Vide Lamb Shanks

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Lamb Shanks
  • 4 lamb shanks
  • kosher salt
  • pepper
  • white pepper
  • florida pepper
  • rosemary

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Set sous vide temperature to 143 degrees.
  2. Rub the shanks with the salt/pepper mixture, place in vacuum bags, line with rosemary and vacuum seal.
  3. Sous vide for about 8 hours.
  4. Finish a hot grill.
  5. Chimichurri
  6. 1 bunch fresh parsley
  7. 2-3 medium cloves garlic, peeled
  8. 1 bunch mint leaves
  9. 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  10. 3 Tbs sherry vinegar
  11. salt/pepper
  12. 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  13. Place parsley, garlic, mint, vinegar, salt/pepper, and red pepper flakes in the food processor. Pulse until finely chopped, stopping to scrape down the sides of the bowl as necessary. With the processor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil.

Served with

roasted potatoes; asparagus hash; chimichurri

Tags

grill
