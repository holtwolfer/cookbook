Jambalaya

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 2 chicken breasts, cubed
  • 1 Tbs creole spice mix
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 1/2 pablano pepper, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 3-4 big stalks celery, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 jar tomato paste
  • 1 qt canned tomatoes
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 cup white wine
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 2 cups rice
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 pkg Andouille sausage, sliced (linguica is less spicy)

Instructions

  1. Rub the chicken with the creole spice mix.
  2. Heat oil in large dutch oven over medium heat. Sautee the onions, celery, green pepper, and half of the red pepper until soft, 8-10 minutes.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until just about cooked through and then add the sausage and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add the tomato paste and garlic. Stir and sautee for 1-2 minutes. Deglaze with the white wine and add the rice. Cook for 2-3 minutes. Add tomatoes, bay leaves, worcestershire, hot sauce, rest of the red bell pepper and stock. Bring to simmer and then put over low heat. Cook, uncovered, stirring frequently to avoid burning on the bottom, until liquid absorbed, rice is done, about 30 minutes.

Served with

Crystal hot sauce;
