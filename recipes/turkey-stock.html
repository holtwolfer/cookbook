Turkey Stock

Ingredients

  • 4 large turkey wings
  • turkey necks or backs
  • 4-5 celery stalks
  • 3 carrots
  • white wine
  • 1 leek
  • 1 onion
  • 20 peppercorns
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • parsley stalks
  • 1-2 scallions
  • (Started with a 19 lb turkey. Breast, thigh and most drumstick meat went into ground turkey. Rest of turkey was broken down and roasted for stock.)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400. Roughly chop 1-2 large carrots, 1 leek, 1/2 large onion, 2-3 stalks celery into baking dish (red oval). Place turkey wings on top. Season lightly with salt and then drizzle with olive oil. Roast until browned, turning every 30 mins.
  2. Moved to large stock pot, water added to cover meat. Other half of veggies added raw to the stock pot. Roasting pan was deglazed with white wine, added to stock. Peppercorns added.
  3. Canning:
  4. 20 mins @ 10 lbs
