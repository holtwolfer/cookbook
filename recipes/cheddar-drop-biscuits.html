Cheddar Drop Biscuits

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 6 tablespoons (3/4 stick) cold unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 6 oz Cheddar, coarsely grated (1 1/2 cups)
  • 3 scallions, finely chopped (used chives)
  • "buttermilk" 1 Tbs lemon juice, bring up to 1 cup with milk
  • butter
  • garlic powder
  • oregano
  • Preheat oven to 450°F.

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, baking powder, sugar, baking soda, and salt in a bowl, then blend in butter with your fingertips until mixture resembles coarse meal. Stir in Cheddar and scallions. Add buttermilk and stir until just combined.
  2. Drop dough in 12 equal mounds about 2 inches apart onto a buttered large baking sheet. Bake in middle of oven until golden, 18 to 20 minutes.
  3. Melt the butter with garlic powder and oregano and brush on top of the biscuits when done
