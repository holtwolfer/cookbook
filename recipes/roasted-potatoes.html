Roasted Potatoes

Ingredients

  • Basic Recipe for Roasted Potatoes
  • potatoes, chopped
  • onions, red or yellow
  • olive oil
  • paprika
  • salt, pepper

Instructions

  1. Put veggies on parchment paper on sheet pan. drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with seasonings. use parchment paper to toss the veggies around until coated.
  2. Variations:
  3. Veggies: carrots, fennel, peppers, root veggies
  4. Flavorings: oregano, sage, sumac, vinegars, lemon juice, garlic
  5. Mustard Roasted Potatoes
  6. 2 tablespoons whole-grain Dijon mustard
  7. 2 tablespoons honey
  8. 2 tablespoons orange juice
  9. 2 teaspoons orange zest
  10. 2 teaspoons finely chopped rosemary, plus 4 whole sprigs
  11. Preheat oven to 425oF.
  12. Mix 'sauce' ingredients together and toss with potatoes on parchment paper. Roast for about 45 minutes until the potatoes are lightly browned and tender.
  13. Green Goddess Sauce
  14. SAUCE:
  15. 1 packed cup parsley leaves
  16. 1 packed cup mixed herbs, such as chives, mint, dill, basil or cilantro, plus a few sprigs of dill, for garnish
  17. 4 anchovy fillets
  18. 1 to 2 garlic cloves
  19. 1/2 lemon, juiced
  20. ½ cup whole-milk yogurt (3/4)
  21. ½ cup mayonnaise (1/4)
  22. Sauce:
  23. While the fish cooks, combine the parsley, mixed herbs, anchovy, garlic, lemon zest, yogurt and mayonnaise in a blender and purée until thick and uniformly green. Taste and season with salt and pepper as needed.

Served with

salmon; roasted potatoes
