Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • (not yet tried)
  • 1 lb of pasta cooked in 3 qts water, reserve 1 cup

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Drain pasta, put back in dutch oven over low heat. Add 4 oz parm Reggiano grated on microplane and 5 Tbs unsalted butter cut into 5 pieces. Stir until incorporated. Remove from heat and cover for 1 minute and then stir a little more before serving in bowls warmed in a 200 degree oven.
