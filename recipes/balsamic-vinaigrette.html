Balsamic vinaigrette

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbs Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • salt/pepper
  • whisk/shake all ingredients together
