Three-Cup Chicken & Root Veggie Stir-Fry

OneNote Chicken section · Original page dated Tuesday, December 15, 2020

Tags

NYTAsianChinesefallwinterroot vegetables

Served with

  • rice

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 2 Tbs canola or other neutral oil
  • 6 cups root vegetables, such as carrots, parsnips, sweet potatoes and-or turnips, cut into 1/4-inch slices and halved or quartered if large (about 2 pounds)
  • 1-2 bell peppers, halved, sliced thinly
  • 2-3 scallions, sliced
  • 1 (1-inch) piece ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 10 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • ¾ cup Chinese rice wine
  • 6 Tbs low-sodium soy sauce
  • 3 Tbs toasted sesame oil
  • 2 Tbs dark brown sugar
  • 2 dried chiles, like chiles de árbol, or 1/4 teaspoon red-pepper flakes
  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves, torn if large

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. In a large skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add the vegetables, ginger and garlic, and cook, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are crisp-tender and browned in spots, 7 to 10 minutes.
  2. Lower the heat to medium and stir in the rice wine, soy sauce, sesame oil, brown sugar and red-pepper flakes. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are tender and the sauce has reduced, 10 to 15 minutes. Remove from heat, and stir in the basil.
  3. Heat a wok over high heat and add 2 tablespoons sesame oil. When the oil shimmers, add the ginger, garlic, scallions and peppers, and cook until fragrant, approximately 2 minutes.
  4. Scrape the aromatics to the sides of the wok, add remaining oil and allow to heat through. Add the chicken, and cook, stirring occasionally, until it is browned and crisping at the edges, approximately 5 to 7 minutes.
  5. Add sugar and stir to combine, then add the rice wine and soy sauce, and bring just to a boil. Lower the heat, then simmer until the sauce has reduced and started to thicken, approximately 15 minutes.
  6. Turn off the heat, add the basil and stir to combine. Serve with white rice.
