Afghani Pumpkin with Lamb

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Sauce:
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbs chopped fresh mint
  • salt
  • Pumpkin:
  • 1 pie pumpkin
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/4 cup canola oil
  • Lamb:
  • canola oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb ground lamb
  • 1-2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2-3 Tbs tomato paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • salt, pepper
  • (water if needed)
  • Yogurt Sauce:

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Mix all ingredients and refrigerate.
  2. Pumpkin:
  3. Preheat oven to 300 degrees.
  4. Scoop the seeds out of pumpkin, peel and cut into 3-4 inch wedges. Place wedges on sheet pan lined with parchment paper, rub with oil and then sugar. Bake for 3 to 3.5 hours brushing the pumpkins with the pan juices after about 2 to 2.5 hours.
  5. Lamb:
  6. Heat the oil in a skillet. Add the onion and cook until tender, 10 minutes. Add garlic and then the lamb. Cook until mostly done, a little browned and then add the tomatoes, paste, spices. Add water if needed (tomatoes should be enough). Cook until meat is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
  7. Orange-Spiced Whole Wheat Couscous
  8. 1 cup fresh orange juice
  9. 1-2 carrots, diced
  10. 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  11. 1/2 tsp cumin
  12. dash of red pepper flakes
  13. 1/2 tsp coriander
  14. 1/2 tsp sumac
  15. 1/4 cup craisins
  16. 1 cup couscous
  17. chopped fresh parsley
  18. scallions
  19. salt/pepper
  20. almonds or pecans?
  21. Combine the orange juice, cinnamon, cumin, red pepper, coriander, craisins, and water in a saucepan and cook over high heat until the mixture boils. Stir in the couscous, cover and remove from heat. Let sit for about 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and season with salt/pepper.
  22. Additions: onion/scallion/shallot/scapes, roasted red pepper, bell pepper, tomato, herbs

Served with

orange-spiced couscous (recipe below)
