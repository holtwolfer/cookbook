Basic Grilled Salmon

Ingredients

Rub

  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp aleppo pepper
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • allspice?

mace?

  • salt/pepper
  • salmon filets
  • olive oil
  • 1 lemon, sliced

Instructions

  1. Drizzle the salmon with olive oil. Rub all over with the rub mixture. Top with lemon slices and grill on foil over high heat with alder chips. Grill until internal temperature is around 120, remove and let rest for 5 minutes.

Served with

  • grilled veggies with mojo
  • bluephies mashed sweet potatoes
