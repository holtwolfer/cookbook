Lemon Chicken Marinade
3 chicken breasts
1/2 cup lemon juice (~2 lemons)
zest from 1 lemon
1/2 cup oil
salt/pepper
1 tsp oregano
1 Tbs honey
1/2 tsp galangal
Mix all ingredients together.  Marinate overnight.
