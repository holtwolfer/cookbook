Cedar Plank Grilled Salmon with Jalapeno-Apricot Sauce (Guy's Salmon)

Notes

Variations:

Ingredients

Salmon:

  • 1.5 - 2 lb salmon filet
  • rosemary sprigs
  • salt/pepper

Sauce:

  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1-2 jalapeños thinly sliced (can sub mild peppers)
  • 2-4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 Tbs paprika
  • 2 tsp sumac
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup white wine
  • 1/2 Tbs rice wine vinegar
  • 3 Tbs coarse ground mustard
  • 1 cup apricot preserves
  • 2/3 cup olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 Tbs butter

Instructions

For the Sauce:

  1. Heat oil in a sauce pan, add jalapeños and cook until caramelized. Add garlic and cook a few minutes and deglaze the pan with white wine before the garlic starts to brown. Add apricot preserves and mustard and simmer until sauce thickens, about 20 minutes.
  2. Thicken sauce with corn starch/water mixture
  3. cream out sauce with 1 Tbs butter

For the Salmon:

  1. Soak cedar plank in water for a few hours. Place salmon on plank, sprinkle with salt and pepper and chopped fresh rosemary.
  2. Coat with jalapeno apricot sauce.
  3. Set up grill with coals on one side. Cook salmon over hot coals in order to get the most smoked flavor. Move plank over to cool side if the plank starts to burn too much.

Served with

  • green beans with vinaigrette
  • mashed potatoes
  • grilled veggies
