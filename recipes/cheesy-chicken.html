Cheesy Chicken

Ingredients

  • Ranch sauce:
  • 1/3 cup sour cream
  • 2 Tbs buttermilk
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 tsp dill
  • 1/4 tsp parsley
  • 1/4 tsp spike
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Chicken:
  • 4 5-6 oz. chicken breasts, butterflied (or tenders)
  • salt/pepper
  • paprika
  • olive oil
  • Parmesan Crumb Topping:
  • 1/2 cup Panko bread crumbs
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • 1/3 cup shredded Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 cup shredded Provolone cheese / mozzarella
  • Season chicken with salt/pepper, paprika.

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a skillet and cook chicken 10 - 12 minutes, until chicken is nicely browned and cooked to 165° F.
  2. For the ranch sauce, mix all ingredients. Set aside.
  3. For Parmesan topping, mix panko, garlic salt, shredded Parmesan and butter.
  4. Position rack in center of the oven. Preheat broiler.
  5. Transfer cooked chicken to oven-safe casserole dish. Spread ranch spread on each breast, top with provolone cheese. Place under broiler until cheese melts, 2-3 minutes.
  6. Sprinkle on Parmesan topping, place under broiler until lightly browned, 3-5 minutes.

Served with

  • broccoli
  • baked potato
