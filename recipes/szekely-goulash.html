Székely Goulash

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 lbs pork shoulder, cut into 1" cubes (2 willow creek pork steaks worked perfectly)
  • 2 Tbs all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 1 27-oz can sauerkraut, drained (about 3 cups, firmly packed)
  • 1-1/2 cups  sour cream (1 normal size container)
  • rye bread for serving
  • Spatzle (or use egg noodles)
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup water
  • Slow Cooker version

Instructions

Place pork, flour, paprika, salt in gallon bag and shake until pork is coated.  We used 2 pork steaks.

Melt 2-3 Tbs butter in slow cooker on brown setting and sautee onions until softened.

Empty the pork into the slow cooker and brown the meat, stirring occasionally 10-15 mins.

Add 1/2 cup hot water.  Cover and set slow cooker to high. Simmer for 1.5 hours.

Fold in the kraut and cook for another hour. Let cool slightly and mix in sour cream.

Make spatzle and mix in.

Original recipe

Place the pork, flour, paprika, and salt in a large plastic bag. Seal, and shake until the pork is evenly coated. Set aside.

Place a 4-quart stock pot or Dutch oven with a tight-fitting lid over medium heat. Melt the lard and add the onion. Cook, stirring, until the onion is soft, about 7 minutes.

Empty the contents of the plastic bag to the pot. Brown the meat on all sides, turning occasionally, about 10–12 minutes. Because of the flour, the pieces of meat will stick together and to the pan. Add 1/2 cup hot water. Cover the pot, and lower the heat. Simmer for 1 hour, stirring occasionally. Add 1/4 cup of water every 15 minutes or so, as needed, just to keep the mixture from sticking to the pan. Stir every 15 minutes. The meat should turn a brownish-reddish color as it cooks, and the pieces should be cooking separately, as opposed to the big ball of 1-inch cubes that go into the pot.

Shortly before the end of the one-hour cooking period, the meat should be tender and cooked through. There should be some liquid left in the pot, but the meat should not be submerged. (It’s okay if there is too much water; that will boil away as you keep cooking.) Fold in the sauerkraut. (For a milder flavor, rinse the sauerkraut in cold water and drain again.)

After the pot has been simmering for 1 hour, add 2 cups of hot water. Bring the mixture to a boil, then cover the pot and lower the heat. Simmer for 2 hours longer, or until meat is tender when pierced with a fork. Remove the pot from the heat.

Place the sour cream in a mixing bowl. Slowly add 1–2 cups of the cooking liquid, whisking until smooth. Pour the sour-cream mixture into the pot, stirring constantly. Cook over low heat for 3–5 minutes until heated through—do not bring to a boil.

Serve the goulash in small bowls with rye bread on the side.

Served with

  • brussels sprouts
  • broccoli
  • egg noodles
