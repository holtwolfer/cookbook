Black Cod with Ginger-Soy Glaze

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbs honey
  • 1 Tbs agave nectar
  • 1 Tbs balsamic reduction
  • 3 Tbs low-sodium soy sauce
  • 3 Tbs rice wine vinegar
  • 3 Tbs mirin
  • 2 tsp sesame oil

Instructions

  1. make more sauce to serve on the side
  2. 1/2 Tbs ginger
  3. 2 scallions chopped
  4. Preheat oven to 350.
  5. Mix all marinade ingredients. Marinate the fish for about 30 minutes. Reserve marinade.
  6. Reduce marinade to a thick sauce over medium heat.

Served with

  • veggie fried rice
