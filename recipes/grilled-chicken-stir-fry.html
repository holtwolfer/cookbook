Grilled chicken stir-fry

Ingredients

  • chicken tenders
  • marinade
  • broccoli
  • carrots
  • green beans
  • bell pepper
  • bamboo shoots
  • 1 Tbs mirin
  • Sauce: 1/4 cup soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp ginger, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp sesame oil.
  • Thickener: 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water.
  • Coconut Milk Rice
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups jasmine rice
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Additions: zest from 1 lime or lime juice or cilantro

Instructions

  1. In a medium pot, bring coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt to a boil. Add rice, stir, and cover. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes. Add lime zest and fluff with a fork.
