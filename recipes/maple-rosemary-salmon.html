Maple-Rosemary Salmon

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Wednesday, December 2, 2020

Tags

grill

Served with

  • roasted potatoes
  • kale and roasted squash salad
  • sweet potatoes

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

cedar plank

  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1/3 cup pure maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup grainy Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 tsp dried rosemary powder
  • 1 Tbs chopped fresh rosemary
  • 1 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1-1/2 lb. skinless salmon fillet, pin bones removed, cut crosswise into strips

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Soak the cedar plank in water for at least an hour.
  2. Cure the salmon in lemon juice for 20-30 minutes.
  3. Prepare a medium-high (400 to 475°F) charcoal or gas grill fire.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk the syrup, mustard, rosemary, lemon juice, and soy sauce. Set aside half of the sauce. Pour half of the sauce over the salmon. Grill on the cedar plank with maple chips for smoke, until salmon reaches ~120 degrees.
  5. Drizzle with the sauce and serve.
