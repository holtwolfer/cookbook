Queso dip

Ingredients

  • 16 oz. velveeta, cut into 1/2 inch cubes
  • 1-2 roma tomatoes, seeded, chopped
  • 1 Tbs taco seasoning
  • 1 Tbs chopped chives or scallions

Instructions

  1. Melt cheese in small sauce pan over medium-low heat, mixing in the veggies.
