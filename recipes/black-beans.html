Black Beans

Ingredients

  • avocado oil
  • red onion, diced
  • jalapeno, diced
  • garlic, minced
  • cumin
  • Mexican oregano
  • bay leaf
  • lime
  • water or stock

Instructions

  1. Heat avocado oil in a small sauce pan. Add onion, jalapeno and sautee until tender. Add garlic, cumin, oregano, bay leaf, lime juice and cook 1 more minute. Add the beans and a little water or stock. Simmer until thickened.

  3. shredded cheese (oaxaca, pepper jack, queso fresco, cojita)
  4. sour cream
