Grilled Sunchokes

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs honey
  • 1 Tbs fresh lemon juice
  • 4 tsp olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 tsp sumac
  • fresh thyme
  • 1 lb sunchokes, cut into ½-inch-thick slices
  • 3 large shallots, peeled and cut into wedges ((did not use))

Instructions

  1. Mix all sauce ingredients together in a bowl and toss the sunchokes in it.
  2. Grill over medium heat, about 5 minutes per side. Toss with the marinade.
