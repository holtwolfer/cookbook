Pork Chops with Maple Glaze

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup dark maple syrup
  • 2 tsp Dijon-style mustard
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp finely minced or rasped shallot
  • 2-3 thick-cut (1&1/2" inch thick) pork chops, with fat layer intact
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs butter for cooking
  • 1 Tbs cold butter for sauce

Instructions

  1. In small bowl, combine maple syrup, mustard, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce and shallot. Set aside.
  2. Pat chops dry and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat a thick, heavy-bottomed skillet. Add 2 Tbs of butter. Sear pork chops over high heat until a light gold to medium brown crust forms, about 5 minutes per side. Remove chops from pan.
  4. Lower heat to medium. Melt remaining 1 Tbs butter in pan. Pour a third of the sauce mixture into the pan and place chops in it. Allow glaze to caramelize onto bottom of chops for a couple of minutes. Pour remaining glaze on top of chops, turn tops face down and repeat caramelization. If your glaze starts to scorch, reduce heat and swirl in a teaspoon or two of water into glaze.
  5. Remove chops from pan and rest meat on warm platter loosely covered with foil for 5 to 10 minutes. Internal temperature should be 135-140.
  6. Meanwhile, remove pan from heat and swirl in cold butter to emulsify glaze into a sauce. Keep sauce in warm pan until ready to serve.

Served with

  • roasted potatoes
  • asparagus
  • mashed potatoes
  • peas
  • green beans
  • sprouts
