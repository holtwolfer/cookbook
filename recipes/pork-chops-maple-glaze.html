Main dish

Pork Chops with Maple Glaze

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, combine maple syrup, mustard, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce and shallot. Set aside.
  2. Pat chops dry and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat a thick, heavy-bottomed skillet. Add 2 Tbsp butter. Sear pork chops over high heat until a light gold to medium brown crust forms, about 5 minutes per side. Remove chops from pan.
  4. Lower heat to medium. Melt remaining 1 Tbsp butter in pan. Pour a third of the sauce mixture into the pan and place chops in it. Allow glaze to caramelize onto bottom of chops for a couple of minutes. Pour remaining glaze on top of chops, turn tops face down and repeat caramelization. If your glaze starts to scorch, reduce heat and swirl in a teaspoon or two of water.
  5. Remove chops from pan and rest meat on a warm platter loosely covered with foil for 5 to 10 minutes. Check doneness with a food thermometer.
  6. Meanwhile, remove pan from heat and swirl in cold butter to emulsify glaze into a sauce. Keep sauce in warm pan until ready to serve.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup dark maple syrup
  • 2 tsp Dijon-style mustard
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp finely minced or rasped shallot
  • 2–3 thick-cut (1 1/2 inch thick) pork chops, with fat layer intact
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 Tbsp butter for cooking
  • 1 Tbsp cold butter for sauce
