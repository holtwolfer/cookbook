Lamb & Eggplant Curry

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lamb stew meat
  • 1 Tbs ? curry powder
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp aleppo pepper
  • 1/2 large white onion, coarsely chopped
  • 2 scallions, coarsely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ inch fresh ginger, chopped
  • 4 whole allspice berries
  • 2 thyme sprigs, leaves stripped
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 4 Tbs ghee
  • 1 ½ cups diced potato
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • 1 large eggplant, cubed
  • 1 bell pepper, large chop
  • cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 to 2 small Scotch bonnet peppers, seeded and chopped
  • Mango chutney or mango pickle, for serving
  • Fresh cilantro leaves, for serving
  • chick peas
  • coconut rice

Instructions

  1. Pat lamb dry with paper towels and toss with curry powder, salt/pepper, ground ginger, and aleppo pepper.
  2. Combine onion, scallion, garlic, fresh ginger, allspice, thyme leaves and oil in food processor; purée until smooth. Scrape mixture over lamb and toss to combine. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight.
  3. Heat 2 Tbs ghee in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Stir in 2 tsp curry powder and heat until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Working in batches to avoid overcrowding the pot, brown the meat on all sides.
  4. Add enough water to just cover meat. Bring liquid to a simmer, covered, then uncover the pot and cook gently for 45 minutes.
  5. Stir potato, carrot and pepper into pot. Simmer until vegetables are fork tender and meat is cooked through, about 30 to 45 minutes longer.
  6. Using a slotted spoon, transfer meat and vegetables to a bowl. Simmer cooking liquid until it has reduced and thickened to a saucy consistency (to taste), about 15 minutes. Taste sauce and add more salt if needed. Pour sauce over meat. Serve over rice.

Served with

naan; rice
