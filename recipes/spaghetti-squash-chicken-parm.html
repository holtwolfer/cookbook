Spaghetti Squash Chicken Parm

Ingredients

  • SQUASH
  • 2 spaghetti squashes, halved lengthwise and seeded
  • 3 Tbs olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • CHICKEN
  • 1 pkg chicken tenderloins
  • 1 cup panko
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 25 grams salt and vinegar potato chips, not kettle cooked or thick cut (like 2 big handfuls)
  • 3 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • 1.5 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 2/3 cup flour
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 4 Tbs ghee
  • 2 Tbs olive oil
  • SAUCE
  • 2 x 28 oz canned tomatoes (whole and/or crushed)
  • 4-5 Tbs butter
  • 1/2 onion, peeled
  • 1-2 bay leaves
  • 1-2 parmesan rinds
  • salt to taste
  • freshly grated Parmesan
  • fresh mozzarella pearls
  • fontina, grated
  • fresh basil, dried oregano to garnish
  • Squash:

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450 degrees. Brush the cut side of each squash half with 1 tablespoon olive oil, then season with salt and pepper. Place squash, cut-sides down, on a parchment-lined baking sheet and roast until the squash is tender when poked with a fork, 35 to 40 minutes.
  2. Combine the tomatoes, their juices, the butter, onion half, bay leaves, and parmesan rinds in a Dutch oven. Add a pinch or two of salt. Rinse the tomato cans with ~1/4 cup of water and add to the sauce. Adjust the thickness with water as desired throughout cooking.
  3. Place over medium heat and bring to a simmer. Cook, uncovered, for about 45 minutes. Stir occasionally.
  4. Discard the onion, bay leaves and parmesan rinds before serving.
  5. Combine the breadcrumbs, potato chips, oregano, basil, garlic powder, parmesan in a gallon zip-top bag and crush into a course meal.
  6. Set up breading station with a piece of parchment for the uncooked and one for cooked chicken.
  7. Unwrap the chicken and blot well with paper towels then dredge the chicken first in the flour (shake off the excess), then dip in the beaten eggs (again, drain the excess), and finally coat with the breadcrumbs before placing on a piece of parchment paper.
  8. Position an oven rack in the top third of the oven and heat to 375.
  9. Melt 2 Tbs of the ghee with 1 Tbs of the olive oil in a sauté pan over medium-high heat. When the butter foams, add the breaded chicken to the pan, but don’t overcrowd. Cook until crisp and golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer to a second piece of parchment, wipe out the pan and repeat with the remaining butter, oil and chicken.
  10. Flip the squash and use a fork to scrape the squash into spaghetti strands. Top with sauce, chicken, more sauce, mozzarella, fontina. Roast until the top is golden brown and mozzarella has melted, 15-20 minutes. (can also speed this up using the broiler)
  11. Top with dried oregano, fresh basil
