Sean Brock’s BBQ Sauce

Ingredients

  • 30 minutes
  • 1⁄2 cup pork or chicken stock
  • 2 1⁄2 cups cider vinegar
  • 3⁄4 cup ketchup
  • 2 1⁄2 tbsp. light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp. fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp. smoked paprika
  • 3⁄4 tsp. kosher salt
  • 1⁄2 tsp. freshly ground black pepper
  • 1⁄2 tsp. garlic powder
  • 1⁄2 tsp. onion powder
  • 1⁄4 tsp. chile powder

Instructions

  1. NOTES: was way too vinegary
  2. Heat all ingredients in a 4-qt. saucepan over medium-high; simmer, stirring occasionally, until reduced by 2⁄3, about 30 minutes. Sauce will keep, covered, in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
