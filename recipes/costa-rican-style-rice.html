Costa Rican-Style Rice

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1.5 cups water and/or stock
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 tsp annatto powder
  • 2 tsp salsa lizano
  • bay leaf

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in the pot, add annatto powder and salsa. Stir briefly until sizzling.
  2. Stir in rice to coat.
  3. Add water or stock and the bay leaf.

Served with

  • pollo asado
  • steaks w/ chimichurri
