Smashed Potatoes

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • potatoes
  • Minimalist Sauce:
  • olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • (add other spices or herbs such as paprika, spike, sumac, thyme, oregano)
  • Salt & Vinegar:
  • malt vinegar drizzled on before and after cooking
  • Parmesan, herb:
  • drizzle/brush with olive oil or butter. sprinkle with some grated parmesan cheese and some chopped herbs like chives, thyme, parsley.
  • Butter & herb:

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Add sour cream and chives and cheddar or other baked potato toppings to make them like potato skins
  2. Preheat oven to 425.
  3. Parboil the potatoes until just tender when stabbed with a fork.
  4. Remove from water to a lined baking sheet and smash them down flat with a potato masher. Let them cool/dry.
  5. Drizzle or brush them with a 'sauce', preferably on both sides. Bake for 20-30 minutes until they are lightly browned and have a little crisp to them.
