The Last Word
¾ oz gin
¾ oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)
¾ oz green Chartreuse (sub Dolin Genepy Le Chamois Liqueur)
¾ oz lime juice
Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
