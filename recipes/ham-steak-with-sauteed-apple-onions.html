Ham Steak with Sauteed Apple & Onions

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs unsalted butter two 1/2-pound ham steaks 2  apples—peeled, cored and thinly sliced 1/2 medium Vidalia or other sweet onion, thinly sliced 1 tsp sugar 1 Tbs coarse ground mustard 1 rosemary sprig OR dry sage salt/pepper 1/4 cup calvados 1 cup apple juice (preferably cider) 1 Tbs cider vinegar 1-2 Tbs heavy cream

Instructions

In large cast iron skillet, melt the butter giving it enough time to warm up enough to brown the ham. Add the ham and cook over moderate heat until browned on both sides, about 5 minutes. Transfer the ham to a plate and keep warm. Cover with foil to keep from drying out.

Add a little more butter if needed and then onions.  Stir to coat. Cook 2-3 minutes, sprinkle sugar on, cook a few more minutes to start to caramelize.

Add the apple, rosemary or sage to the skillet, season with salt and pepper and cook over moderately high heat, stirring occasionally, until golden, about 2 minutes.

Add the calvados and cook until evaporated, about 1 minute. Add the apple juice, and some coarse ground mustard, and cook over moderate heat until the apple is tender, about 2 minutes.

Return the ham steak and cook, turning, until the pan juices are thickened, about 5 minutes longer.

Add a little butter or spoon over juices to keep ham from drying out.

Remove the ham and cover to keep warm. Stir in 1-2 Tbs of heavy cream to the sauce and cook 1-2 minutes to blend flavors.

Serve the ham steak with the pan sauce.

Served with

  • roasted sweet potatoes, roasted potatoes, mashed squash, brussels sprout hash, corn, rolls, biscuits
