Grilled Skirt Steak

Ingredients

  • 1.5 - 2 lbs skirt steaks (or flat iron / flank / hanger)
  • marinade:
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • olive oil
  • balsamic vinegar
  • soy sauce
  • salt/pepper
  • paprika

Instructions

  1. Mix all marinade ingredients in a gallon bag and marinate the steak for 30-35 minutes.
  2. Heat grill to medium high heat or charcoal with hot and cool sides.
  3. rare: 125-130ºF
  4. medium rare: 130-135ºF

Served with

  • rice
  • roasted potatoes
  • zucchini + peppers
