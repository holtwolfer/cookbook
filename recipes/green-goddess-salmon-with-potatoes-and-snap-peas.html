Green Goddess Salmon With Potatoes and Snap Peas

Ingredients

  • Green Goddess Salmon with Potatoes & Snap Peas
  • 1 ½ lbs small new or baby potatoes, halved or quartered if large
  • 4 Tbs olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 small bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 (1 1/2-lb) piece skin-on center-cut salmon
  • 3-4 sprigs of dill
  • 1 lemon, sliced, deseeded
  • SAUCE:
  • 1 packed cup parsley leaves
  • 1 packed cup mixed herbs, such as chives, mint, dill, basil or cilantro, plus a few sprigs of dill, for garnish
  • 4 anchovy fillets
  • 1 to 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • ½ cup whole-milk yogurt (3/4)
  • ½ cup mayonnaise (1/4)
  • 8 oz snap peas, trimmed and halved on the bias (about 2 cups)
  • 4 small cucumbers, halved lengthwise and cut on the bias into 1-inch pieces (about 1 ¼ pounds)

Instructions

  1. Preheat grill on medium. Use alder chips to smoke.
  2. Potatoes/veggies:
  3. Toss potatoes with salt/pepper, paprika, olive oil in a large bowl. Cook potatoes for 45 minutes in grill basket over medium heat, lowering if they start to burn. Add bell pepper with 5-10 minutes left and then snap peas with 5 minutes left. Add cucumbers to the empty bowl. When grilled veggies are done, add to bowl. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and add juice from 1/2 lemon. Toss to coat.
  4. Sauce:
  5. While the fish cooks, combine the parsley, mixed herbs, anchovy, garlic, lemon zest, yogurt and mayonnaise in a blender and purée until thick and uniformly green. Taste and season with salt and pepper as needed.
  6. Salmon:
  7. Season the salmon with salt/pepper, paprika, drizzle with olive oil, place sprigs of dill on top and cover with slices of lemon. Grill on foil over medium heat with alder chips to smoke, about 20 minutes, until temperature reads around 125. Remove, cover and let rest.
