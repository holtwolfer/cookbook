Turkey - Dry-Brined Apple-Garlic Turkey

Ingredients

  • 1 turkey (14 to 16 pounds)
  • kosher salt
  • 1 Tbs black pepper
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 pkgs of poultry herbs (sage, rosemary, thyme)
  • 1 bunch of parsley, stems roughly trimmed
  • 6 garlic cloves, peeled and halved
  • 1 head of garlic, top sliced off, drizzled with olive oil
  • 3-5 apples, quartered
  • 1-2 large onions, quartered
  • 1 small celeriac, cut into 1 inch chunks
  • 1 bottle hard apple cider (750 ml)
  • dry white wine, as needed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 3-4 Tbs melted butter

Instructions

Dry Brining:

  1. Remove any giblets from the cavity and reserve for stock or gravy. Pat turkey and turkey neck dry with paper towel; rub turkey all over with 1/2 teaspoon salt per pound of turkey, the pepper and the lemon zest, including the neck. Transfer to a 2-gallon (or larger) resealable plastic bag. Tuck herbs and 6 garlic cloves inside bag. Seal and refrigerate on a small rimmed baking sheet (or wrapped in another bag) for at least 1 day and up to 3 days, turning the bird over every day (or after 12 hours if brining for only 1 day).

Preparing the Turkey:

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees.
  2. Remove turkey from fridge, pat dry with paper towels.
  3. Work the skin away from the breast and then rub underneath with butter (2-3 Tbs)
  4. Stuff the cavity with about 3/4 of the herbs, apple and onion quarters, and some of the celeriac. Place remaining herbs, apples, onions, celeriac in the roasting pan. Add garlic cloves, bay leaves, and neck/heart/liver to the pan.
  5. Place the whole head of garlic in between the legs and then truss the legs to close up the cavity. Fold the wings back.
  6. In the bottom of a large roasting pan, add the cider and enough wine to fill the pan to a 1/4-inch depth.
  7. Brush the turkey skin generously with 3-4 Tbs melted butter.
  8. Put turkey into the oven and roast 30 minutes. (if using convection rotate after 30 mins)
  9. Cover breast with aluminum foil. Reduce oven temperature to 350 degrees and continue roasting until an instant-read thermometer inserted in the thickest part of a thigh reaches a temperature of 165 degrees, about 1 1/2 to 2 hours more. 14.5 lb turkey started at 8:50, done around 12:30
  10. Transfer turkey to a cutting board to rest for 30 minutes before carving.
  11. Around 3.5 hours cooking time then resting.
