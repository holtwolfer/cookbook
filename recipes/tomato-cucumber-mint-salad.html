Tomato, Cucumber & Mint Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 large English cucumbers
  • 1/3 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 Tbs sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 large tomatoes, seeded, chopped
  • 2/3 cup red onion, chopped
  • ½ cup fresh mint, chopped
  • 3 Tbs olive oil

Instructions

  1. Cut cucumbers in half, lengthwise and deseed.  Cut halves into ½ inch slices on the bias, place in large bowl.  Add the vinegar, sugar and salt.  Let stand at room temperature for 1 hour, stirring occasionally.  Add tomatoes, red onion, mint and oil.  Toss to blend.  Season with salt and pepper.
