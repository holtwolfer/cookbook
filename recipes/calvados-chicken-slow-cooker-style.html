Calvados Chicken - Slow-cooker style

Tags

slow cookereasy, weeknight

Ingredients

  • Slow Cooker Calvados Chicken with Potatoes
  • chicken:
  • olive oil
  • 3 chicken breasts / thighs
  • 1 white onion cut into big chunks
  • 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1-2 tsp dry sage
  • 1/2 tsp dry mustard
  • sauce:
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock
  • 1/3 cup apple cider
  • 1/2 cup calvados
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • potatoes (about salt tato size)
  • apple slices

Instructions

  1. Add olive oil to crockpot on brown setting, add chicken and onions, brown.
  2. Add garlic, sage, mustard cook 1 min
  3. Add all sauce ingredients except cream. (may need to adjust amounts…?)
  4. Cook a while on low.
  5. Add potatoes (and apple slices) with 1 hour to go.
  6. Remove chicken, potatoes, apples, add cream to sauce.
  7. 2 apples, thinly sliced
  8. brussel sprouts, halved
  9. onion, cut into chunks
  10. herbs... sage or rosemary or thyme
  11. onions, cut into chunks
  12. Preheat oven to 375.  Brussels sprouts and apple slices placed in red oval baking dish, sprouts to one side, apples and onions on the other.  Chicken placed on apples, seasoned with salt, pepper, paprika.  Sprouts seasoned with salt and pepper.   Everything drizzled with olive oil and then apple cider and stock added to bottom.
  13. Tatos and onions placed on baking sheet lined with parchment.  Season with salt and pepper, paprika, mustard powder.  Add a few dollops of dijon and drizzle with olive oil.  Toss together using the parchment paper until coated.
  14. Halve the squash, scoop out seeds, add a dollop of butter to each half.
  15. when chicken is done remove chicken and sprouts.  Pour all of some (to taste) of the liquid in the dish into a sauce pan, add calvados and more stock if needed and bring to a boil and allow to reduce a little and burn off the alcohol.  Off the heat stir in a little butter and then mix in half and half.
  16. pearl or cipollini onions, whole
  17. roast the chicken breasts and onions with a little oil in a dutch oven, uncovered
  18. 1/2 cup chicken stock
  19. reduce to 1/3 of a cup
  20. add 3/4 cup half and half
  21. potatoes, cut into pieces
  22. sweet potatoes, whole (they were very small)
  23. beets, cut into pieces
  24. leeks, sliced lengthwise
  25. oven at 375. garlic wrapped in foil, all other veggies except kale oiled and placed on a cookie sheet lined with parchment paper. cook ~45 minutes adding the kale for the last 10 minutes. Chop up veggies and mash in the roasted garlic.
  26. used blue paella like dutch oven for 3 hens.  Put tatoes, carrots, celery root, small onions (quartered) around.  stirred up veggies with salt, pepper, paprika, olive oil (also on hens), added some chicken stock and roasted at 400 degrees for a little over an hour.  Should have flipped birds halfway through.
  27. Took veggies and birds out of pan, heated pan on stove top and added pan drippings, apple juice and calvados, reduced, stirred in some skim milk.  (should be half and half or cream)
