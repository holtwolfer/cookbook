Zucchini Fritatta

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 2 small zucchinis, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 small red onion, chopped
  • (other veggies)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • salt/pepper
  • paprika
  • 8 eggs, beaten
  • cheese, grated

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees.
  2. Heat oil in cast iron skillet. Sautee veggies until softened, add garlic and seasonings.
  3. Add eggs and cheese and make sure the eggs are evenly distributed.
  4. Cook until just set and then transfer to oven to finish cooking for 5-10 minutes until cooked through.
