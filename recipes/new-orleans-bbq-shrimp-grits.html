New Orleans BBQ Shrimp & Grits

Ingredients

New Orleans BBQ Shrimp

  • 1-2 lbs extra-large shrimp (21-25 per pound, see note)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (substitute paprika)
  • 2 Tbs oil
  • 6 Tbs butter, cut into 6 pieces
  • 2 tsp flour
  • 2 Tbs tomato paste (tomato sauce also works)
  • 1 tsp minced fresh rosemary
  • 1 tsp minced fresh thyme
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3/4 cup bottled clam juice
  • 1/2 cup beer
  • 1 Tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • Andouille or other sausage
  • 1/2 cup fresh or frozen corn kernels
  • 2 cups water (fixed)
  • 4 Tbs butter
  • 4 scallions, white parts minced, green parts sliced thin
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp hot sauce
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 cup old-fashioned grits
  • 4 oz Monterey Jack cheese, shredded (1 cup)

Instructions

  1. Sear shrimp: Pat shrimp dry with paper towels and sprinkle with salt and paprika. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in large skillet over medium-high heat until just smoking. Cook shrimp, without moving, until spotty brown on one side, about 1 minute; transfer to large plate.
  2. Make Sauce: Melt 1 tablespoon butter in empty skillet over medium heat. Add flour, tomato paste, rosemary, thyme, oregano, and garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in clam juice, beer, and Worcestershire, scraping up any browned bits, and bring to boil. Simmer to reduce, about 5 minutes. Return shrimp and any accumulated juices to skillet. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until shrimp are cooked through, about 2 minutes. Off heat, stir in remaining butter until incorporated. Serve.

Creamy Cheesy Grits

  1. 4 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded (1 cup)
  2. Puree corn and 1/4 cup water in blender until smooth, about 1 minute; set aside.
  3. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in medium saucepan over medium heat. Add scallion whites and cook until softened, about 2 minutes.
  4. Stir in 2 cups water, milk, hot sauce, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper and bring to boil.
  5. Slowly whisk grits into saucepan until no lumps remain. Reduce heat to low and cook, stirring frequently, until thick and creamy, about 15 minutes.
  6. Off heat, stir in Monterey Jack, cheddar, pureed corn, and remaining 2 tablespoons butter until incorporated. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve sprinkled with scallion greens.
