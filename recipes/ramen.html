Ramen

Ingredients

  • ginger
  • garlic
  • scallions

Instructions

  1. Cut pork into very thin strips and marinate. Stir-fry in a wok over high heat.
  2. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3. Soft-boiled eggs
  4. Bring water to a gentle simmer. Add eggs straight from fridge. Keep at a simmer for 6 mins.
  5. Marinating the soft-boiled eggs:
  6. Place peeled whole eggs in a mixture of soy sauce and mirin
  7. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  8. Shoyu broth
  9. For the Tare:
  10. 1/4 oz kombu (7 g; about 1 (7- by 2-inch) piece)
  11. 1 oz (30 g) shaved katsuobushi flakes
  12. 1/4 cup dry sake
  13. 1/4 cup soy sauce (koikuchi)
  14. ginger
  15. **Soak kombu in water for 8 to 24 hr**
  16. For the Tare: Transfer kombu and ~1 cup (maybe a little more?) soaking water to a medium saucepan and bring to a bare simmer over medium-high heat. Remove from heat and add katsuobushi flakes. Let steep 3 minutes.
  17. Strain into a measuring cup through a funnel lined with a coffee filter. (This is the dashi)
  18. In a saucepan, combine dashi with sake and soy sauce and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to maintain a steady simmer and cook until reduced by half.
  19. Broth:
  20. Bring chicken/turkey broth to a boil. Remove from heat. Add kombu, ginger, and garlic and let steep for 45 minutes.
  21. Bring stock back to a simmer and add veggies. Cook until just tender.
  22. Serve by starting with ~1 Tbs of tare or more to taste and then adding noodles and broth with veggies.
  23. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  24. Seaweed
  25. Place wakame and hijiki in separate small bowls. Cover with boiling water and let rest until rehydrated and tender, about 5 minutes. Drain and press with paper towels. Roughly chop wakame. Add wakame and hijiki to a large bowl.
  26. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  27. Noodles
  28. Boil in the broth according to package.
  29. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  30. Beef
  31. sirloin, flank, skirt
  32. soy sauce
  33. mirin
  34. oil
  35. balsamic vinegar
  36. scallions
  37. garlic
  38. Cut the steak into thin strips. Combine all marinade ingredients and marinate the steak for 20 minutes. Cook by adding to the boiling broth.
  39. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  40. Menma
  41. 1 lb bamboo shoots
  42. 3 cups water
  43. 1 oz katsuobushi (bonito flakes)
  44. 1/4 oz konbu
  45. 1/2 cup sake
  46. 3 Tbs soy sauce
  47. 1-1/2 Tbs sugar
  48. Combine water katsuobushi, and konbu in a bowl and soak for 1 hour.
  49. Strain the water into a sauce pan. Add sake, soy sauce, and mirin. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook, uncovered, for 10 minutes. Stir in the sugar until dissolved and simmer 10 more minutes.
  50. Refrigerate in cooking liquid until ready to use.

Notes and variations

Ramen is a collection of broth, tare, protein, vegetable, and topping preparations, not a single standardized recipe.
