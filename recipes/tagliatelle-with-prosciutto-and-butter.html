Tagliatelle With Prosciutto and Butter

Ingredients

  • 8 Tbs unsalted butter
  • 2.5 oz prosciutto, torn into bite-size pieces
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 lb handmade fresh tagliatelle or store-bought tagliatelle
  • 3/4 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more for garnish, if desired
  • Optional veggies:
  • olive oil
  • cherry tomatoes
  • spinach/greens
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

Bring a large pot of water to a boil over high heat.

In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium-high heat until frothy and golden, about 1 minute.

Add half the prosciutto in one flat layer. Cook until crisp, 1 to 2 minutes, then transfer cooked prosciutto to a paper towel-lined plate.

If adding veggies: add a little olive oil to the pan and cook the spinach and cherry tomatoes until just cooked and tomatoes just start to burst, season with salt/pepper. Set aside in a bowl to serve.

Repeat with remaining prosciutto, leaving it in the skillet, and remove skillet from heat.

Cook the pasta.

Just before your pasta is ready, return the skillet to the heat and warm over medium. Do not drain the pasta, but use a slotted pasta fork or tongs and transfer the cooked pasta directly to the skillet.

Working quickly, add Parmigiano-Reggiano and about 1/4 cup of the pasta cooking water and swirl vigorously to emulsify, jostling the pan at the same time, and cook just until sauce is silky, about 1 minute.

Divide the pasta among shallow bowls, sprinkle with pepper and remaining prosciutto and serve immediately, along with more Parmigiano-Reggiano if desired.

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • added veggies
