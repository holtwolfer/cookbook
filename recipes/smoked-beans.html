Smoked Beans

Ingredients

    Instructions

    1. Smoked Beans
    2. 1 can baked beans
    3. 1 can black beans, not drained
    4. 1 red bell pepper, chopped
    5. 1 green bell pepper, chopped
    6. 1 medium red onion, chopped
    7. 1 Tbs paprika
    8. 1 Tbs brown sugar
    9. 1 Tbs bourbon
    10. 1 small frozen bunch collards, chopped
    11. salt/pepper
    12. Mixed all ingredients in a square foil pan, put on the bottom rack in the smoker at 12:00.
