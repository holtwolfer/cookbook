Honey-Mustard Almond and Panko Crusted Salmon

Ingredients

  • 1-2 salmon filets
  • salt/pepper
  • 2 Tbs honey
  • 2 Tbs Dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbs mayo
  • 2 Tbs sour cream
  • 1/2 Tbs paprika
  • 1/4 cup almonds or pecans, fine chop
  • 1/2 cup panko
  • 1-2 Tbs olive oil
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbs Dijon mustard

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450.
  2. Place salmon filets on a sheet pan and season with salt and pepper. Mix honey, mustard, mayo, sour cream, paprika together to make the sauce. Mix olive oil into the nut and panko mix until coated.
  3. Spread sauce over the salmon filets and top with the nut/panko mix.
  4. Roast for about 20 minutes until the salmon is around 120 degrees.
  5. Serve with: roasted potatoes, carrots, peppers, onions; side salad with balsamic vinaigrette;

  7. potatoes, cut into chunks
  8. carrots
  9. onions
