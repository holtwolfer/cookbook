Ratatouille

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant
  • 1 zucchini
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 small red onion
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, unpeeled
  • olive oil
  • salt/pepper
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 3-4 tomatoes, chopped
  • parsley
  • basil
  • goat cheese
  • chick peas
  • sausage
  • balsamic vinegar

Instructions

  1. Roast the red peppers and garlic. Chop eggplant, zucchini, onion and toss with salt/pepper and olive oil. Grill in a grilling basket. Grill sausage.
  2. Served with couscous and "mix-ins".

Tags

vegetarian; grill; summer; veggies

Notes and variations

Optional sausage is listed among mix-ins; omit for a vegetarian preparation.
