Corned Beef

Tags

St. Patrick's DayIrishSmarchboiled stuff

Served with

  • soda bread
  • fries

Ingredients

  • Corned Beef & Cabbage
  • Beef:
  • 2-3 lb corned beef
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp all spice
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 1 tsp coriander seeds
  • 1 medium carrot, cut into chunks
  • 2-3 celery stalks, cut into chunks
  • 1-2 leeks, cut into chunks
  • chunks of celery root if you have it
  • 1 can guiness
  • chicken or beef stock and water to cover
  • Veggies:
  • 3-4 carrots, cut into chunks
  • salt potatoes
  • onion, cut into chunks
  • 3-4 celery stalks, sliced
  • cabbage, cut into chunks
  • celery root (optional)
  • Rinse off beef, put in slow cooker with spices and carrot, leeks, celery. Add guiness and then just cover with stock and/or water.  Bring to a boil and then put on low setting.  Cook for 4-5 hours on low.

Instructions

  1. Add veggies and turn to brown to heat back up, switch to low and cook until veggies are tender, about 45 minutes.
