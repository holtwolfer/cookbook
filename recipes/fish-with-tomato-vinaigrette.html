Fish with Tomato Vinaigrette

Tags

fishwhite fishhalibutblack basscod

Served with

  • roasted potatoes
  • couscous
  • mint peas

Ingredients

  • Poached Fish Fillets with Sherry-Tomato Vinaigrette
  • Fish
  • 4 (6-oz) skinless white fish fillets, 1 inch thick
  • salt/pepper
  • 4 oz marinated artichoke hearts

Vinaigrette

  • 4 oz cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 small shallot, peeled
  • 4 tsp sherry vinegar
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 Tbs chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/2 cup olive oil (ideally reserved from cooked fish)
  • 2 oz cherry tomatoes, cut into 1/8-inch-thick rounds

Instructions

  1. FOR THE VINAIGRETTE: Process whole cherry tomatoes, shallot, vinegar, 3/4 teaspoon salt, and pepper with reserved 1/2 cup fish cooking oil or 1/2 cup olive oil if making ahead in blender until smooth, 1 to 2 minutes. Add any accumulated fish juice from platter, season with salt to taste, and blend for 10 seconds
  2. For the fish:
  3. Pat the fish dry with paper towels and season with salt, pepper, and sumac.
  4. Heat carbon steel pan over medium high heat. Sautee artichokes until lightly browned. Remove, keep warm.
  5. Wipe out pan. Heat 2 Tbs butter in the pan. Sautee the fish until lightly browned on both sides and 120-125 degrees, about 4 minutes per side. Add whole cherry tomatoes to the pan with about 2 minutes of cooking time left and continue to cook after the fish is removed if necessary. Tomatoes should just blister and burst.

Sous vide

  1. Set sous vider to 132oF
  2. Season the fish with salt and pepper. Drop into ziploc bag and place thyme sprigs on either side. Seal and cook for 45 minutes. Remove from bag, discard thyme sprigs and dredge in flour.
  3. Original Recipe - poaching the fish
  4. FOR THE FISH: Adjust oven racks to middle and lower-middle positions and heat oven to 250 degrees. Pat fish dry with paper towels and season each fillet with 1/4 teaspoon salt. Let sit at room temperature for 20 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, toss artichokes and cornstarch in bowl to coat. Heat 1/2 cup oil in 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Shake excess cornstarch from artichokes and add to skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until crisp and golden, 2 to 4 minutes. Add garlic and continue to cook until garlic is golden, 30 to 60 seconds. Strain oil through fine-mesh strainer into bowl. Transfer artichokes and garlic to ovenproof paper towel–lined plate and season with salt. Do not wash strainer.
  6. Return strained oil to skillet and add remaining ¼ cup oil. Place onion half in center of pan. Let oil cool until it registers about 180 degrees, 5 to 8 minutes. Arrange fish fillets, skinned side up, around onion (oil should come roughly halfway up fillets). Spoon a little oil over each fillet, cover skillet, transfer to middle rack, and cook for 15 minutes.
  7. Remove skillet from oven. Using 2 spatulas, carefully flip fillets. Cover skillet, return to middle rack, and place plate with artichokes and garlic on lower-middle rack. Continue to cook fish until it registers 130 to 135 degrees, 9 to 14 minutes longer. Gently transfer fish to serving platter, reserving 1/2 cup oil, and tent fish loosely with aluminum foil. Turn off oven, leaving plate of artichokes in oven.

Notes

Notes: if grilling, artichokes can also be cooked in foil pack or on cast iron on the grill
