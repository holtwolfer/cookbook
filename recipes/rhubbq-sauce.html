RhuBBQ Sauce

Ingredients

  • 5 stalks rhubarb, chopped
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 cup sugar (1/2 cup + date syrup + honey + balsamic glaze)
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1-2 tsp crushed red chilies, or to taste
  • 1 + 1/2 tsp ground mustard
  • paprika
  • spike
  • sumac
  • 1 tsp coarse salt

Instructions

  1. Roughly chop the rhubarb, onion, and bell pepper.  Combine all sauce ingredients in a pot and heat until boiling, cover and reduce to a low simmer for 30-45 minutes.
  2. Using an immersion blender puree sauce until smooth and thick.  If sauce remains too thin after pureeing, return to heat and cook until sauce reaches desired consistency.
