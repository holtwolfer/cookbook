Fennel Citrus Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs fennel bulbs
  • 1/4 cup plus 2 Tbs olive oil
  • 1/4 cup freshly squeezed oj
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan

Instructions

  1. Cut the fronds from the fennel and reserve some of the leaves for garnish.
  2. Cut in half, remove core, slice 1/4 inch slices
  3. Heat 2 Tbs oil in a large sauce pan.  Add the fennel and saute for 2 to 3 mins over medium heat until translucent and slightly cooked but still al dente.  Transfer to a bowl.
  4. Whisk the orange juice, salt, pepper in a small bowl.  While whisking, slowly add the olive oil.  Pour over fennel and add the cheese and fennel leaves.
