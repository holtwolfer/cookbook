Belmont Jewel Cocktail
No Belmont party kicks off without the Belmont Jewel, a refreshing bourbon cocktail that’s as much a tradition as the race’s 1.25-mile distance (shortened from 1.5 miles for 2025 due to Saratoga’s track). This recipe, published in 2011 and verified via the NYRA’s 2025 site, is simple and crowd-pleasing.
Ingredients (makes 6 drinks):
9 oz Woodford Reserve bourbon (or any bourbon)
12 oz lemonade
6 oz pomegranate juice
ice
lemon wedges or maraschino cherries for garnish
Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
Add 1.5 oz bourbon, 2 oz lemonade, and 1 oz pomegranate juice per drink.
Shake well and strain into a rocks glass filled with ice.
Garnish with a lemon wedge or cherry.
Repeat for each serving or scale up in a pitcher for self-service.
