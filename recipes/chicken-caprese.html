Chicken Caprese

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (6 to 8 ounces each)
  • salt/pepper
  • 8 oz mozzarella, cut into 1/4-inch slices
  • 6 oz tomatoes, cut into 1/2-inch slices
  • 1 ½ cups loosely packed fresh basil leaves
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • Pesto, store-bought or homemade, for serving
  • (Stuff with basil leaves or pesto)
  • Pat chicken dry. Cut a lengthwise pocket into the thick side of each breast without cutting all the way through to the other side. Season breasts inside and out using 3/4 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
  • Stuff each breast with a quarter of the mozzarella, 2 to 3 tomato slices (depending on size) and 4 to 5 basil leaves. Enclose the filling as much as possible by pulling the chicken over the filling. (The chicken can be prepared to this point, covered and refrigerated up to 24 hours.)

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large 12-inch nonstick pan over medium heat. Sauté garlic, stirring, until golden, about 4 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, scoop out garlic and transfer to a paper towel-lined plate, leaving the oil in the pan.
  2. Increase heat to medium-high and sauté chicken until golden on the bottom, about 6 minutes. If the breasts darken too quickly, lower heat. Flip and cook for 5 minutes, then cover and cook 2 to 3 minutes more, or until chicken is cooked through and filling is hot.
  3. Using a spatula, carefully transfer chicken to a platter or four plates. Pour pan juices over the chicken. Garnish with pesto, reserved garlic and remaining basil leaves.
  4. 2 cups fresh basil leaves (no stems)
  5. 2 Tbs pine nuts or walnuts
  6. 2 large cloves garlic
  7. ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  8. ½ cup freshly grated parmesan cheese
  9. Combine basil leaves, pine nuts or walnuts and garlic in a food processor and process until very finely minced.
  10. With the machine running slowly dribble in the oil and process until the mixture is smooth.
  11. Add the cheese and process very briefly, just long enough to combine. Store in refrigerator or freezer.

Served with

  • veggie rice
