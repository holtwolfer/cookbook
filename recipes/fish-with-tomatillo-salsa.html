Fish with Tomatillo Salsa

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Wednesday, October 13, 2021

Tags

whitefish

Served with

  • tortilla chips

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • fish - halibut, yellow-eye, cod, salmon
  • salt/pepper

Salsa Verde

  • 3 lbs tomatillos
  • 2 large jalapenos
  • 5 small garlic cloves
  • 1 ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 ½ bunches fresh cilantro
  • ¾ cup loosely packed Italian parsley
  • 1/3 cup packed fresh mint leaves

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Simmer tomatillos and peppers in boiling water until soft, about 15 minutes. Coarsely chop with garlic and cumin in a food processor. Add next 3 ingredients. Blend until herbs are chopped and salsa is chunky.
  2. Makes 4 cups.
  3. Fish:
  4. season with salt/pepper. Grill or pan-fry.
