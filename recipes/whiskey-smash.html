Directions
In a shaker, add the lemon wedges and simple syrup. Muddle for 5–10 seconds, ensuring each wedge is pressed.
Add the bourbon, simple syrup, and mint leaves, and fill the shaker with ice. Shake hard for an additional 12–15 seconds.
Double strain through a fine mesh strainer into a rocks glass filled with fresh ice. Garnish with a mint sprig.
