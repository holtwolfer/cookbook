Spring Green Risotto

Ingredients

  • 6 cups chicken or vegetable stock (4)
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 bunch ramps, sliced thinly (4 ramps)
  • 2 cups arborio rice (1.5)
  • 1 ½ cups white wine (3/4)
  • ¼ cup parsley, chopped finely
  • 2 cups saute mix, sliced into very thin strips
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 cup grated parmesan style cheese, or goat cheese?
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Puree mixed greens, lemon juice
  2. Heat the broth in a stockpot until it begins to steam. In a deep sided pot or thick bottomed Dutch oven, heat the butter, olive oil, and ramps. When the ramps are soft and translucent add the Arborio rice and saute 2-3 minutes.
  3. Add the white wine to the rice and stir until almost all of the wine is absorbed. Begin to add small ladles of the hot broth to the rice, stirring occasionally. The rice should be at a steady but not vigorous boil.
  4. Add more broth when each grain of rice is just coated with liquid. You don’t need to stir constantly but be attentive and don’t let the rice scorch.
  5. After about 15 minutes taste the rice for doneness, it should be tender but not mushy, and most of the broth will be gone. If you run out of broth simply add water until done. Stir in the parsley and sauté mix and cook for one minute more.
  6. Add the Parmesan, stir once and serve in a deep bowl.

Notes and variations
