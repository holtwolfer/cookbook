Halibut Caddy Ganty

Ingredients

  • 1 lb halibut
  • 1 cup white wine
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs (probably more)
  • 1/2 cup minced onions
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • salt, pepper

Instructions

  1. Marinate halibut in the wine for 2-3 hrs
  2. Preheat oven to 400
  3. Remove halibut from marinade, pat dry with paper towels.  Season halibut with salt, pepper (added paprika)
  4. Coat halibut with bread crumbs, place on baking sheet w/ parchment.
  5. Mix sour cream, mayo, mustard, onion, and chopped dill and coat the fish with mixture.
  6. Bake about 20 minutes, finish under broiler to brown a little. Final temp should be around 130. Garnish with dill.

Served with

  • salt potatoes
  • corn
  • roasted green beans tossed in oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, pepper
  • peas
  • rice & mixed steamed veggies
