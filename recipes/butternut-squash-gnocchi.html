Butternut Squash Gnocchi

OneNote Pasta section · Original page dated Thursday, December 10, 2020

Tags

LidiaItalianpasta

Served with

  • butter and sage sauce
  • tomato sauce
  • red bell pepper and tomato sauce
  • garlic bread

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 1 lb butternut squash (about half a medium squash)
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 2 medium russet potatoes (about 12 oz)
  • 1/4 cup grated Grana Padano
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more as needed

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Roast the squash:

Preheat oven to 400 degrees. Scoop seeds from squash half, drizzle with oil and roast, cut-side down in a baking dish until tender, about 45 mins to 1 hr.

Let the squash cool and then scoop the flesh out. Set the squash in a cheesecloth and let hang or place in strainer in the fridge overnight. You should have about 3/4 cup.

Cook the potatoes until tender. Cool and peel. Rice and let cool completely. You should have about 2 cups.

In a large bowl, combine the squash, potatoes, grated cheese, eggs, salt, and nutmeg and mix until smooth. Sprinkle in 1 1/4 cups flour and mix to combine. Dump the dough onto a work surface, and knead until it comes together. If the dough is still sticky, add the remaining 1/4 cup flour, and knead just until smooth. Do not over-knead the dough.

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Divide dough into 8 equal pieces. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment and dust lightly with flour. Roll each piece of dough into a 1/2 thick rope. Cut cross-wise into 3/4 inch pieces. One piece at a time roll gnocchi along the back of a fork with tines dipped in flour, making ridges on 1 side and a dimple on the other. Transfer to floured baking sheet.

Cook the gnocchi in 2 batches in the boiling water, giving them just a couple minutes more after they float to the surface. Remove with a spider and transfer to the sauce.
