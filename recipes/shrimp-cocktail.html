Shrimp Cocktail

From the OneNote Appetizers section · Original page dated Saturday, December 20, 2025

Review before cooking or publishing:
  • Original page contains Cocktail Sauce, not instructions for preparing shrimp.

Ingredients

  • Cocktail Sauce
  • 1 cup
  • ⅔ cup ketchup
  • 3 Tbs prepared horseradish, store-bought or homemade
  • 1 Tbs fresh lemon juice, plus more to taste
  • 1 Tbs chili sauce
  • ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • ¼ tsp hot sauce
  • dash worcestershire sauce

Instructions

  1. Mix together all the ingredients in a small bowl until well blended. Taste and add more lemon juice or hot sauce as needed. Split into 2 batches and add more horseradish to one. Cocktail sauce will keep in the refrigerator in a covered container for at least 2 weeks.

Shrimp Cocktail
Saturday, December 20, 2025
8:49 AM
Cocktail Sauce
Yield:
1 cup
⅔ cup ketchup
3 Tbs prepared horseradish, store-bought or homemade
1 Tbs fresh lemon juice, plus more to taste
1 Tbs chili sauce
¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
¼ tsp hot sauce
dash worcestershire sauce
Mix together all the ingredients in a small bowl until well blended. Taste and add more lemon juice or hot sauce as needed. Split into 2 batches and add more horseradish to one. Cocktail sauce will keep in the refrigerator in a covered container for at least 2 weeks.
