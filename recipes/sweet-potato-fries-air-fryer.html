Sweet Potato Fries (Air Fryer)

Ingredients

  • Air Fryer Sweet Potato Fries
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, cut into thin strips
  • 2 Tbs canola oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • (cayenne pepper)

Instructions

  1. Preheat air fryer to 400.
  2. Toss fries with all ingredients to coat. Fry at 400 for 10-12 minutes.

Served with

burgers
