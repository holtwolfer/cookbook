Grilled Steaks with Chimichurri, Rice & Veggies

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    1. Season steaks with salt and pepper and vacuum seal with fresh herbs. Sous-vide at 129 for 1 hour.
    2. Could also add some skewered shrimp
    3. Veggies
    4. zucchini
    5. bell pepper
    6. green beans
    7. red onion
    8. cherry tomatoes
    9. Skewer cherry tomatoes or grill as is if on the vine.
    10. Toss other veggies with olive oil, sherry vinegar, salt, pepper, paprika and grill in basket.
