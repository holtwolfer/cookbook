Grilled Steaks with Chimichurri, Rice & Veggies

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Monday, March 9, 2026

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

Steak

  • rib eyes, flank, skirt, flat iron

Chimichurri

  • 1 bunch cilantro, rough chop
  • 1 bunch parsley, rough chop
  • 2-3 scallions, rough chop
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, rough chop
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp Aleppo pepper
  • salt/pepper
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 3 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1 Tbs sherry vinegar
  • 1 tsp agave nectar
  • 1 tsp Lizano salsa
  • 1/4 cup olive oil

Grilled Veggies

  • zucchini
  • bell pepper
  • green beans
  • red onion
  • cherry tomatoes
  • * Notes: could add skewered shrimp *

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Steaks

  1. Season steaks with salt and pepper and vacuum seal with fresh herbs. Sous-vide at 129 for 1 hour.
  2. Finish over super high heat, rotating 45 degrees for grill marks and about 4 minutes per side.

Chimichurri

  1. Combine everything but olive oil in food processor and chop. While chopping slowly add the olive oil.

Veggies

  1. Skewer cherry tomatoes or grill as is if on the vine.
  2. Toss other veggies with olive oil, sherry vinegar, salt, pepper, paprika and grill in basket.

Served with

  • Costa Rican-style rice
  • brazi bites
