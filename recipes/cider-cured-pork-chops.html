Cider-Cured Pork Chops

Ingredients

  • 4 Tbs kosher salt
  • 1 Tbs freshly ground black pepper
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 cups apple cider, plus more as needed
  • 3 1 1/2-inch-thick center-cut or loin-cut pork chops, bone in
  • To make the brine, dissolve the salt in 4 cups of warm water in a large bowl. Add the pepper, pour in the apple cider and mix. Drop the pork chops into the brine and add enough additional apple cider so that the chops lurk in the liquid like frogs in a pond. Cover and refrigerate for at least 8 hours and up to 72.

Instructions

Heat grill for super hot direct grilling in the middle. Add apple or cherry woodchips to the smoker box. Remove the chops and grill for 1-2 minutes per side. Cover with foil and let rest for about 10 minutes.

Served with

  • apples
  • cheesy mashed potatoes
  • green beans
