Sweet Red Bell Pepper Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 red bell peppers
  • 2-3 medium paste tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 Tbs tomato paste
  • salt, pepper

Instructions

  1. heat olive oil in dutch oven over medium heat.  Add onion and sautee 4-5 minutes then add red bell pepper.  Continue to cook until peppers are softened.  Add garlic, cook 2 minutes.  Add tomato sauce rinsing cans with a little water and adding to the pot, bay leaves, lower heat to low and simmer

Notes and variations
