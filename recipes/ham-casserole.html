Ham Casserole

Tags

Easterham

Served with

  • a smile

Ingredients

  • CONSIDER GOING BACK TO THE ORIGINAL RECIPE NEXT TIME… THIS ONE MAY BE TOO MUCH
  • 1/2 cup green pepper, chopped
  • 1/2 cup onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup pimento (or roasted red pepper)
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 16 oz. mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 can cream of mushroom soup (condensed)
  • 4 cups cooked rice (2 cups uncooked before cooking)
  • 4 cups ham, cubed
  • 2 cups grated cheddar cheese

Instructions

Sautee green pepper, onion, mushrooms and pimento in 1 Tbs butter.  Combine all ingredients and bake in a covered casserole for 30 minutes at 350 degrees.

Original Recipe Ham Casserole

¼ cup green pepper, chopped

¼ cup onion, chopped

¼ cup pimento (or roasted red pepper)

8 oz. mushrooms, sliced

1 can cream of mushroom soup (condensed)

2 cups cooked rice (1 cup uncooked before cooking)

2 cups ham, cubed

1 cup grated cheddar cheese

Sautee green pepper, onion and pimento in 1 Tbsp butter.  Combine all ingredients and bake in a covered casserole for 30 minutes at 350 degrees.
