Stir-Fried Citrus-Glazed Chicken

Ingredients

  • Sous Vide Grilled Citrus Chicken
  • Stif-fry version - serve with rice, thinner sauce, thinly slice chicken and fry with oil, soy sauce, mirin, sesame oil, rice vinegar
  • Chicken:
  • 3 chicken breasts, thinly sliced into small pieces
  • Glaze:
  • 1/2 Tbs olive oil
  • 1-2 Tbs minced shallots
  • 1 tsp minced ginger
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 cup orange marmalade
  • 1-2 Tbs cup sweet chili sauce (Mae Ploy)
  • 1 tablespoon white balsamic vinegar or white wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup orange juice
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1 Tbs cornstarch, mixed in 2 Tbs warm water
  • Chicken:
  • sautee in oil, add other ingredients, simmer
  • Glaze / sauce:

Instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, over medium-high heat, add the olive oil. When the oil is hot add the shallots and ginger and saute for 3 minutes. Add the remaining ingredients and reduce heat to a low simmer until thickened. Adjust the consistency with the cornstarch/water.

Served with

  • roasted potatoes
  • roasted parsnips and carrots
