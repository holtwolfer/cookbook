Preakness

Pit Beef

Pit Beef
FOR THE RUB:
2 Tbs kosher salt
1 Tbs sweet paprika
1 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp dried oregano
½ teaspoon black pepper
FOR THE SANDWICH:
1 3-lb piece top round or round steak roast
8 kaiser rolls 
 Prepared horseradish or Horseradish sauce
1 sweet white onion, sliced thin
2 ripe tomatoes, sliced thin
lettuce
Combine ingredients for the rub in a bowl, and mix. Sprinkle 3 to 4 tablespoons all over the beef, patting it in. Place in a baking dish, and cover with plastic wrap. You can cover the beef with the rub for a few hours, but for maximum flavor, leave it for 3 days in the refrigerator, turning once a day.
Prepare a hot grill. Grill beef 30 to 40 minutes, or until outside is crusty and dark brown and internal temperature is about 120 degrees (for rare). Turn beef often. Transfer to a cutting board; let it rest 5 minutes.
Slice beef thinly across grain. Pile beef high on a roll or bread slathered with horseradish sauce. Garnish with onions, tomatoes and sliced lettuce. Serve.
Notes: make sure it is thawed to be rubbed ahead of time.  Added wood chips and covered on weber grill to get smoky flavor, but finished open over full heat to brown/form crust.
Served with: salt potatoes; fries; black-eyed susans; 
Tags: Baltimore; BBQ, barbecue; grill; Raichlen; Preakness;

Belmont Jewel Cocktail
No Belmont party kicks off without the Belmont Jewel, a refreshing bourbon cocktail that’s as much a tradition as the race’s 1.25-mile distance (shortened from 1.5 miles for 2025 due to Saratoga’s track). This recipe, published in 2011 and verified via the NYRA’s 2025 site, is simple and crowd-pleasing.
Ingredients (makes 6 drinks):
9 oz Woodford Reserve bourbon (or any bourbon)
12 oz lemonade
6 oz pomegranate juice
ice
lemon wedges or maraschino cherries for garnish
Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
Add 1.5 oz bourbon, 2 oz lemonade, and 1 oz pomegranate juice per drink.
Shake well and strain into a rocks glass filled with ice.
Garnish with a lemon wedge or cherry.
Repeat for each serving or scale up in a pitcher for self-service.
