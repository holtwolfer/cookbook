Chili

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb pound beef/lamb/bison/shortrib, cubed
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2-3 bell peppers, chopped
  • 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jam jar tomato paste
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 Tbs cocoa
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp aleppo pepper
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 1/4 tsp allspice
  • 1 tsp spike
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 3 1/2 tablespoons chili sauce
  • 1 Tbs balsamic reduction
  • 1/2 lb dried beans soaked overnight
  • 1 qt canned tomatoes
  • 1 can Guiness
  • 1 cup coffee
  • 1 Tbs worcestershire
  • 1 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 Tbs peanut butter
  • 1/4-1 cup stock to adjust thickness

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Heat oil in slow cooker on brown setting. Add the cubed beef and brown a little before adding the ground beef.
  2. Brown and then add onions. Continue to cook until meat is nicely browned.
  3. Clear a spot in the middle and add the spices, toasting for 1-2 minutes and stirring in.
  4. Clear a spot again and add the tomato paste, allow to caramelize, about 2-3 minutes.
  5. Add the garlic, stir to mix well, 1-2 minutes.
  6. Add brown sugar and chili sauce, mixing well, cook 1-2 minutes, then add the beans.
  7. Add tomatoes, beer, coffee, broth, vinegar, worcestershire, bay leaves and beans.
  8. Reduce heat to high setting and simmer for 4 hours.
