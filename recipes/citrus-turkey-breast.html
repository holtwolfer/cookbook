Citrus Turkey Breast

Ingredients

  • Brine:
  • 1 lemon, quartered, seeded
  • 1 lime, quartered, seeded
  • 1 orange, quartered, seeded
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • fennel rub
  • 4 Tbs olive oil
  • 1/4 - 1/2 cup water
  • Injector Sauce:
  • chicken stock
  • butter
  • citrus juice
  • calvados
  • fennel rub

Instructions

  1. Process the citrus, sugar, rub. Drizzle in oil and then add water to make a thick, but pourable paste. Brine the turkey for 24 hours
  2. Remove turkey and inject with sauce, angling the needle in the same hole to avoid making too many holes in the breast, let rest 2-3 hours before smoking.
  3. Smoking time should be 2-3 hours. After 1 hour baste with olive oil or a spray.
