    Original OneNote text (complete)
    Barbacoa
2 lb chuck roast
2 medium onions, diced
8 cloves garlic, minced
1 Tbs taco seasoning
1 1/2 Tbs Mexican oregano
1 (0.36 oz) can chipotle in adobo
1 cup beef broth
1 cup water
1 bay leaf
2 Tbs white vinegar
Place all ingredients except chuck roast in a blender. Blend until smooth.
Add a third of the blended sauce to slow cooker. Place chuck roast in slow cooker and pour remaining sauce over.
Cook 8-10 hours on low. Once cooked, remove bay leaf then shred meat in cooking liquid.
