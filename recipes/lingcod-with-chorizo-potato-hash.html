Lingcod with Chorizo Potato Hash

Ingredients

  • 2 (8 oz) lingcod fillets
  • 3 Tbs olive oil
  • 8 oz fresh chorizo or linguica, casings removed
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 lb potatoes, cut in ½ inch dice
  • 8 oz sweet potatoes, peeled and cut in ½ inch dice
  • paprika
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat and oven to 375°F.
  2. In a medium cast iron skillet heated over medium-high heat, add olive oil. Add chorizo to the pan using the back of a spoon to crumble as it cooks. Cook until well-browned and use a slotted spoon to set aside, leaving drippings in the pan. Saute onion for 3-4 minutes until softened, translucent and just beginning to pick up color. Fold potato cubes and reserved chorizo crumbles in onion and oil mixture until well-coated. Place in oven and bake for 25-30 minutes, tossing intermittently, until potatoes are fork tender and beginning to brown. Season lingcod fillets with paprika, salt and pepper and place on top of the hash and return to oven for 8-10 minutes until fish just begins to flake.

Served with

  • asparagus
  • spinach
