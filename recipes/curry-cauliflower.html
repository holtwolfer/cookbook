Curry Cauliflower

Ingredients

  • Yellow Cauliflower
  • 1 head of cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 2-3 Tbs butter
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp paprika

Instructions

  1. Add cauliflower to a nonstick pan, add about 2 Tbs water and heat over medium to 'steam' the cauliflower. Add more water if needed. When water is evaporated and cauliflower is tender add the butter and spices and mix together. Toss to coat and remove from the heat.
