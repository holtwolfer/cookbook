Twice-baked Broccoli Cheddar Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 4 large baking potatoes
  • 1/2 of a large broccoli, steamed for 5-6 minutes
  • 4 Tbs softened butter
  • 1/4 to 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup shredded sharp Cheddar, plus extra for topping
  • 1/2 cup shredded colby jack, plus extra for topping
  • Spike
  • ground pepper

Instructions

  1. Roast potatoes for about 1 hour at 400.
  2. Reserve some larger broccoli florets for topping and chop up the rest.
  3. Cut a canoe shape out of the potatoes and scoop out with a spoon.
  4. Mash together the potato with remaining ingredients and stuff the potatoes.
  5. Top with reserved broccoli and cheese and bake another 15-20 minutes. Finish under broiler if needed to melt the cheese topping.
