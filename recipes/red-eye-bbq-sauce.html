Red-eye BBQ Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 slice bacon, chopped
  • 1/2 medium onion
  • 3/4 cup strong coffee
  • 3/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 cup worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 3 Tbs brown sugar
  • 2 Tbs dijon mustard

Instructions

  1. Brown onion and fry bacon. Add remaining ingredients. Bring to a boil. Lower to a simmer until thickened.
