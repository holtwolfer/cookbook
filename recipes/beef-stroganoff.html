Beef Stroganoff

Tags

beef

Served with

  • rice
  • spaetzle
  • egg noodles
  • crusty bread
  • broccoli

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef sirloin, cut into 4 inch strips
  • 5 Tbs butter
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • ½ cup onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 Tbs flour
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 Tbs dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbs tomato paste
  • 1 can beef broth
  • 1-2 packets gelatin
  • 1 tsp worcestershire sauce
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 Tbs sherry

Instructions

  1. Pour gelatin out on top of stock.
  2. Cook steak in 3 Tbs butter and brown.  Add mushrooms, onion, and garlic.  Remove from skillet. Add 2 Tbs butter.  Blend in flour, paprika, salt/pepper, and tomato paste.  Pour in broth and cook until mixture thickens. Return meat and mushrooms to the pan.  Add sherry and sour cream.  Heat.  Serve over rice or spätzle or egg noodles.
