Bucatini all'Amatriciana

Ingredients

  • Bucatini all’Amatriciana
  • 3 Tbs olive oil
  • 4 oz guanciale or unsmoked bacon, cut into 1x1/4" strips
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/4 tsp dried crushed red pepper
  • 1 cup finely chopped onion
  • 2 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 1 lb cherry tomatoes, chopped
  • 12 oz bucatini or spaghetti
  • 3/4 cup pecorino romano cheese

Instructions

Heat 1 Tbs oil in heavy large skillet over medium heat. Add half of the guanciale and sautee until crisp, ~5 min. Transfer to paper towel. Reserve for garnish. Add 2 Tbsp oil to same skillet over medium low heat. Add garlic and crushed red pepper, saute ~2 mins. Add onion and remaining guanciale. Sautee until onion is translucent and fat has rendered, ~10 min. Stir in vinegar, cook 1 min. Add tomatoes, simmer 6 min. Season with salt and pepper. Add sauce and cheese to pasta. Sprinkle with reserved guanciale.

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • salad
