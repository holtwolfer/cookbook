Roasted Carrots and Parsnips

Ingredients

  • Roasted Carrots and Parsnips
  • 3-4 large carrots, match-sticked
  • 2-3 large parsnips, match-sticked
  • olive oil
  • honey
  • 1-2 Tbs orange juice
  • salt/pepper
  • fresh thyme, chopped

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425oF.
  2. Toss the veggies with the remaining ingredients and roast for about 30 minutes.
