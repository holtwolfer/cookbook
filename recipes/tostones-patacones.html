Tostones (Patacones)

Ingredients

  • 4 large green plantains (30 ounces; 850g)
  • 2 cups (480ml) neutral cooking oil such as vegetable or canola oil
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder, or more to taste

Instructions

  1. While the oil is preheating, peel the plantains by cutting off the ends and carefully cutting a slit down the sides of each plantain, cutting through the skin but not into the flesh. Remove the skin and then cut the fruit crosswise into 1-inch long chunks.
  2. When oil reaches 325ºF (160℃), add half of the plantain chunks and fry until golden and fork tender, adjusting heat as needed to maintain oil temperature around 300 to 325ºF (150 to 160℃), 7 to 8 minutes, flipping over as needed to ensure even color. Use a slotted spoon to carefully remove the plantain chunks from the oil and transfer them to a cutting board.
  3. Using a tostonera (plantain press) or tortilla press, smash each plantain chunk until flattened and about 1/4 -inch thick (see notes). Repeat frying and smashing the remaining plantain chunks, setting the flattened ones aside to be fried again.
  4. Heat oil to 350ºF (175℃), add half of the flattened plantains back into the oil and fry for a second time until golden all over and starting to float, 2 to 3 minutes. Use a slotted spoon or spider skimmer to remove the tostones and transfer to a large mixing bowl. Return oil to 375ºF (175℃) and repeat frying process with remaining flattened plantains.
  5. Once all are fried, immediately season with a large pinch of kosher salt and garlic powder, and toss to combine. Transfer tostones to a wire rack set in a rimmed baking sheet to drain any excess oil.

Served with

  • bean dip
  • guacamole
  • pico di gallo
