Grilled Ginger Chicken
1 ½ to 2 lbs chicken tenders
salt/pepper
⅓ cup mayonnaise
1 Tbs lime zest (from about 2 limes), plus lime wedges, for serving
1 Tbs finely grated fresh ginger (from a 3-inch piece of peeled ginger)
Pat the chicken dry and season all over with 1 1/2 teaspoons salt. In a medium bowl, stir together the mayonnaise, lime zest and ginger; season with salt and pepper. Add the chicken to the mayonnaise mixture and stir to coat. (The chicken can sit in the marinade for up to 8 hours in the fridge. Let come to room temperature before cooking.)
To grill: Heat a grill to medium-high. Add wood chips to the smoker box. Grill the chicken over direct heat to put on a good sear. Flip and then lower heat a couple minutes later.
To cook in a skillet: Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the chicken until juices run clear, about 5 minutes per side for thighs and about 4 minutes per side for breasts.
Serve chicken with lime wedges, for squeezing on top.
Served with: grilled broccoli tossed with olive oil, salt/pepper, aleppo pepper; coconut milk rice;
