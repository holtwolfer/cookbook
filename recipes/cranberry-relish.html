Cranberry Relish

Notes and variations

Notes: may want to halve the recipe

Ingredients

(Recipe has been halved here)

  • 1 cup cranberries
  • 1/2 small onion
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 6 Tbs sour cream
  • 1 Tbs fresh horseradish

Instructions

  1. Grind cranberries and onion in food processor until finely chopped.
  2. Add remaining ingredients.
  3. Mix well and freeze.

A recipe from uncle Joe and aunt Julie
