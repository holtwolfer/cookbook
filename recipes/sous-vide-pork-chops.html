Sous Vide Pork Chops

Ingredients

  • NOTE:

Instructions

Used 135 for 1 hr sous vide time/temp and then finished on a very hot grill. Turned out ok, but dropping temp to 130 might keep them a little juicier.

Vinaigrette was a tad too lemony.

Sous Vide Pork Chops

3 pork chops

2-3 cloves garlic, minced

2-3 big sprigs of basil, chopped

salt/pepper

Lemon Basil Vinaigrette

juice of 2 lemons

4-5 bunches of basil

1-2 cloves garlic

1/4 cup olive oil

Set sous vidalator to 130 degrees.

Season the chops with salt/pepper and rub both sides with garlic and basil. Seal in vacuum bags and place in sous vide bath for 1 hour.

Heat grill to high/ build hot charcoal fire/ heat a cast iron pan

Finish over high heat for 2-4 minutes per side, until lightly browned.

Served with

  • couscous
  • roasted potatoes
  • green beans sauteed with garlic
