Pork Chops with Cherry Sauce

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • Grilled Pork Chops with Cherry Sauce
  • 4 pork loin chops, preferably bone-in and at least 1 inch thick
  • 1 Tbs minced fresh rosemary
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 3 Tbs butter
  • 1 Tbs minced shallot / scallion / leek / ramp
  • 1 cup cherries, stemmed, pitted and halved or frozen cherries
  • ½ cup fruity red wine (may need to reduce if using frozen cherries)
  • Chops:

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

Pat the chops dry, and rub them all over with a mixture of the rosemary, salt, pepper and garlic. Cover, and marinate for up to 2 hours at room temperature, or overnight in the refrigerator. Bring the chops back to room temperature before grilling.

Heat one side of a charcoal or gas grill. Cook the chops over the hottest part of the fire until well seared on both sides, about 3 or 4 minutes per side. Move them to the cool part of the grill, cover and cook until done, anywhere from 1 to 10 minutes, depending on the heat of the fire and the thickness of the chops. Cook to 135 degrees in the thickest part of the chop. Transfer them to a platter, cover loosely with aluminum foil and let them rest while you make the sauce.

Sauce:

Put 1 tablespoon of the butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. When the foam subsides, add the shallots, and cook until soft, 2 or 3 minutes. Add the cherries, wine and whatever juices have accumulated around the pork chops; cook, stirring occasionally, until the liquid reduces into a thin syrup, 5 or 6 minutes. Stir in the remaining 2 tablespoons butter, a little at a time, until it’s incorporated into the sauce. Season with salt and pepper. Pour the sauce over the chops, and serve.

Cherry Sauce from Frozen

1 cup (packed) thawed frozen cherries without juice

1/2 cup wine

1 tsp corn starch

1 Tbs warm water

salt/pepper

Sous Vide Method:

Heat sous vider to 130oF.

Rub the chops with salt/pepper and garlic.

Place in vacuum bags, add rosemary stalks to either side of each chop. Seal the bags.

Sous vide for 1 hour.

Finish on a very hot grill or cast iron skillet.
