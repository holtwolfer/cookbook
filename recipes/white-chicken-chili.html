White Chicken Chili

Tags

slow cooker

Ingredients

  • Green Chicken Chili
  • 3 chicken breasts
  • 2 Tbs oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 1-2 anaheim chilis, chopped
  • 1-2 green bell peppers, chopped
  • tomatillos, chopped
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • coriander
  • paprika
  • 2 cans white beans or dried
  • chicken stock
  • 3 Tbs lime juice
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • tomatoes
  • avocado
  • cilantro
  • scallions
  • queso fresco

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in slow cooker, cook onions, add tomatillos and peppers. Cook until softened, add in garlic, cumin, coriander, paprika, oregano. Mash half the beans, and add all beans.
  2. Add chicken and enough stock to cover. Lower to high setting.
  3. Cook about 3-4 hours.
  4. Take chicken out and shred it (should be cooked enough that it will fall apart in the cooker) Add it back in.
  5. Shortly before serving add lime juice, sour cream, tomatoes.
  6. Serve with avocado, cilantro, scallions, queso fresco.
  7. Serve with crusty bread or rice?
