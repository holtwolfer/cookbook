Pretzel-Crusted Fish

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Monday, August 3, 2020

Tags

whitefishcodbassfish fry

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

Pretzel-Crusted Black Bass

  • 2 black bass filets, cut into smaller pieces
  • salt/pepper
  • flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp dry mustard
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 Tbs yellow mustard
  • 1 Tbs mayo
  • 1 cup pretzel crumbs
  • oil

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Season fish with salt and pepper. Mix flour, paprika and dry mustard on a plate. Whisk eggs and yellow mustard together in a bowl. Place pretzel crumbs on another plate.
  2. Dredge fish pieces in flour, dip in egg mixture and bread.
  3. Bake:
  4. Preheat oven to 425. Bake on wire rack on lined sheet pan.

Fry

  1. Heat oil in a dutch oven. Fry about 2 minutes per side and remove to a paper towel-lined plate and place in the oven on warm.

Notes

Notes: turned out kind of dry, not really any mustard flavor. Might try coating with mustard mixture and then crumbs. And/or cooking it piccata-style
