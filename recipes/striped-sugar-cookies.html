Striped Sugar Cookies

Review needed: This source entry may contain only photographs or partial instructions. Consult the preserved original below.

Instructions

    Notes and variations

    Active Time:

    30 mins

    Chill Time:

    2 hrs

    Total Time:

    3 hrs 5 mins

    Yield:

    3 3/4 dozen cookies

    3 cups all-purpose flour (about 12 3/4 ounces), plus more for dusting

    1 teaspoon baking powder

    1/2 teaspoon table salt

    1 1/4 cups unsalted butter, softened

    1 1/4 cups granulated sugar

    2 large egg yolks

    1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract

    1 teaspoon grated lemon zest

    Food coloring

    White sanding sugar

    Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl. Beat butter and sugar in a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment on medium-high speed until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes. Scrape down sides of bowl; beat for about 10 seconds.

    Add egg yolks, 1 at a time, beating well after each addition. Beat in vanilla and zest. Add flour mixture to bowl; beat on low speed until just combined, about 1 minute. Scrape down sides of bowl; increase mixer speed to medium, and beat for 10 more seconds.

    Divide dough into 3 equal portions, and place in 3 separate bowls. Add desired food coloring to each bowl; knead to combine. Divide 1 tinted dough into 3 portions, about 1 3/4 ounces, 3 1/2 ounces, and 5 1/2 ounces. Repeat the process with the remaining 2 dough portions.

    Roll each dough portion into a 7 1/2- x 3 1/2-inch slab on a floured work surface. (The slabs will vary in thickness.) Stack the rolled out doughs on top of each other, varying the color pattern as desired and very lightly brushing tops of each slab with water before adding another to the stack. Wrap the stack in plastic wrap, and refrigerate until very firm, at least 2 hours.

    Preheat oven to 350°F with racks in upper and lower third positions. Line 2 to 3 baking sheets with parchment paper.

    Trim sides of the dough stack to create clean edges. Cut the stack in half lengthwise to create two slimmer stacks, about 1 1/2 x 7 1/2 inches each. Slice each dough stack crosswise into 1/4-inch-thick cookies. Transfer to prepared baking sheets, leaving 2 inches between each cookie. Sprinkle cookies with sanding sugar.

    Bake cookies on upper third and lower third racks, rotating baking sheets from top to bottom halfway through baking time, until bottoms are light golden, about 10 minutes. Cool on the pan for 15 minutes. Repeat process with remaining unbaked cookies.

    Make ahead

    Cookie dough can be refrigerated for up to three days or frozen for three months. Let the dough temper slightly before slicing for clean, even layers.
