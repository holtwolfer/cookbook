Cranberry-Raspberry Sauce

Ingredients

  • 6 cups (24 oz.) cranberries (buy 4 pints or 2 lbs)
  • 8 cups (36 oz. ) raspberries
  • 2/3 cup crancello liqueur
  • 1-1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 Tbs fresh grated ginger
  • 4 Tbs chopped fresh mint
  • pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Place the cranberries, raspberry liqueur, sugar, ginger, mint and salt in a saucepot, saving the fresh raspberries for later.
  2. Heat the pot to medium heat and simmer for about 10 minutes, stir the cranberries every few minutes. Once the cranberries have popped and a sauce has formed, add the fresh raspberries. Stir and simmer another 5 minutes, until the raspberries have just cooked through. Serve warm or cold.
  3. Additions: Door county cherry cider
