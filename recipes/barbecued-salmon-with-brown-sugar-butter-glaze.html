Barbecued Salmon with Brown Sugar Butter Glaze

Ingredients

  • 1 center-cut salmon fillet (2 pounds), preferably skin-on

For the Marinade:

  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ¼ cup mirin
  • ¼ cup sake
  • 3 Tbs sesame oil
  • 2 Tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 scallions, trimmed and thinly sliced (set aside 3 tablespoons scallion greens for garnish)
  • 1 Tbs minced fresh peeled ginger
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

For the Sauce:

  • reserved marinade
  • ½ stick (4 Tbs) unsalted butter
  • ¼ cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 Tbs sriracha
  • ½ tsp freshly grated lemon zest
  • 1 Tbs fresh lemon juice, or to taste
  • 3 Tbs tamari or soy sauce
  • 3 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 1 Tbs olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • 1 Tbs cornstarch
  • 2 Tbs hot water
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Soak the cedar plank in water for at least 1 hour, preferably longer.

Marinating:

  1. Place the soy sauce, mirin, sake, sesame oil, Worcestershire sauce, scallions, ginger, and garlic in a mixing bowl and whisk to mix. Pour the marinade over the salmon in a ziploc bag. Let the salmon marinate in the refrigerator for 2 to 4 hours.
  2. Pull the salmon out, reserving the marinade.

Grilling:

  1. Set up your grill for indirect grilling and heat to medium-high (400 degrees).
  2. Arrange the salmon fillet on the plank and place the plank in the center of the grill away from the heat. Add wood chips or chunks to the fire. Indirect grill the salmon until browned and cooked through, 20 to 30 minutes basting 2-3 times with the sauce towards the end of cooking. Cook the salmon to about 125 degrees.
  3. Meanwhile make the sauce: Pour the marinade into a saucepan. Boil until reduced by half. Whisk in the butter, sugar, lemon zest and juice, and black pepper. Thicken with about 1 Tbs of cornstarch whisked into about 2 Tbs hot water.
  4. Serve the salmon on the plank. Brush one final time with the barbecue sauce and sprinkle with scallion greens. Serve the remaining barbecue sauce on the side.

Served with

  • Israeli couscous with red onion/carrots/bok choi
  • kale salad
  • roasted potatoes
  • hasselback potatoes
