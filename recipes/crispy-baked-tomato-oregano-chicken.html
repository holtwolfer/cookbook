Crispy Baked Tomato-Oregano Chicken

Tags

nytweeknight

Served with

  • pasta
  • orzo

Ingredients

  • Try without the panko or maybe our chicken parm breading mix
  • use some fresh oregano or basil
  • add mozzarella pearls
  • 2 Tbs full-fat yogurt
  • 1 Tbs tomato paste
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1 Tbs plus 1 tsp dried oregano, divided
  • 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 2-inch pieces, or breasts cut to form cutlets
  • salt
  • 2 pints cherry or grape tomatoes, halved
  • 2 Tbs olive oil
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan
  • ¾ cup panko bread crumbs
  • up sauce ingredients
  • cooking time is waaay longer

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425 degrees. In a large bowl, combine the yogurt, tomato paste, soy sauce and 1 Tbs oregano. Add the chicken; season lightly with salt and toss to coat.
  2. Add the halved tomatoes to a 9-by-13-inch glass baking dish, season with salt and the olive oil, and spread out into an even layer. Nestle the chicken on top of the tomatoes. Sprinkle the Parmesan and remaining 1 teaspoon oregano over everything. Cover with a layer of panko.
  3. Bake in the oven until the top crisps and the tomatoes collapse and get saucy, 20 to 25 minutes for breasts and 22 to 25 for thighs, depending on the thickness.
