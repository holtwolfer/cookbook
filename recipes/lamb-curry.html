Lamb Curry

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs olive oil
  • 2 lbs boneless lamb shoulder or 3-4 lbs bone-in lamb shoulder
  • 1 red onion, chopped
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 Tbs garlic, minced
  • 2 cup chicken stock
  • 2 Tbs ginger, minced
  • 2 Tbs curry powder
  • 1.5 cups yogurt
  • cilantro, chopped to garnish
  • cashews
  • 1.5 cups basmati rice

Instructions

  1. Heat the 2 Tbs olive oil and brown the lamb meat in the slow cooker and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Make sure there is no more than 2 Tbs of fat.
  3. Add onion and garlic and cook until softened, 5-10 minutes, stirring often. Add ginger and curry powder and stir until fragrant, 2 minutes.
  4. Add the stock and bring to a boil, reduce to high setting. Cook 5-6 hours until lamb is tender, switch to the low setting and let it cool slightly before adding the yogurt and cooking a little longer just to incorporate flavor.
