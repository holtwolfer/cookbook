Apple dutch baby pancake

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 3/4 cup unbleached all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1.5 Tbs sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1.5 cup milk
  • 1.5 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 Tbs unsalted butter, melted
  • 6 Tbs unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup white sugar, divided (Original 1/2 cup)
  • 3/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2-3 large tart apples - peeled, cored and sliced

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, blend eggs, flour, baking powder, sugar and salt. Gradually mix in milk, stirring constantly. Add vanilla, melted butter. Let batter stand for 30 minutes or overnight.
  2. Preheat oven to 425 F. Melt butter in a 12 inch cast iron skillet, rolling butter up on the sides of the pan.
  3. Add apple slices and sautee over medium-high heat, sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon. Cook, stirring occasionally until the apples are softened. Spread them out in an even layer and cook over high heat for 1-2 minutes then gently pour the batter mixture over the apples.
  4. Bake for 15 minutes at 425. Reduce heat to 375 and bake for 10 minutes.
