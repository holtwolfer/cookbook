Mississippi Roast

Tags

nytslow cookerstay with me here

Served with

  • egg noodles
  • steamed broccoli
  • cauliflower
  • crusty bread
  • dinner rolls

Ingredients

  • 1 bone-in chuck roast, 2 lbs
  • salt/pepper
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 Tbs 'butter'
  • 1 jar sweet cherry peppers, rough chop
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • ½ tsp dried dill
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 Tbs buttermilk (just under a Tbs of milk, add a little lemon juice)
  • chicken or beef stock
  • 1/4 cup sour cream

Instructions

  1. Place roast on a cutting board and rub the salt and pepper all over it. Sprinkle the flour all over the seasoned meat and massage it in.
  2. Heat the slow cooker on the 'brown' setting. Brown the roast for 4-5 minutes per side until a brown crust forms on all sides. Add the butter to the top of the roast and the peppers to the top and sides. Add enough chicken stock to just cover the bottom of the cooker. Put the lid on the slow cooker, and set the machine to low.
  3. As the roast heats, make a ranch dressing. Combine the mayonnaise, vinegar, dill and paprika in a small bowl and whisk to emulsify. Add the buttermilk if using, then whisk again. Remove the lid from the slow cooker and add the dressing to the top of the roast. Replace the top and allow to continue cooking, undisturbed, for 6 to 8 hours, or until you can shred the meat easily using 2 forks. Mix the meat with the gravy surrounding it. Mix in a little sour cream.
