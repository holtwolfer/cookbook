Sole Meuniere

Tags

nytwhitefishrockfishbasswalleye

Served with

  • salt potatoes
  • summer squash
  • broccoli
  • baked tato
  • green beans

Ingredients

  • 6 (4-oz) skinless, boneless sole or other thin fish fillets, patted dry
  • white wine to cover fish
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • salt

ground white or black pepper

  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 Tbs clarified butter
  • 4 Tbs unsalted butter, diced, at room temperature
  • 3 Tbs minced parsley
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges, for serving

Instructions

  1. Marinade the fish in white wine with a squeeze of lemon juice for 30 minutes to an hour.
  2. Heat oven to 200 degrees and place a large oven-safe plate or baking sheet inside.
  3. Place flour on a large, shallow plate. Season both sides of fish fillets with salt and pepper to taste. Dredge fish in flour, shaking off excess.
  4. In a 12-inch nonstick or enamel-lined skillet over medium-high heat, heat 2 Tbs clarified butter until bubbling. Place half of the fish fillets in the pan and cook until just done, 2 to 3 minutes per side, then transfer to the plate or baking sheet in the oven to keep warm. Repeat with more butter and fish.
  5. Arrange the fish on a warm serving platter. Top with parsley. In reserved skillet, heat remaining 4 Tbs unsalted butter until bubbling and golden, 1 to 2 minutes, then pour evenly over fillets. Serve immediately, with lemon wedges on the side.
