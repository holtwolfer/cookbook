White Lady
2 oz gin
1 oz triple sec
1 oz lemon juice, freshly squeezed
1 large egg white
1 lemon twist, for garnish
Combine all ingredients except garnish in a cocktail shaker without ice. Dry shake vigorously and without ice for 30 seconds to emulsify egg white.
Add ice and shake an additional 15–20 seconds to chill and dilute the drink.
Strain into a chilled coupe glass and garnish with a lemon twist.
