  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Do not thaw.
  2. Rinse ice off with water, steam over high heat for about 20 minutes in a large pot with steamer basket upside-down in the bottom and about 1 inch of water.

  4. 1/2 tsp salt

Steamed Dungeness

20 mins or a little less
Soy Caramel Sauce for Crab
2 Tbs soy sauce
1/4 cup sake
3 Tbs mirin
1 tsp sugar
1 Tbs lemon juice
1/4 cup cold butter
In a small saucepan, add the soy sauce, sake, mirin, sugar, and lemon juice.  Bring to a boil over high heat, then lower the heat and reduce to a simmer.  Cook the sauce until it is reduced by half, 5 to 7 minutes.  Turn the heat down to low and whisk in the butter 1 Tbs at a time, adding each only after the previous one has melted.
Avocado Herb Sauce for Crab
1 avocado
1/4 cup cilantro
1/4 cup Greek yogurt 
1 Tbs mayo
1 Tbs lime juice
1/2 tsp champagne vinegar
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp hot sauce
Combine all ingredients in a food processor and blend to a smooth puree.
Lemon Panko Sauce for Crab
1/2 cup panko
1/2 Tbs lemon zest
1/4 cup parsley, chopped
1/4 tsp salt
pinch of cayenne
1/4 cup butter
In a small bowl, mix the panko with the lemon zest, parsley, salt and cayenne.
Melt the butter and pour it into small bowl.
Serve the panko mixture and butter separately, dipping the crab into melted butter and then into the panko.
