Cheese Sauce for Veggies

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs butter 2 Tbs flour salt/pepper 1 cup milk (works best with 2% milk) 1-1/2 cups freshly shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add flour then whisk for one minute. Slowly whisk in milk then season with salt and pepper and switch to a wooden spoon. Stir and cook until thickened, about 4-5 minutes. Turn off heat then add cheese and stir until melted and smooth. Taste then season with additional salt and pepper if necessary. Serve immediately.
