--Best Meatballs--

Tags

Italianpastaspaghettiground beef

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • salad
  • bread and oil dip

Ingredients

  • Meatballs
  • 1 slee1ve saltines (20ish), crushed
  • salt & vinegar chips, crushed
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb ground pork or Italian sausage
  • 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • ------try adding 10oz frozen chopped spinach-----

Instructions

Preheat oven to 400 degrees.

Combine saltines and milk in large bowl and let sit for 5 minutes for saltines to soften. Mash with fork until smooth paste forms.

Add Parmesan, garlic powder, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper to saltine mixture and mix. Add beef, pork, soy sauce and combine with hands. Divide meat mixture into 24 scant ¼-cup portions. Roll portions between your wet hands to form balls. Place meatballs on a sheet pan and then bake until cooked through and slightly browned.

About 35-40 minutes.
