Grilled Ring Bologna

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ring bologna (I prefer Boar's Head brand)
  • 2 tsp Plain ol' yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp Your favorite BBQ seasoning (of course I recommend mine)
  • 1/4 cup Your favorite BBQ sauce (if it's super sweet, add a little vinegar to it)
  • Method

Instructions

  1. Start your fire and prepare for indirect cooking at medium heat (about 225º).
  2. Score one side of the baloney in a crosshatch pattern (see the picture above) about 1/8" deep.
  3. Smear the entire ring of baloney with the mustard.
  4. Dust all sides of the baloney with the seasoning.
  5. Cook the baloney indirect for about 90 minutes, without turning.
  6. Brush the entire ring with the BBQ sauce and cook another 15 minutes.
  7. Remove the ring to a platter.
  8. Coat the ring with the remaining BBQ sauce and let rest 10 minutes.
  9. Cut the ring into 1/2" slices, serve and enjoy.
