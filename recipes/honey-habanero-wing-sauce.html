Honey Habanero Wing Sauce

Ingredients

  • 3-4 celery stalks, chopped
  • 1-2 medium carrots, chopped
  • 20? scotch bonnets, halved or quartered
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • sugar, enough to coat peppers, ~2-3 Tbs
  • paprika, ~1-2 Tbs
  • sumac
  • pepper
  • 2-3 Tbs honey
  • 1-2 red bell peppers, chopped

Instructions

  1. celery, carrots, scotch bonnets sauteed until softened.  sugar, paprika, salt, pepper added.  Cooked for about 5 minutes until peppers look caramelized and then enough vinegar to just barely cover peppers is added.  Cooked over low heat for another 5-10 minutes and then processed, adding chopped up red bell and another stalk of celery.  Thickness adjusted with more cider vinegar and a splash of apple cider.
