Slaw

Ingredients

  • 3 cups shredded cabbage
  • 2-3 carrots, shredded
  • 2 Tbs rice vinegar
  • 2 Tbs cider vinegar
  • 1/3 cup sour cream
  • 3 Tbs mayo
  • salt/pepper ( a little heavy on the pepper)
  • 1-2 Tbs sugar
  • dry mustard

Instructions

  1. whisk the sour cream, mayo, vinegars, salt, pepper, sugar, mustard together.
  2. shred the cabbage and carrots and add to medium bowl. Stir in the dressing mix.
