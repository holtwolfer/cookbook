Summer Harvest Tortilla Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 ears of corn, or 1 1/2 cups frozen
  • 1 zucchini, chopped
  • 24 oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cups broth
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder / aleppo pepper
  • 1 can black beans
  • avocado
  • tortilla chips
  • lime
  • cilantro
  • flour tortillas
  • sour cream
  • salsa
  • cheese

Instructions

  1. Heat oil, sautee onions, then garlic. Stir in bell pepper, corn, and zucchini. Sautee 10 minutes. Add tomatoes, broth, cumin, aleppo, salt/pepper. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer for 10-15 minutes. Add black beans for 2 mins.
