Black Cod with Lemon Pepper Aioli and Greens

OneNote Seafood section · Original page dated Thursday, October 10, 2019

Tags

fishwhite fishsitka

Served with

  • roasted potatoes, salt potatoes, cous cous,

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

Aioli*

  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 tablespoons dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • ½ teaspoon grated lemon zest
  • 1 large garlic clove, grated or finely minced
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
  • ¼ cup canola oil
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • Black Cod

juice of 1-2 lemons

  • 4, 8oz pieces skin-on black cod
  • ~1/2 cup flour
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • salt/pepper

Greens

  • 2 bunches of swiss chard or 1 ½ to 2 pounds of any green vegetable, cut in 2 inch pieces 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • Aioli*
  • Cous cous
  • olive oil
  • onion, minced
  • broccoli, stalk minced, florets cut small
  • broccoli raab, chopped
  • 1 1/4 cup Israeli cous cous

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. Combine egg yolk with mustard, lemon juice, zest, garlic, salt and pepper. In a slow and steady stream, add the oils until the mixture is emulsified. This can be done in a food processor, blender, or by hand with a whisk. Set aside.
  2. *If you don’t want to make your own aioli, add mustard, lemon juice and zest, garlic, salt and pepper to ½ cup good quality mayonnaise. It will be our secret.
  3. Black Cod
  4. Squeeze lemon juice on cod, let sit for 20-30 mins, flipping once or twice.
  5. Mix flour, paprika. Pat the fish dry with paper towels, season with salt and pepper and then coat with the flour mixture.
  6. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil over medium-high heat in a large, preferably non-stick, skillet. Season fish generously with salt and pepper. Cook skin-side down for 4-5 minutes until golden brown and crisp. Gently flip fish and cook another 2-3 minutes. Set aside to rest.
  7. Greens
  8. Adjust heat to medium and add 3 tablespoons olive oil to the same skillet. Quickly sauté garlic and red pepper flakes, approximately 30 seconds, until fragrant. Add greens to the pan and season with salt and pepper. Use tongs to move the chard around the pan to wilt evenly. Add lemon juice just before serving.
