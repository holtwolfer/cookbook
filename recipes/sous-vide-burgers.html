Sous Vide Burgers

Ingredients

  • Sous Vide Burgers - Basic Recipe
  • 1.5 lbs ground beef, formed into patties
  • salt/pepper
  • Seal burgers in ziploc bags and cook in the water bath for 1 hour at 132oF.

Instructions

  1. Remove and finish on the grill or hot cast iron pan.
  2. Smashed burgers
  3. Onion griddle burgers
  4. jalapeno popper burger
  5. Balsamic Quick Pickles
  6. balsamic vinegar
  7. chile balsamic vinegar
  8. water
  9. garlic, minced
  10. cucumber slices
  11. Microwave pickling liquid to boil and then add cucumber slices and let sit.
  12. Smoked Gouda & Caramelized Onion Burger
  13. (see serious eats recipe ------>)
  14. Caramelized onions:
  15. 1 large onion, cut in half and sliced
  16. 1 Tbs olive oil
  17. 1 Tbs butter
  18. salt/pepper
  19. 1 tsp worcestershire sauce
  20. 2 tsp sherry vinegar
  21. The rest:
  22. sliced smoked gouda
  23. pretzel rolls
  24. lettuce
  25. tomato
  26. Onions:
  27. Heat the oil and butter over medium in a carbon steel or cast iron skillet, add the onions, stirring to coat.
  28. Cook for 5 minutes and then reduce the heat to medium-low, season with salt and pepper and stir in the worcestershire sauce and sherry vinegar. Cook, stirring occasionally until golden brown and caramelized.

Served with

  • fries
  • quick pickles
