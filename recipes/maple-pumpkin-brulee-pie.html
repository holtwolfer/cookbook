Maple Pumpkin Brulee Pie

Ingredients

  • Maple Pumpkin Brulé Pie
  • flour, for dusting
  • ½ recipe Flaky Butter Pie Dough
  • ¼ cup dark brown sugar
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 15 oz pumpkin purée
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup maple syrup
  • 2½ Tbs potato starch
  • 2½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1½ tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
  • ½ tsp ground cloves
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ cup Demerara sugar
  • in @ 3:20 crust protectors at 3:55, decorations on at 4:15
  • On a lightly floured surface, roll dough into a 12″ round. Fit into a 9″ pie plate. Trim edges and crimp; chill 30 minutes. Bake 350 for 8 to 10 minutes

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 375°. Whisk sugars and eggs in a bowl until pale and fluffy. Add pumpkin, cream, syrup, potato starch, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, clove, and salt; whisk until smooth. Pour filling over dough; using a spatula, spread into an even layer. Bake around 30 minutes and then add the crust protectors. Once set enough (around 40-45 minutes add any pie dough decorations (leaves). Continue baking until just set in the center, around 1 hr 20 mins. Transfer pie to a rack; let cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until cold, about 1 hour.
  2. Sprinkle Demerara sugar evenly over surface of pie. Guide the flame of a blowtorch back and forth over surface until sugar caramelizes. Serve immediately.
  3. Flaky Pie Dough
  4. MAKES ENOUGH FOR 2 CRUSTs and you should make enough for 2 crusts.
  5. 2 1⁄4 cups flour
  6. 1 Tbs sugar
  7. 1 tsp. salt
  8. 12 Tbs unsalted butter, cubed and chilled
  9. 6 Tbs ice-cold water
  10. Whisk flour, sugar, and salt in a bowl. Using a dough blender, two forks, or your fingers, cut butter into flour mixture, forming pea-size crumbles. Add water; work dough until smooth but with visible flecks of butter. (Alternatively, pulse ingredients in a food processor.) Divide dough in half and flatten into disks. Wrap disks in plastic wrap; chill 1 hour before using.
