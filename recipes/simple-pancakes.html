Simple Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 Tbs sugar, optional
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 ½ to 2 cups milk
  • 2 Tbs melted and cooled butter (optional), plus unmelted butter for cooking, or use neutral oil
  • maple syrup
  • Griddle surface 350-375 degrees

Instructions

  1. Heat a griddle or large skillet over medium-low heat. In a bowl, mix together dry ingredients.
  2. Beat eggs into 1 1/2 cups milk, then stir in 2 Tbs melted cooled butter, if using. Gently stir this mixture into dry ingredients, mixing only enough to moisten flour; don't worry about a few lumps. If batter seems thick, add a little more milk.
  3. Place a tsp of butter on griddle. When butter foam subsides, ladle batter on, making pancakes of any size you like. Adjust heat as necessary; usually, first batch will require higher heat than subsequent batches. Flip pancakes after bubbles rise to surface and bottoms brown, after 2 to 4 minutes.
  4. Cook until second side is lightly browned. Serve, or hold on an ovenproof plate in a 200-degree oven for up to 15 minutes.

Notes and variations

Served with

sausage; bacon; hashbrowns; eggs
