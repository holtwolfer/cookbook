Stuffed Pork Chops

Tags

pork

Served with

  • fried apples
  • green beans
  • mashed tatos
  • roasted tatos

Related recipes

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

  • 2 thick-cut pork chops
  • 7 slices of bread, lightly toasted, cubed
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 small carrot, diced
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • sage
  • paprika
  • salt/pepper
  • dash powdered rosemary
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup broth
  • (fresh herbs would be snice.)
  • [could add cider/cider vinegar]

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

preheat oven to 350

Stuffing:

sautee veggies in butter until softened, add seasonings, stir 2-3 minutes and transfer to a bowl. Toss with the bread. Whisk the stock and egg together and add to the bowl, tossing.

Chops:

cut a slit in the chops for the stuffing. Stuff thoroughly. Season with salt/pepper. Make a foil pouch for each chop and top with a dollop of butter. Bake for 1-1.5 hours to 145 degrees.
