Sloppy Joes

Ingredients

Sauce

  • 8-oz can tomato sauce
  • ½ cup ketchup
  • 1 Tbs chili sauce
  • 1 Tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • ½ tsp dry mustard
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 tsp molasses
  • 2 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tsp balsamic glaze
  • 1 garlic clove, finely minced
  • ¼ tsp orange zest
  • pinch of ground cloves
  • hot sauce to taste

Beef

  • 1.5 lb ground beef
  • 1-2 carrots, diced
  • 1 medium bell pepper, chopped
  • 2-3 celery stalks, minced
  • ½ small onion, finely chopped

To Serve

  • 6 hamburger buns, toasted

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan over low heat combine the sauce ingredients. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Mix well, and simmer while preparing the meat.
  2. Heat a heavy nonstick or cast-iron skillet, at least 10 inches in diameter, over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef, and sauté, stirring occasionally, for 4 to 5 minutes, or until the meat is no longer pink. Remove grease.
  3. Add the onion to the pan, and sauté, stirring frequently, for about 5 minutes, or until the onion is translucent. Return the meat to the pan, and add the sauce. Heat for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally. Taste and season with salt, pepper or more hot sauce, if desired. Spoon 1/2 cup of the mixture over each bun.

Slow Cooker Version

  1. Heat on brown setting and add the ground beef.
  2. When it is just cooked through remove excess fat and then add the onion, carrot, bell pepper, and celery.
  3. Cook for about 5 minutes and then add the sauce ingredients. Mix in and then switch to the low slow cooker setting. Add a little water if it seems too dry.

Served with

  • fries, sweet potato fries
  • tots, sweet potato tots
  • hasselback sweet potatoes
  • peas
