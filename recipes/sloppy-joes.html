Sloppy Joes

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Thursday, December 19, 2019

Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

Sauce

  • 8-oz can tomato sauce
  • ½ cup ketchup
  • 1 Tbs chili sauce
  • 1 Tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • ½ tsp dry mustard
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 tsp molasses
  • 2 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tsp balsamic glaze
  • 1 garlic clove, finely minced
  • ¼ tsp orange zest
  • pinch of ground cloves
  • hot sauce to taste

Beef

  • 1.5 lb ground beef
  • 1-2 carrots, diced
  • 1 medium bell pepper, chopped
  • 2-3 celery stalks, minced
  • ½ small onion, finely chopped

To Serve

  • 6 hamburger buns, toasted

Instructions (first-pass extraction)

  1. In a saucepan over low heat combine the sauce ingredients. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Mix well, and simmer while preparing the meat.
  2. Heat a heavy nonstick or cast-iron skillet, at least 10 inches in diameter, over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef, and sauté, stirring occasionally, for 4 to 5 minutes, or until the meat is no longer pink. Remove grease.
  3. Add the onion to the pan, and sauté, stirring frequently, for about 5 minutes, or until the onion is translucent. Return the meat to the pan, and add the sauce. Heat for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally. Taste and season with salt, pepper or more hot sauce, if desired. Spoon 1/2 cup of the mixture over each bun.

Slow Cooker Version

  1. Heat on brown setting and add the ground beef.
  2. When it is just cooked through remove excess fat and then add the onion, carrot, bell pepper, and celery.
  3. Cook for about 5 minutes and then add the sauce ingredients. Mix in and then switch to the low slow cooker setting. Add a little water if it seems too dry.

Served with

  • fries, sweet potato fries
  • tots, sweet potato tots
  • hasselback sweet potatoes
  • peas

12 ounces lean ground beef (90% lean or higher)
1 tablespoons olive oil
1 medium onion, diced
1 medium red bell pepper, diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
8 ounces sliced white button mushrooms, finely chopped
One 15-ounce can no-salt added tomato sauce
1 tablespoons tomato paste
1 ½ tablespoons molasses
1 tablespoon chili powder
2 teaspoons apple cider vinegar
1 teaspoon mustard powder
½ teaspoon salt
¼ teaspoon fresh ground black pepper
4 whole wheat hamburger buns
Add:
Celery, Carrots, Tomatoes, red wine, balsamic, paprika, ketchup
Directions
Brown the beef in a very large skillet over a medium-high heat, breaking it up with a spoon as it cooks, 3-4 minutes. Transfer the meat to a plate. Add the oil to the pan, and heat over a medium-high heat until it is shimmering. Add the onion and peppers and cook, stirring occasionally, until the onions are golden and peppers have softened, 5-6 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute more, then add the mushrooms and cook, stirring once or twice, until they release their water and begin to brown, about 5 minutes.
Reduce the heat to medium. Return the beef to the skillet and stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, molasses, chili powder, vinegar, mustard powder, salt and pepper.
Simmer until the mixture thickens, and most liquid is evaporated, 5-10 minutes. To serve, pile about ¾ cup of the mixture onto each split hamburger bun.
