Sous Vide Fried Chicken

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut up, breast cut into 2-3 pieces
  • salt/pepper
  • 1.5 cups flour
  • 1/4 cup corn starch
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp non-salt spike
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1/4 tsp ground mustard
  • salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk (1-2 Tbs lemon juice, bring up to 1 cup with milk)
  • 4 cups canola oil for frying
  • Set sous vide temperature to 155
  • Season chicken with salt/pepper and vacuum seal.
  • (We put everything in and cooked for 4.5 hours… you can seal white and dark meat separately and cook dark for 3 hours, white for 1)

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the flour, corn starch, baking powder, spices. Add 5-6 Tbs buttermilk and work in with fingertips.
  2. Dunk chicken pieces in buttermilk to coat, let excess run off.
  3. Toss in flour mixture to coat. Press mixture into each piece to make a thick layer.
  4. Heat oil to 350-375 in blue dutch oven.
  5. Add chicken, skin side down, 3 pieces at a time to the oil. Temp should drop to 300. Adjust heat to maintain 300. Fry until golden brown on first side, about 3 minutes (don't touch it for 3 minutes). Carefully flip with tongs and cook on other side until browned, about 1-2 minutes longer.
  6. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and place in the oven to keep warm.
  7. Melt butter in a sauce pan, add the flour and whisk constantly until lightly browned, about 2 minutes.
  8. Slowly add the broth, whisking constantly. Bring to a boil, season with salt and pepper.
  9. Add more stock if too thick.
  10. Additions: onions/scallions/leeks/shallots - sautee in the butter and then add the flour

Served with

  • mashed potatoes
  • gravy
  • corn
  • biscuits
  • slaw
