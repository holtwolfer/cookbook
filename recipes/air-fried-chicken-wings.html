Air-Fried Chicken Wings

Ingredients

Wings

  • 2 lb chicken wings, separated
  • 1 ¼ tsp baking powder

Wing Sauce

  • 1/2 cup Franks
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 Tbs paprika
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 1 Tbs sherry vinegar
  • cayenne to adjust the heat

To Serve:

  • blue cheese dressing
  • carrot sticks
  • celery sticks
  • (can also add more relish tray-style veggies)

Instructions

Sauce:

  1. Mix all ingredients and heat in a sauce pan. Bring to a boil and simmer for 5 minutes.

Wings:

  1. Coat with baking powder and then let dry at rt for up to 1 hr.
  2. Try coating with sauce and returning to air fryer for a few minutes.
  3. Preheat air-fryer to 360.
  4. Toss wings with baking powder salt/pepper.
  5. Fry for about 20-25 minutes, tossing a couple of times.
  6. Increase heat to 390 for about 5 minutes to crisp.
  7. Remove from fryer and toss immediately with wing sauce.

Served with

  • fries
  • carrots, celery
  • nachos
