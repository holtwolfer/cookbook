Air-Fried Chicken Wings

Ingredients

  • Air-Fried Wings
  • 1 ¼ lb chicken wings
  • 1 ¼ tsp baking powder
  • blue cheese dressing or dip
  • carrot sticks
  • celery sticks
  • Coat with baking powder and then let dry at rt for up to 1 hr.
  • Try coating with sauce and returning to air fryer for a few minutes.
  • Preheat air-fryer to 360.
  • Toss wings with baking powder salt/pepper.
  • Fry for about 20-25 minutes, tossing a couple of times.
  • Increase heat to 390 for about 5 minutes to crisp.
  • Remove from fryer and toss immediately with wing sauce.
  • Wing Sauce
  • 1/2 cup Franks
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 Tbs paprika
  • 1 Tbs butter
  • 1 Tbs sherry vinegar
  • cayenne to adjust the heat

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients and heat in a sauce pan. Bring to a boil and simmer for 5 minutes.
  2. Blue Cheese Wing Dip
  3. 3 ounces blue cheese, crumbled (about 3/4 cup)
  4. 1 Tbs fresh lemon juice or apple cider vinegar
  5. 1 to 3 Tbs milk or buttermilk, or to taste
  6. Stir all ingredients together.
  7. Barbecue Chicken Wings
  8. 3 pounds of chicken wings
  9. 1 tablespoon wine or rice vinegar
  10. 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  11. 1 teaspoon chili powder
  12. 2 tablespoons finely chopped onion
  13. 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  14. Cut 3 pounds of chicken wings into three sections; save the wing tips for stock. Toss the wings with a little neutral oil to keep them from sticking.
  15. Heat a charcoal or gas grill; the fire should be moderately hot and the rack 4 to 6 inches from the heat. Leave one side of the grill cooler for indirect cooking.
  16. Put the wings on the cool side of the grill. Cover the grill and cook, checking and turning once or twice, until most of the fat has been rendered and the wings are cooked through, 15 to 20 minutes.
  17. While the wings cook, combine 1/2 cup ketchup, 2 tablespoons water, 1 tablespoon wine or rice vinegar, 1 teaspoon soy sauce, 1 teaspoon chili powder, 1 teaspoon cumin, 2 tablespoons finely chopped onion and 1 teaspoon minced garlic in a large bowl.
  18. When the wings are cooked, add them to the bowl with the sauce, and toss to coat. Now put the wings on the hot part of the grill, and cook, uncovered, turning as necessary, until they’re nicely browned on both sides.

Served with

  • fries
