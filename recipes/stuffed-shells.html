Stuffed Shells

Tags

Italianpastakid-friendly

Served with

  • garlic bread

Ingredients

  • FOR THE SAUCE:
  • 4 Tbs butter
  • 1 medium onion, cut in half
  • salt
  • 1 jar tomato paste
  • 1 (28-ounce) can whole tomatoes
  • 1 qt tomatoes in sauce
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1-2 bay leaves
  • 1-2 pieces of parmesan rind
  • FOR THE FILLING AND ASSEMBLY:
  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 8 oz shitake mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 large package fresh spinach
  • salt/pepper
  • 1 lb fresh ricotta (about 2 cups)
  • 10 oz brick mozzarella, grated (about 2 1/2 cups)
  • fresh mozzarella balls
  • fontina
  • 5 ½ oz Parmesan, finely grated (about 2 cups)
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 12 ounces jumbo shells
  • basil?
  • note- made too much of the sausage/veggie mixture to mix with the ricotta mixture. Used less than 1 12 oz box of shells.

Instructions

Make the sauce: In a large Dutch oven heat all sauce ingredients, using water to rinse out tomato can/jar and bring to a simmer. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 45 minutes to an hour until thickened.

Heat the oven to 375 degrees. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.

Make the filling:

Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the sausage and cook until mostly done, remove excess grease. Add the onion and sautee until starting to get tender, a few minutes. Add the mushrooms and cook another 2 minutes. Add the spinach a handful at a time and let it cook down. Add the garlic and season with salt and pepper. Cook a few more minutes and then remove from heat.

In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta with 1 1/2 cups mozzarella, 1 cup Parmesan and the egg yolks. Stir to combine, then set aside. Mix in the sausage, spinach.

Add the shells to the boiling water and cook until just shy of al dente. (You’ll want to cook the shells about 2 minutes less than the minimum time listed on the package, as the shells will continue to cook in the oven) Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain the pasta and rinse it under cold water to cool. Count out 24 shells. (You will have cooked off more shells than will fit in the dish; that’s insurance in case any rip. Reserve extras for another use.)

Stir the pasta water into the sauce, then add half the sauce to a 3-quart/9-by-13-inch baking dish and spread it in an even layer. Spoon about 2 tablespoons filling into each of the 24 shells, lining up the stuffed shells in the dish as you go. Spoon the remaining sauce over the shells, then sprinkle with the remaining 1 cup mozzarella and 1 cup Parmesan.

Cover the dish tightly with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Remove the foil and bake, uncovered, until the cheese is melted and the sauce is bubbling, 15 to 20 minutes. (If you’d like to brown the cheese, you can broil for a few minutes.) Let sit 5 minutes, then serve.
