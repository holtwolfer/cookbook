Sous Vide Steaks

Ingredients

  • Sous Vide Rib-Eye Steak
  • rosemary or thyme
  • butter

Instructions

  1. Set sous videlator to 129oF. Season the steaks with salt and pepper. Slide into bags and line with fresh herbs. Cook in water bath for 1 hour.
  2. (baked potato = 450 for 45 mins to 1 hr)
  3. Cast iron skillet finished with butter and herbs
  4. 2-3 Tbs butter
  5. fresh herbs (ex. rosemary, thyme, sage)
  6. sliced alliums (ex. garlic, shallots, chives, ramps, leeks, garlic scapes)
  7. Heat skillet over high heat. Brown the steak on one side, flip, add the butter, herbs, alliums and start spooning the melting butter over the steak. Flip one more time and spoon a little butter over the other side and remove. Let rest 10 minutes, tented with foil.
  8. Charcoal Grill
  9. Grill over direct very hot coals, about 1-2 minutes per side to get a nice brown crust
  10. Fire Pit

Served with

  • cheesy hash browns
  • baked potatoes
  • hasselback potatoes
  • fingerlings
  • asparagus
  • broccoli
  • green beans
