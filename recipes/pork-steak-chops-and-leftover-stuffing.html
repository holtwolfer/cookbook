Pork Steak/Chops and Leftover Stuffing

OneNote Pork section · Original page dated Monday, December 2, 2019

Tags

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • Pork Steaks with Leftover Stuffing
    • 2 pork steaks, trimmed
    • bourbon
    • leftover stuffing
    • spike/pepper

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    Preheat oven to 375.

    Heat large cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add pork steaks and brown on both sides, about 4 minutes per side. Remove steaks. Deglaze with bourbon. Add stuffing to the skillet, place steaks on top and season with spike and pepper. Put in oven and cook until pork is 345 degrees, about 20 minutes.

    Original OneNote text (complete)
    Pork Steaks with Leftover Stuffing
2 pork steaks, trimmed
bourbon
leftover stuffing
spike/pepper
Preheat oven to 375.
Heat large cast iron skillet over medium-high heat.  Add pork steaks and brown on both sides, about 4 minutes per side.  Remove steaks.  Deglaze with bourbon.  Add stuffing to the skillet, place steaks on top and season with spike and pepper.  Put in oven and cook until pork is 345 degrees, about 20 minutes.
