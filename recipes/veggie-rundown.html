Veggie Rundown

Ingredients

  • Veggie Rundown
  • 1 Tbsp Butter
  • 3 stalks Escallion, chopped
  • 1 small Onion, chopped
  • 1/4 Scotch Bonnet Pepper, sliced
  • 1 tsp Fresh Thyme
  • 4 cups Mixed julienned vegetables: carrots, zucchini, yellow squash, broccoli, string beans, tomatoes or any vegetables in season
  • 1 cup Coconut Milk
  • Salt and Black Pepper to taste
  • In skillet melt butter and sautee seasonings: onion, escallion, scotch bonnet pepper, thyme.

Instructions

  1. Sautee seasonings until onion is transparent
  2. Add mixed vegetables to the skillet and sautee
  3. Add coconut milk to pan let simmer until coconut milk thickens and coats vegetables
  4. Season with salt and black pepper to taste
  5. Tags: Jamaican; veggie; side;
