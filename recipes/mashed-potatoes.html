Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup roughly chopped parsley
  • 2/3 cup olive oil
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 lb. Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • milk or heavy cream
  • butter

Instructions

  1. Purée parsley, 1/3 cup oil, salt, and pepper in a food processor until smooth; set aside.
  2. Bring a 2-qt. saucepan of salted water to a boil. Add potatoes and garlic to pan; boil until tender, 18–20 minutes, then drain and transfer to a big bowl.
  3. Add stock, salt, and pepper; mash until smooth and keep warm.
