Mashed Potatoes

OneNote Beef section · Original page dated Thursday, March 5, 2020

Tags

Served with

    Ingredients (first-pass extraction)

    • 1/2 cup roughly chopped parsley
    • 2/3 cup olive oil
    • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
    • 1/2 lb. Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and chopped
    • 1/2 cup chicken stock
    • 3 cloves garlic
    • milk
    • butter
    • Purée parsley, 1/3 cup oil, salt, and pepper in a food processor until smooth; set aside.

    Instructions (first-pass extraction)

    1. Bring a 2-qt. saucepan of salted water to a boil. Add potatoes and garlic to pan; boil until tender, 18–20 minutes, then drain and transfer to a big bowl.
    2. Add stock, salt, and pepper; mash until smooth and keep warm.
