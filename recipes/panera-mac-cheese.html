Panera Mac & Cheese

Ingredients

  • 3 cups water
  • 8 oz. shell pasta
  • 1 Tbs salt
  • 2 Tbs butter
  • 2 Tbs flour
  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup white American cheese, cubed
  • 1 1/4 cups shredded sharp white cheddar
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/4 tsp mustard powder

Instructions

In a 2-qt pot over high heat, add water and bring to a boil. Add pasta and 2 Tbs salt. Cook according to package instructions, drain, set aside.

Return pot to stove over medium heat and add butter. Once melted, add flour and whisk 1 minute. Slowly whisk in milk and cream. Continue whisking while mixture comes to a boil and thickens, about 3 minutes.

Reduce heat to low and whisk in American cheese. Once thoroughly melted, add in remaining salt, Cheddar, pepper, and mustard powder. Whisk until smooth.

Remove sauce from heat and fold in pasta. Serve immediately.

Served with

  • chicken strips
  • broccoli
  • crusty toasted bread
