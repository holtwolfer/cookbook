Dorayaki (Japanese Pancake)

Ingredients

  • Dorayaki
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 Tbs honey
  • 1 cup flour, sifted
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup water
  • canola oil
  • Fillings:
  • red bean paste
  • nutella
  • cream
  • custard

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs, sugar, honey in a mixing bowl until blended and thick.
  2. Sift the flour and baking powder into the bowl. Whisk until smooth.
  3. Slowly add the water while stirring with a spoon.
  4. Cook in oiled cast iron skillet as you would pancakes, adding about 1/8 cup of batter for pancakes that are about 3 inches in diameter.
  5. Spread red bean paste, nutella or other filling on one pancake and top with another to make a sandwich.
