Pan-Fried Black Bass

Tags

fishwhite fish

Served with

  • smashed potatoes, cauliflower and carrots with panko and parmesan

Ingredients

black bass filets, cut into smaller pieces

  • 1-2 lemons, juiced
  • butter
  • fresh thyme
  • 1 garlic clove, thinly sliced
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

  1. Squeeze lemon juice over black bass and let sit for 30 minutes to an hour, flipping a couple times.
  2. Season fish with salt, pepper, and paprika on all sides.
  3. Heat a cast iron or carbon steel skillet over medium high heat and melt a little butter to coat the pan.
  4. Brown the fish on one side, while melting more butter with the thyme and garlic. Flip the fish and baste with the butter using a spoon until it is cooked through, about 2-3 minutes.
