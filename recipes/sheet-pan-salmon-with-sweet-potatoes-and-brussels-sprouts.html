Sheet-Pan Salmon with Sweet Potatoes and Brussels Sprouts

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp aleppo pepper
  • 1 1/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1 tsp balsamic glaze
  • 1/2 tsp date syrup (optional) or maple syrup
  • 1 lb sweet potatoes (about 3 potatoes), cut into 2-in. pieces
  • 3 Tbs olive oil, divided
  • 1 Tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 8 oz brussels sprouts, halved vertically

(add broccoli; cauliflower)

  • 4 (6-oz.) skinless salmon fillets
  • 2 Tbs chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • lime wedges

Instructions

  1. Stir together paprika, pepper, brown sugar, garlic powder, cumin, coriander, aleppo pepper, and 3/4 tsp of the salt in a shallow bowl; set aside.
  2. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss together sweet potatoes, 1 Tbs of oil, and 1/4 tsp salt, balsamic glaze, date syrup or maple syrup. Spread in an even layer on 1 end of a lined baking sheet. Bake in preheated oven 7 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, toss together Brussels sprouts (and any other veggies), 1 Tbs of the oil, and 2 teaspoons of the spice mixture, and 1 Tbs balsamic vinegar in a large bowl. Add Brussels sprouts to pan with potatoes, and bake 10 minutes.
  4. Rub salmon with remaining 1 Tbs oil, and sprinkle with remaining spice mixture. Add salmon to pan. Cook until salmon is lightly browned and firm (medium) and Brussels sprouts and potatoes are tender, 10-15 minutes. Sprinkle with parsley, and serve with lime wedges.
