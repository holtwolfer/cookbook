Sweet & Sour Sauce Dipping Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (160g) apricot jam or preserves
  • 2 Tbs (40g) corn syrup
  • 2 Tbs (30ml) water
  • 5 tsp (25ml) distilled white vinegar
  • 1 1/2 tsp (4g) cornstarch
  • 1 tsp (3g) chicken-flavored broth powder, such as Orrington Farms
  • 1/2 tsp (3ml) soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp (3g) yellow mustard
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/8 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/8 tsp finely ground white pepper

Instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, combine jam, corn syrup, 2 tablespoons (30ml) water, vinegar, cornstarch, broth powder, soy sauce, mustard, cayenne pepper, garlic powder, and white pepper. Using an immersion blender, blend until throughly combined and smooth. Set over medium-high heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until mixture boils and becomes clear, 2 to 3 minutes. Transfer sauce to a heatproof bowl and let cool at room temperature. Set aside.
