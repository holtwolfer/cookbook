Chicken Artichoke Tomato Goat Cheese Pasta

Ingredients

  • 1 lb gemelli (or linguine, penne, others)
  • Leftover chicken from grilled lemon chicken
  • 2 Tbs olive oil
  • pinch crushed red pepper
  • 2 cans artichokes (or about 1/2 of a costco jar)
  • 1 medium red onion, coarse chop
  • 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 8-10 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 roasted red pepper, chopped
  • 28 oz. can peeled, whole tomatoes
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 bunches of fresh thyme (left whole to grab out later)
  • 1/4 cup white wine
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar, divided
  • 1-2 tsp oregano (or fresh basil)
  • 10 oz. goat cheese (1/2 crumbled to serve)
  • 1/2 bunch of parsley, chopped
  • Cook the pasta.
  • In large dutch oven heat the olive oil and crushed red pepper over medium heat and saute the red onions until tender (3-5 minutes). Stir in the garlic and cook 1-2 minutes, until fragrant. Deglaze with white wine and 2 Tbs balsamic vinegar, letting it mostly evaporate (4-5 minutes). Add sun-dried tomatoes, roasted red pepper, oregano, thyme, bay leaf, stirring for 1-2 minutes. Then add canned tomatoes, breaking them up by hand. Let the sauce thicken a little, stirring occasionally for about 15 minutes.

Instructions

  1. Stir in about half of the goat cheese. Stir in the leftover chicken. Strain pasta and add to the sauce with about 1 Tbs of the cooking water. Add 2 Tbs balsamic vinegar and stir to combine.

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • crusty bread + dipping sauce
