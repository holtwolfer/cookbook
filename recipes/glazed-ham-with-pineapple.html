Glazed Ham with Pineapple

Ingredients

  • 4 Tbs butter
  • 1 can pineapple rings, reserving liquid
  • 1/4 cup orange juice
  • 1 Tbs cherry liquid
  • 1 Tbs Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 cup cider vinegar
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp molasses
  • 1-2 oranges, sliced (optional)
  • salt/pepper

Instructions

Heat a large cast iron skillet set over medium-high heat, and melt 2 Tbs of butter. Add ham steaks and cook on each side, until lightly browned, about 2-3 minutes a side. Transfer to plate.

Add 2 Tbs of butter to skillet. Add pineapple and orange slices (if using) and saute until lightly browned, about 4 to 5 minutes per side. Remove the pineapple to the plate.

Add orange, cherry and pineapple juices to the pan and reduce by 1/2.

Mix in the mustard, vinegar, brown sugar, soy sauce, molasses, stir 1 minute, reduce heat to low. Cook until thick, sticky, and bubbly. Remove from heat and stir in 1-2 Tbs butter. Transfer ham steaks back into saute pan, coating both sides with the glaze. Place pineapple rings on top with a cherry in each. Drizzle some glaze over top and transfer skillet to the oven to heat through.

3 3/4 cups chicken broth

1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro

1 Tbs brown sugar

Add broth and bring to boil, stirring occasionally. Cover pot. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until rice is tender and broth is absorbed, about 25 minutes. Season pilaf to taste with salt and pepper. Sprinkle with cilantro and serve.

Ingredients

1 can (8 ounces) sliced pineapple

2 boneless fully cooked ham steaks (about 2 pounds)

1/4 cup packed brown sugar

1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard

4 maraschino cherries

Served with Bell Pepper Rice Pilaf

Drain pineapple, reserving 1 tablespoon juice; set pineapple aside (discard remaining juice or save for another use). Cut each ham steak in half; place in an ungreased baking pan.

Combine brown sugar, mustard and reserved juice; spread over ham. Top with pineapple and cherries. Bake, uncovered, at 350° for 20-25 minutes or until heated through.
