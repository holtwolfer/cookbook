Seared Albacore with Chimichurri Rojo

Ingredients

Seared Tuna with Chimichurri Rojo

  • 3 fillets albacore tuna

Rub

coriander

  • paprika
  • smoked paprika
  • sumac

aleppo pepper

  • cumin

Chimichurri Rojo

  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 large tomato, seeded, finely chopped
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • ½ cup parsley, chopped (substituted radish and turnip greens)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp minced red chili
  • ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ lemon, zested
  • 2 Tbs sherry or red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 Tbs canola
  • lime wedges

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, combine shallot, garlic, tomato, parsley, oregano, chili, paprika, lemon zest, vinegar, olive oil and salt (to taste). Add 1 tbsp water to thin the sauce out. Stir to combine.
  2. Allow the chimichurri to rest for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to mellow and meld.
  3. Rub the tuna with the spice mix.
  4. Bring a cast iron skillet (or other heavy skillet) to high heat and add oil. Sear the tuna quickly on all sides, about 1 minute for the first side and then 30 seconds for remaining sides.
  5. Slice the tuna in ½ inch slices and serve with the chimichurri to the side or spooned on top.

Served with

  • roasted sweet potatoes
  • stir-fried veggies with balsamic
