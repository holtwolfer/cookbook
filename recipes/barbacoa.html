Barbacoa

Ingredients

  • salt/pepper
  • 3 lb chuck roast
  • 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 Tbs cumin
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1/2 Tbs smoked paprika
  • 1/2 Tbs coriander
  • 1/2 Tbs aleppo pepper
  • 1⁄2 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1⁄4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 2 Tbs lime juice
  • 2 Tbs orange juice
  • 1/2 med red onion
  • 1 large roasted red pepper
  • 2 teaspoons Mexican oregano
  • 1/2 jar tomato paste
  • vegetable oil
  • 2 cup beef broth
  • 3 bay leaves

Instructions

Adobo sauce:

  1. combine vinegar, limejuice, roasted red pepper, garlic, onion, oj, oregano  in  processor until smooth.

    2. Beef:

  2. Cut roast into large chunks.
  3. Sear all sides of roast on brown setting in slow cooker.
  4. Add  garlic, cumin, paprikas, coriander, black pepper, salt and cloves to toast
  5. Add tomato paste, stir 1-2 mins to caramelize
  6. pour adobo sauce over meat.
  7. Pour in the beef broth and add bay leaves. It's ok if beef is not fully covered, just flip it once in a while.
  8. Cook on high setting 5-6 hours.
  9. Near the end of cooking time remove about 1 cup of the sauce and heat in a skillet until reduced to a thick sauce. Remove/reserve all but about 1/3 cup of the sauce. Shred the meat into the skillet and coat with the sauce. Serve with the reserved sauce on the size. (it will be pretty flavorful)

Served with

  • hard or soft tortilla shells
  • rice
  • black (cuban black beans)
  • cheese
  • pico de gallo
  • guacamole
  • sour cream
  • peppers & onions
  • avocado
  • lettuce
  • shredded cabbage
  • cilantro
  • tomatoes
  • cilantro lime rice
