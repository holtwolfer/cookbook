Jalapeno Poppers

From the OneNote Appetizers section · Original page dated Friday, September 20, 2019

Review before cooking or publishing:
  • A second ingredient-only variation appears after the tags; verify before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 8-10 jalapenos
  • 8-10 small sweet peppers
  • 1 container whipped cream cheese
  • 2 Tbs sour cream
  • 1 Tbs lime juice
  • grated jack cheese
  • fresh mozzarella, crumbled
  • cumin
  • paprika
  • smoked paprika
  • chimichurri spice blend
  • proscuitto
  • panko
  • avocado oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400. Cut peppers in half. Mix cream cheese, sour cream, cheeses, spices, proscuitto together. Spread on pepper halves. Mix panko and oil until it gets to the consistency of wet sand. Top the peppers with panko. Bake for 10-15 minutes.
