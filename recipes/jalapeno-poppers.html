Jalapeno Poppers

Tags

appetizerpopperssuper bowlbar foodpacker snackers

Ingredients

  • 8-10 jalapenos
  • 8-10 small sweet peppers
  • 1 container whipped cream cheese
  • 2 Tbs sour cream
  • 1 Tbs lime juice
  • grated jack cheese
  • fresh mozzarella, crumbled
  • cumin
  • paprika
  • smoked paprika
  • chimichurri spice blend
  • proscuitto
  • panko
  • avocado oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400. Cut peppers in half. Mix cream cheese, sour cream, cheeses, spices, proscuitto together. Spread on pepper halves. Mix panko and oil until it gets to the consistency of wet sand. Top the peppers with panko. Bake for 10-15 minutes.
