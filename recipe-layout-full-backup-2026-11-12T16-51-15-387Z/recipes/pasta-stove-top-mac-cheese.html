Stove-Top Mac & Cheese

Tags

kid-friendlylunchbbq sidepasta

Served with

  • peas
  • grilled ring bologna

Ingredients

  • Stove Top Mac & Cheese
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 cups milk
  • 16 oz macaroni
  • 2 cup shredded american cheese 8 oz
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • pinch cayenne (paprika)
  • 1 cup cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cup pipit …Maybe less of both???

Instructions

bring water and milk to a boil over high heat. stir in macaroni and reduce heat to medium low.

cook until soft, 6-8 minutes.

add american cheese and mustard and paprika

stir until cheese is melted, about 1 min

remove from heat

stir in cheddar until evenly distributed, but not melted

cover and let stand for 5 minutes
