Spaghetti & Meatballs II

Tags

healthy ladyNYTpasta

Served with

  • garlic bread
  • side salad

Ingredients

  • Marcella Hazan’s Tomato Sauce
  • 2 cups tomatoes, in addition to their juices (for example, a 28-ounce can of San Marzano whole peeled tomatoes)
  • 5 Tbs butter
  • 1 onion, peeled and cut in half
  • salt

Instructions

Combine the tomatoes, their juices, the butter and the onion halves in a saucepan. Add a pinch or two of salt.

Place over medium heat and bring to a simmer. Cook, uncovered, for about 45 minutes. Stir occasionally, mashing any large pieces of tomato with a spoon. Add salt as needed.

Discard the onion before tossing the sauce with pasta. This recipe makes enough sauce for a pound of pasta.

Meatballs

1/3 cup quick-cooking oats

1 dozen saltine crackers, crushed

3 Tbs milk

1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

1 large egg, lightly beaten

2 Tbs very finely minced onion

2 Tbs minced fresh parsley

1-2 garlic cloves, minced

salt/pepper

1/2 tsp oregano

1/2 tsp paprika

1/2 tsp aleppo pepper

1/2 tsp sumac

1 lb ground beef or turkey

olive oil

1 lb spaghetti

In a large bowl soak the oats in the milk for a few minutes. Add all remaining ingredients except the meat and mix well. Mix the beef or turkey in until just blended, not overworking.

Form the mixture into small balls.

Heat olive oil in a large, non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the meatballs and cook, turning often to brown them all over.

Transfer the meatballs to the sauce and cover. Continue to cook over low heat until meatballs are cooked through, about 15 mintues.
