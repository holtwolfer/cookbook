Special Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 2 Tbs mayo
  • 1 Tbs ketchup
  • 1/2 tsp soy sauce
  • dash of Franks
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • garlic powder
  • onion powder
  • spike (non-salt)
  • aleppo pepper
  • 1 Tbs chili sauce
  • mustard
  • worcestershire sauce
  • lemon juice or zest
  • bbq sauce
  • celery salt
  • pickle juice
  • relish

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients and whisk until smooth, combined.
