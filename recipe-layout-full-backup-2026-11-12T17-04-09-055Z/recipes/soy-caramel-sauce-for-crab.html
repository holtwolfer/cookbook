Soy Caramel Sauce for Crab

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbs soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup sake
  • 3 Tbs mirin
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 Tbs lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup cold butter

Instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, add the soy sauce, sake, mirin, sugar, and lemon juice.  Bring to a boil over high heat, then lower the heat and reduce to a simmer.  Cook the sauce until it is reduced by half, 5 to 7 minutes.  Turn the heat down to low and whisk in the butter 1 Tbs at a time, adding each only after the previous one has melted.
