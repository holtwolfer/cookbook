Adobo Chicken

Ingredients

FOR THE ADOBO:

  • 2-3 limes, juiced (about 4 Tbs)
  • 1 Tbs olive oil
  • 1-2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 Tbs homemade sazón

FOR THE CHICKEN:

  • 3 chicken breasts, butterflied
  • 2-3 limes
  • 1-2 small oranges
  • Lime wedges, hot sauce and chopped cilantro leaves, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Mix all adobo ingredients together in a small bowl.
  2. Marinate the chicken in lime and orange juice for about 30 minutes.
  3. Grill over medium heat with peach chips. Flip and add adobo to the top.

Served with

  • grilled corn
