Crab Cakes

Ingredients

Gerties Crab Cakes

  • 1 egg + 1 yolk
  • 1 Tbs dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbs mayo
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 2 tsp old bay
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce

dash of hot sauce

  • 1 lb backfin
  • 5 oz can lump
  • 1/2 sleeve ritz crackers, crushed into crumbs

ghee

  • olive oil
  • 2 large eggs, well beaten
  • ½ cup chopped celery
  • 1 cup crushed Saltine crackers or ritz plus panko?
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon-style mustard
  • 1 teaspoon Old Bay Seasoning
  • ¼ teaspoon red hot pepper flakes
  • 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped parsley sprigs
  • ½ cup finely chopped scallions
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly ground pepper
  • 1 pound crab meat, lump preferred, shell and cartilage removed
  • ½ cup finely ground fresh bread crumbs
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 3 Tbs Dijon- or Creole-style mustard
  • ¼ cup white-wine vinegar
  • 1 Tbs paprika
  • 1 cup grapeseed oil
  • (2 Tbs grated horseradish, fresh or prepared, did not use)
  • 1 clove garlic, rough chop
  • 3-4 scallions, rough chop
  • 2 stalks celery, rough chop
  • 1/2 bunch of parsley, rough chop
  • 2 Tbs ketchup
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon hot sauce
  • 1 pound jumbo lump crab meat, picked over
  • 20 saltine crackers, finely crushed
  • 1/4 cup canola oil
  • 4-5 medium potatoes, cut into largish fries

Instructions

  1. Mix egg, mayo, mustard, pepper, Old Bay, worceshershire, hot sauce together until frothy.
  2. Place crab meat in a large bowl and sprinkle on the cracker crumbs.
  3. Pour egg mixture over top.
  4. Gently toss to mix together.
  5. Form cakes.
  6. Heat small amount of ghee and olive oil in a skillet and sautee the cakes turning several times until golden brown, about 8 minutes total cooking time.
  7. If they brown too quickly lower the heat and cover with foil to make sure they cook through.
  8. Crab Cakes Baltimore-Style
  9. This is a classic crab cake inspired by those that were served at Obrycki's Crab House, a rollicking fish restaurant in a former row house on East Pratt Street in Baltimore. They are simply delicious.
  10. lemon juice

dash of Frank's Red Hot

  1. ¼ cup vegetable oil
  2. lemon wedges
  3. In a large mixing bowl combine the eggs, celery, saltines, mayonnaise, mustard, Old Bay Seasoning, pepper flakes, Worcestershire sauce, parsley, scallions, salt and pepper, and blend well. Add the crab meat, folding it in lightly without breaking it up.
  4. Divide the mixture into 12 equal portions. Shape them into hamburger-like patties. Dredge them lightly in the bread crumbs.
  5. Heat approximately 2 tablespoons of the oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Saute the crab cakes 2 to 3 minutes on each side or until golden brown, using the remaining 2 tablespoons oil if necessary. Drain on paper towels immediately. Serve the crab cakes with this French Creole-inspired remoulade sauce.

Rémoulade Sauce

  1. salt/pepper
  2. In small food processer, chop the garlic, scallions, celery, parsley. Add the egg yolk, mustard, vinegar, paprika, ketchup, worcestershire, salt/pepper and process until smooth. Slowly add the oil in a fine stream while mixing. Chill in refrigerator. It will thicken.
  3. Lemon wedges, for serving
  4. No Need for Filler
  5. This is my favorite crab cake recipe. If you don’t overmix, and don’t pack your mounds too tightly, you will experience pure, unadulterated crab cake heaven. I like ones that don’t have a lot of filler, no minced red pepper, no parsley—none of the usual crap that chefs typically ruin a good crab cake with. I make these in double batches and put the leftovers in the fridge. If you haven’t eaten a cold crab cake on toast with sliced tomato and Russian dressing, then you’re missing out. You can also make these as a master batch: Instead of just eight, I make 24 to 26 mini donut-hole-size cakes for parties.
  6. Instructions
  7. Total Time: 1 hr 25 min
  8. Servings: 4
  9. In a small bowl, whisk the mayonnaise with the egg, mustard, Worcestershire sauce and hot sauce until smooth.
  10. In a medium bowl, lightly toss the crabmeat with the cracker crumbs. Gently fold in the mayonnaise mixture. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  11. Scoop the crab mixture into eight 1/3-cup mounds; lightly pack into 8 patties, about 1 1/2 inches thick. In a large skillet, heat the oil until shimmering. Add the crab cakes and cook over moderately high heat until deeply golden and heated through, about 3 minutes per side. Transfer the crab cakes to plates and serve with lemon wedges.

Air Fryer Fries

  1. 1.5 Tbs extra-virgin olive oil
  2. 1.5 tsp kosher salt, more as needed
  3. Put potatoes in a large bowl and cover with cold water. Soak for at least 30 minutes (or up to overnight, stored in the refrigerator), then drain and pat very dry.
  4. Heat the air fryer to 360 degrees
  5. In a dry bowl, toss potatoes with 1 tablespoon oil and 1.5 tsp salt. Transfer to air fryer and fry at 360 degrees for 10 minutes, tossing 2-3 times.
  6. Transfer fries to a sheet of parchment, spreading them in an even layer, and let cool to room temperature, at least 30 minutes and up to 4 hours.
  7. (This step is optional; it gives the fries a slightly crispier exterior. If you want to skip it, keep the potatoes in the fryer and proceed to the next step.)
  8. Turn the air fryer heat up to 390 degrees. Add fries and cook until golden and crisp, about 10 to 15 minutes, tossing occasionally.

Notes

Notes: really good with the remoulade sauce.
