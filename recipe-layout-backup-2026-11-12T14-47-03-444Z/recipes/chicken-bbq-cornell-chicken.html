Chicken BBQ (Cornell Chicken)

Tags

grillsummerchickenNY

Served with

  • For authentic upstate NY chicken bbq dinner serve with macaroni salad, baked beans and a dinner roll. Also good with: corn
  • salt potatoes
  • grilled beans
  • potato salad

Ingredients

  • Chicken BBQ (Cornell Chicken Barbecue)
  • Mop Sauce:
  • 1 cup oil
  • 2 cups cider vinegar
  • 1.5 Tbs salt (original 3)
  • 1 Tbs poultry seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 egg
  • 2 Chickens, halved
  • Make mop sauce by whisking all ingredients together.

Instructions

  1. Set up charcoal grill with coals on one half, piling more on the outside to create tapered heat from one side to the other.
  2. Start over cold side of grill with applewood on the hot to get it started cooking and get some good smoke flavor
  3. Start chicken skin side up on the hot side of the grill. Flip the chicken and apply mop sauce often, rotating the chicken from the medium heat to high heat as needed and move to the ‘cool’ side if cooking too fast.
