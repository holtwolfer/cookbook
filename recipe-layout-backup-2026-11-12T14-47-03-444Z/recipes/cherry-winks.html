Cherry Winks

Ingredients

  • 1
  • cup sugar
  • 3/4
  • cup shortening
  • 2
  • tablespoons milk
  • 1
  • teaspoon vanilla
  • 2
  • eggs SAVE $

  • cups all-purpose flour
  • 1
  • teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2
  • teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2
  • teaspoon salt
  • 1
  • cup chopped pecans
  • 1
  • cup chopped dates
  • 1/3
  • cup chopped maraschino cherries, patted dry with paper towels
  • 1 1/2
  • cups coarsely crushed corn flakes cereal
  • 15
  • maraschino cherries, quartered

Instructions

  1. 1 In large bowl, beat sugar and shortening with electric mixer on medium speed, scraping bowl occasionally, until well blended. Beat in milk, vanilla and eggs. On low speed, beat in flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt, scraping bowl occasionally, until dough forms. Stir in pecans, dates and 1/3 cup chopped cherries. If necessary, cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate 15 minutes for easier handling.
  2. 2 Heat oven to 375°F. Spray cookie sheets with cooking spray. Drop dough by rounded teaspoonfuls into cereal; coat thoroughly. Shape into balls. Place 2 inches apart on cookie sheets. Lightly press maraschino cherry quarter into top of each ball.
  3. 3 Bake 10 to 15 minutes or until light golden brown. Cool 1 minute; remove from cookie sheets to cooling racks.

Notes and variations

From <https://www.pillsbury.com/recipes/cherry-winks/475409f0-2c9f-45e5-b4a1-8562ef1069c0>

1 1/4 cup flour

3/4 cup shortening

1 tsp baking powder

1/2 tsp salt

1 tsp baking soda

1 cup sugar

1 tsp vanilla

2 eggs

2 Tbs milk

1 cup pecans, chopped

1 cup dates, chopped

1/3 cup marashino cherries, chopped

3 cups corn flakes

Sift together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt.

Combine shortening and sugar. Add eggs, milk, vanilla. Blend in dry ingredients. Add nuts, dates, and cherries. Shape into balls using 1 level Tbs of dough for each cookie. Crush cornflakes. Place each cookie which has been rolled in the cornflakes on a greased cookie sheet. Top with 1/4 cherry.

Bake 10-12 minutes at 375 degrees.
