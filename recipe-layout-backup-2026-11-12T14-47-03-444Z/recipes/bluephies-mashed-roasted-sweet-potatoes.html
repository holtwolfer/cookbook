Bluephies Mashed Roasted Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 4-5 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1/2 tsp dried ginger
  • 2 Tbs Mae Ploy
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 2 tsp salt
  • Roasted:

Instructions

  1. Roast (or microwave) whole sweet potatoes.
  2. Grilled:
  3. Cut potatoes into 1 inch chunks and toss with olive oil, salt/pepper, paprika in a foil packet. Grill over high heat, opening the foil at some point to get some smoke.
  4. Make the sauce by mixing all ingredients together.
  5. Mash roasted veggies with the sauce mixture.
