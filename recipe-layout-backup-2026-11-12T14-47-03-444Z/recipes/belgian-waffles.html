Belgian Waffles

Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 Tbs baking powder
  • 3 Tbs sugar
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • 2 cups milk
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 10 Tbs butter (5 oz), melted and slightly cooled
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Whisk dry ingredients together in a medium bowl.
  2. Whisk milk, egg yolks, melted butter andvanilla together in another medium bowl.
  3. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients. Pour the milk mixture into the well and whisk until the ingredients are just combined.
  4. Beat egg whites with an electric hand mixer until stiff, but not dry, peaks form.
  5. Gently fold egg whites into batter. For best results, let the batter rest while preheating
  6. the waffle maker.
  7. Select BELGIAN setting and the desired darkness setting.
  8. Preheat the waffle maker until the LCD turns orange and 'HEATING' disappears.
  9. Pour approximately ½ cup of batter evenly on the center of each waffle square. Close
  10. the lid but keep the locking latch open.
  11. At the end of the cooking cycle, open the lid carefully and remove your waffles.
  12. Repeat with remaining batter.

Notes and variations

Makes 8 waffles
