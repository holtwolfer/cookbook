Daikon & Carrot Slaw

Ingredients

  • Daikon and Carrot Slaw
  • 2-3 daikon radishes, matchsticked
  • 2-3 carrots, matchsticked
  • lime juice
  • rice vinegar
  • soy sauce
  • chili paste
  • ginger
  • lemongrass
  • sesame seeds
  • oil
  • dill?

Instructions

  1. Mix lime juice, rice vinegar, soy sauce, chili paste, ginger, lemongrass, oil and pour over the veggies. Top with sesame seeds.
