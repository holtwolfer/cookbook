Chili-Glazed Salmon with Ginger Pea Pods

Ingredients

  • 1 large salmon filet
  • 1/4 cup sweet chili sauce (mae ploy)
  • 3 Tbs soy sauce
  • 2 Tbs grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • olive oil
  • 8 ounces sugar snap peas
  • 1 1/2 Tbs rice wine
  • 1 bunch watercress
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2-3 daikon radishes, matchsticked
  • 2-3 carrots, matchsticked
  • lime juice
  • rice vinegar

Instructions

  1. Combine chili sauce, 2 Tbs soy sauce, 1 Tbs ginger and whisk together. Coat salmon with some of the sauce and marinade 30 minutes.
  2. In a wok or deep frying pan saute garlic and ginger in olive oil for 1-2 minutes. Add sugar snap peas and cook until slightly tender, but still crispy, 2-5 minutes. Mix in watercress, rice wine and 1 Tbs soy sauce and cook 1-2 minutes, drizzle and mix in sesame oil.
  3. Grill:
  4. Soak a cedar plank for 1-2 hours. Grill over medium heat until internal temperature is around 122-125.
