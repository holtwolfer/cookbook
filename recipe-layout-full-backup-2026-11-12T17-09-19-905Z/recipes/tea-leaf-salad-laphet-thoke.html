Tea Leaf Salad (Laphet Thoke)

Ingredients

  • Tea Leaf Salad
  • ¼ to ½cup fermented tea leaves
  • 1 Tbs dry shrimp powder
  • 1 Tbs peanut oil
  • 2 large julienne plum tomatoes
  • 1 cup very thinly sliced cabbage
  • 1 tsp fried garlic (see note)
  • 2 Tbs roasted peanuts
  • 2 Tbs fried dried peas
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 2 to 3 tsp lime juice
  • ⅛ to ¼ tsp minced bird's-eye chili or other hot chili, like a serrano
  • 1 to 2 tsp fish sauce (nam pla or nuoc mam)
  • cilantro leaves, sliced fried garlic and whole green chilies, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in large bowl, and mix well. Divide between two plates, and serve, with garnishes.
  2. To make fried garlic, slice as many cloves of garlic lengthwise as desired. Sauté slowly in peanut oil to cover until garlic is lightly browned on both sides. Remove from oil, and place on paper towels to dry. Refrigerate in tightly covered container.
